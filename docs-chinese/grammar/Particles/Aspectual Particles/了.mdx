# 了 (le)

Completion of an action (when used right after a verb: Verb + 了)

Instead of marking time (*past/present/future*), **了** does two distinct jobs:
1. **Verb + 了 :** Marks the **completion / realization** of an action.
2. **Sentence-Final 了:** Marks a **change of state / new situation** (*"now" / "anymore"*).

| Type | Position | Function | Mental Shortcut |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **了** *(Aspect)* | Directly after **Verb** | Action is **completed / finished** | *"Done!"* |
| **了** *(Modal)* | At the **end of sentence** | A **new situation / state change** occurred | *"Things have changed / Now"* |
| **Double 了** | Both **after verb** AND **at the end** | Action completed up to now AND **still continuing** | *"Have been doing for... and still am"* |

---

## Verb + 了: Completed Action (Aspect Marker)

When placed directly after a verb, 了 functions as an **aspect marker** indicating that an action is **completed or realized**. 

:::info
了 le is Not Past Tense. It indicates completion of an action, not grammatical past tense. It can be used for actions completed in the past, actions happening routinely, or actions that will be completed in the future before another action begins.
:::

When using verbal 了 with a direct object, the object normally takes a specific modifier (such as a quantity, measure word, or descriptive adjective) so the sentence does not sound abruptly unfinished.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

### Consecutive Actions ("As Soon As... Then...")

When two events occur sequentially, attach 了 directly to the **first verb** to show that Action 1 must conclude before Action 2 starts. 

:::note
Subject + Verb 1 + 了 + (Obj 1) + 就 + Verb 2 + (Obj 2)
:::

| Time Frame | Structure | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Future / Planned** | Verb 1 + **[了 le](le0.wav)** ... **[就 jiù](jiu4.wav)** + Verb 2 | [我到了家就给你打电话。 Wǒ dàole jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.](wo3dao4le0jia1jiu4gei3ni3da3dian4hua4.wav) <br /><small>⤷ As soon as I arrive home, I will call you.</small> |
| **Sequential Flow** | Verb 1 + **[了 le](le0.wav)** ... + Verb 2 | [我们吃了晚饭去看电影。 Wǒmen chīle wǎnfàn qù kàn diànyǐng.](wo3men0chi1le0wan3fan4qu4kan4dian4ying3.wav) <br /><small>⤷ After eating dinner, we will go watch a movie.</small> |
| **Past Completed** | Verb 1 + **[了 le](le0.wav)** ... **[就 jiù](jiu4.wav)** + Verb 2 | [他下了班就回家了。 Tā xiàle bān jiù huí jiā le.](ta1xia4le0ban1jiu4hui2jia1le0.wav) <br /><small>⤷ As soon as he got off work, he went home.</small> |

## Sentence-Final 了: Change of State

When **了** appears at the very end of a sentence, it signals that **the situation has changed**. It was not like this before, but it is now.

:::note
**[Statement / Condition]** + **了 (*le*)**  
*(Implies: "...now" or "...anymore")*
:::

| State Before | Sentence with 了₂ | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| It was dry | [下雨了！ Xiàyǔ le!](xia4yu3le0.wav) | It has started raining! *(It wasn't raining before).* |
| I didn't know | [我知道了。 Wǒ zhīdào le.](wo3zhi1dao4le0.wav) | I know now / I understand now. |
| He was single | [他有女朋友了。 Tā yǒu nǚpéngyou le.](ta1you3nv3peng2you0le0.wav) | He has a girlfriend now. |
| I used to eat meat | [我不吃肉了。 Wǒ bù chī ròu le.](wo3bu4chi1rou4le0.wav) | I don't eat meat anymore. |
| You were small | [你长大了！ Nǐ zhǎngdà le!](ni3zhang3da4le0.wav) | You've grown up! |

> 💡 **Notice the Negation Difference:**  
> * [我不去。 Wǒ bú qù.](wo3bu2qu4.wav) = I am not going *(simple refusal).*  
> * [我不去了。 Wǒ bú qù le.](wo3bu2qu4le0.wav) = I'm not going **anymore** *(I originally planned to go, but changed my mind).*

## Double 了: "Present Perfect Continuous"
What happens when you put **了** after the verb **AND** at the end of the sentence?  
It means the action started in the past, completed up to this moment, **and is still ongoing right now**.

:::note
**Subject** + **Verb** + **了** + **[Duration]** + **Object** + **了**
:::

### Compare Single 了 vs. Double 了

| Structure | Chinese Sentence | What it Really Means |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Single 了**<br />*(Finished)* | [我学了一年中文。 Wǒ xuéle yì nián Zhōngwén.](wo3xue2le0yi4nian2zhong1wen2.wav) | I studied Chinese for one year. *(I'm not studying anymore).* |
| **Double 了**<br />*(Still ongoing)* | [我学了一年中文了。 Wǒ xuéle yì nián Zhōngwén le.](wo3xue2le0yi4nian2zhong1wen2le0.wav) | I have been studying Chinese for a year **(and I am still studying it)**. |
| **Single 了**<br />*(Finished)* | [他睡了八个小时。 Tā shuìle bā gè xiǎoshí.](ta1shui4le0ba1ge4xiao3shi2.wav) | He slept for 8 hours. *(He is awake now).* |
| **Double 了**<br />*(Still ongoing)* | [他睡了八个小时了。 Tā shuìle bā gè xiǎoshí le.](ta1shui4le0ba1ge4xiao3shi2le0.wav) | He has been asleep for 8 hours **(and is still sleeping)**. |

When you state a **specific time point** (e.g., *tomorrow*, *next week*, *at 3:00*), you must use 就要...了 instead of 快 or 快要:

❌ [明天天气快要下雨了。 Míngtiān tiānqì kuàiyào xiàyǔ le.](error.wav) *(Cannot use specific time with 快要)*

✔️ [明天我们就要出发了。 Míngtiān wǒmen jiùyào chūfā le.](ming2tian1wo3men0jiu4yao4chu1fa1le0.wav) *We are setting off tomorrow.*

## 了 (liǎo) as a Verb and Potential Complement

When pronounced liǎo instead of neutral le, this character retains its original lexical meaning: *"to finish"*, *"to resolve"*, or *"to be able to"*.

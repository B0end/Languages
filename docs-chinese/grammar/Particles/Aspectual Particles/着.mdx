# 着 (zhe / zháo / zhuó / zhāo)

The character **着** is one of the most famous polyphonic characters (多音字, *duōyīnzì*) in Mandarin Chinese. It has **four different pronunciations**, each tied to distinct grammatical categories and registers:

| Character | Pinyin | Category | Function / Core Meaning | Quick Anchor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **着** | **`zhe`** (Neutral) | Aspect Particle | Continuous state or accompanying action | [门开着; mén kāi zhe; the door is open](men2kai1zhe0.wav) |
| **着** | **`zháo`** (2nd Tone) | Resultative Verb | Attaining a goal, falling asleep, or catching fire | [睡着了; shuìzháo le; fell asleep](shui4zhao2le0.wav) |
| **着** | **`zhuó`** (2nd Tone) | Formal Verb Root | Clothing, landing, or applying focus | [着陆; zhuólù; to land](zhuo2lu4.wav) |
| **着** | **`zhāo`** (1st Tone) | Noun | A move (chess), trick, or strategy | [高招; gāozhāo; brilliant move](gao1zhao1.wav) |

---

## 1. Aspect Particle: `zhe` (Neutral Tone)

When pronounced in the neutral tone, **着** marks an **ongoing state** or describes **how an action is accompanied**.

### A. Continuous Static State
Shows that a state resulting from a prior action is maintained.

> **Pattern:** `Location / Subject + Verb + 着 + (Noun)`

| Context | Example Phrase / Sentence | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Doors & Windows** | [门开着。; Mén kāi zhe.; The door is open.](men2kai1zhe0.wav) | The door remains in an open state |
| **Wall Placement** | [墙上挂着一张画。; Qiáng shang guà zhe yì zhāng huà.; A painting is hanging on the wall.](qiang2shang0gua4zhe0yi4zhang1hua4.wav) | The painting is suspended on the wall |
| **Wearing Clothes** | [他穿着一件红色的衣服。; Tā chuān zhe yí jiàn hóngsè de yīfu.; He is wearing a red shirt.](ta1chuan1zhe0yi2jian4hong2se4de0yi1fu0.wav) | The clothes are currently on his body |
| **Table Placement** | [桌子上放着一本书。; Zhuōzi shang fàng zhe yì běn shū.; A book is lying on the table.](zhuo1zi0shang0fang4zhe0yi4ben3shu1.wav) | The book rests in that position |

### B. Accompanying Action (Manner of Action)
When two actions occur together, Verb 1 + 着 acts as the adverbial manner describing Verb 2.

> **Pattern:** `Subject + Verb 1 + 着 + (Object) + Verb 2`

| Accompanying Manner | Meaning | Full Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [笑着说; xiào zhe shuō; say with a smile](xiao4zhe0shuo1.wav) | Smiling while speaking | [他笑着对我说谢谢。; Tā xiào zhe duì wǒ shuō xièxie.; He smiled and said thank you to me.](ta1xiao4zhe0dui4wo3shuo1xie4xie0.wav) |
| [站着说话; zhàn zhe shuōhuà; speak while standing](zhan4zhe0shuo1hua4.wav) | Standing during conversation | [别站着说话，快坐下吧。; Bié zhàn zhe shuōhuà, kuài zuòxia ba.; Don't speak while standing; please sit down.](bie2zhan4zhe0shuo1hua4kuai4zuo4xia0ba0.wav) |
| [听着音乐做作业; tīng zhe yīnyuè zuò zuòyè; do homework while listening to music](ting1zhe0yin1yue4zuo4zuo4ye4.wav) | Dual activity | [她喜欢听着音乐做作业。; Tā xǐhuan tīng zhe yīnyuè zuò zuòyè.; She likes doing homework while listening to music.](ta1xi3huan0ting1zhe0yin1yue4zuo4zuo4ye4.wav) |

### C. Negation Rule
To negate a state marked by 着, use **没 (méi)** before the verb.

* [门没开着。; Mén méi kāi zhe.; The door is not open.](men2mei2kai1zhe0.wav)
* [他没穿着外套。; Tā méi chuān zhe wàitào.; He is not wearing a coat.](ta1mei2chuan1zhe0wai4tao4.wav)

## 2. Resultative & Action Verb: `zháo` (2nd Tone)

When pronounced **`zháo`**, it acts as a **result complement** meaning to successfully attain/reach a goal, or as a verb denoting catching fire, chills, or worry.

> **Pattern:** `Action Verb + 着 (zháo) + 了`

## 5. Critical Distinction: 着 (`zhe`) vs. 在 (`zài`)

Many learners confuse the static aspect marker **着** with the dynamic continuous marker **在**:

| Feature | **在 (`zài`)** — Dynamic Progression | **着 (`zhe`)** — Static Continuation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nature** | An active, ongoing dynamic action in motion | A continuous state or resting condition |
| **Action Example** | [他穿衣服呢。; Tā zài chuān yīfu ne.; He is putting on clothes.](ta1zai4chuan1yi1fu0ne0.wav)<br />*(He is physically buttoning his shirt)* | [他穿着衣服。; Tā chuān zhe yīfu.; He is wearing clothes.](ta1chuan1zhe0yi1fu0.wav)<br />*(The clothes are already on him)* |
| **Door Example** | [他在开门。; Tā zài kāi mén.; He is opening the door.](ta1zai4kai1men2.wav)<br />*(His hand is turning the doorknob)* | [门开着。; Mén kāi zhe.; The door is open.](men2kai1zhe0.wav)<br />*(The door stands open)* |

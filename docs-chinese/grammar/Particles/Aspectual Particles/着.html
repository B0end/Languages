着 (zhe / zháo / zhuó / zhāo)

The character 着 is one of the most famous polyphonic characters (多音字, duōyīnzì) in Mandarin Chinese. It has four different pronunciations, each tied to distinct grammatical categories and registers:

CharacterPinyinCategoryFunction / Core MeaningQuick Anchor
着zhe (Neutral)Aspect ParticleContinuous state or accompanying action门开着; mén kāi zhe; the door is open
着zháo (2nd Tone)Resultative VerbAttaining a goal, falling asleep, or catching fire睡着了; shuìzháo le; fell asleep
着zhuó (2nd Tone)Formal Verb RootClothing, landing, or applying focus着陆; zhuólù; to land
着zhāo (1st Tone)NounA move (chess), trick, or strategy高招; gāozhāo; brilliant move

1. Aspect Particle: zhe (Neutral Tone)​

When pronounced in the neutral tone, 着 marks an ongoing state or describes how an action is accompanied.

A. Continuous Static State​

Shows that a state resulting from a prior action is maintained.

Pattern: Location / Subject + Verb + 着 + (Noun)

ContextExample Phrase / SentenceEnglish Meaning
Doors & Windows门开着。; Mén kāi zhe.; The door is open.The door remains in an open state
Wall Placement墙上挂着一张画。; Qiáng shang guà zhe yì zhāng huà.; A painting is hanging on the wall.The painting is suspended on the wall
Wearing Clothes他穿着一件红色的衣服。; Tā chuān zhe yí jiàn hóngsè de yīfu.; He is wearing a red shirt.The clothes are currently on his body
Table Placement桌子上放着一本书。; Zhuōzi shang fàng zhe yì běn shū.; A book is lying on the table.The book rests in that position

B. Accompanying Action (Manner of Action)​

When two actions occur together, Verb 1 + 着 acts as the adverbial manner describing Verb 2.

Pattern: Subject + Verb 1 + 着 + (Object) + Verb 2

Accompanying MannerMeaningFull Example Sentence
笑着说; xiào zhe shuō; say with a smileSmiling while speaking他笑着对我说谢谢。; Tā xiào zhe duì wǒ shuō xièxie.; He smiled and said thank you to me.
站着说话; zhàn zhe shuōhuà; speak while standingStanding during conversation别站着说话，快坐下吧。; Bié zhàn zhe shuōhuà, kuài zuòxia ba.; Don't speak while standing; please sit down.
听着音乐做作业; tīng zhe yīnyuè zuò zuòyè; do homework while listening to musicDual activity她喜欢听着音乐做作业。; Tā xǐhuan tīng zhe yīnyuè zuò zuòyè.; She likes doing homework while listening to music.

C. Negation Rule​

To negate a state marked by 着, use 没 (méi) before the verb.

  • 门没开着。; Mén méi kāi zhe.; The door is not open.
  • 他没穿着外套。; Tā méi chuān zhe wàitào.; He is not wearing a coat.

2. Resultative & Action Verb: zháo (2nd Tone)​

When pronounced zháo, it acts as a result complement meaning to successfully attain/reach a goal, or as a verb denoting catching fire, chills, or worry.

Pattern: Action Verb + 着 (zháo) + 了

Vocabulary / PatternMeaningExample Sentence
睡着了; shuìzháo le; fell asleepResult of trying to sleep他昨天晚上很晚才睡着。; Tā zuótiān wǎnshang hěn wǎn cái shuìzháo.; He didn't fall asleep until very late last night.
找着了; zhǎozháo le; found it successfullyResult of searching我的钥匙终于找着了。; Wǒ de yàoshi zhōngyú zhǎozháo le.; I finally found my keys.
猜着了; cāizháo le; guessed correctlyResult of guessing你猜着了，这就是我的礼物。; Nǐ cāizháo le, zhè jiù shì wǒ de lǐwù.; You guessed it right; this is my gift.
着火; zháohuǒ; to catch firePhysical combustion大楼着火了，快跑！; Dàlóu zháohuǒ le, kuài pǎo!; The building caught fire, run quickly!
着凉; zháoliáng; to catch a chill / catch coldCatching sickness天气变冷了，小心着凉。; Tiānqì biàn lěng le, xiǎoxīn zháoliáng.; The weather turned cold; be careful not to catch a chill.
着急; zháojí; anxious / worriedAnxious emotional state别着急，事情总会解决的。; Bié zháojí, shìqing zǒng huì jiějué de.; Don't worry, things will work out eventually.

3. Formal & Written Verb Root: zhuó (2nd Tone)​

In formal speech, academic writing, and fixed compounds, zhuó means "to put on clothing," "to touch down / land," or "to apply focus."

WordEnglish MeaningExample Sentence
着陆; zhuólù; to land / touch downTouchdown (aircraft/spacecraft)飞机已安全着陆。; Fēijī yǐ ānquán zhuólù.; The plane has landed safely.
着手; zhuóshǒu; to set about / start onBegin practical action我们明天着手调查这个问题。; Wǒmen míngtiān zhuóshǒu diàochá zhè ge wèntí.; We will start investigating this problem tomorrow.
着重; zhuózhòng; to emphasize / stressPlace priority on老师着重讲解了这个重点。; Lǎoshī zhuózhòng jiǎngjiě le zhè ge zhòngdiǎn.; The teacher emphasized this key point.
穿着; chuānzhuó; attire / clothingFormal noun for clothing他的穿着非常得体。; Tā de chuānzhuó fēicháng détǐ.; His attire is very proper.

4. Noun (Move / Trick): zhāo (1st Tone)​

In games (chess, martial arts) and colloquial speech, zhāo refers to a strategic move, tactic, or trick.

TermMeaningExample Sentence
高招; gāozhāo; brilliant move / clever ideaClever strategy这真是一个高招！; Zhè zhēn shì yí gè gāozhāo!; This is truly a brilliant move!
绝招; juézhāo; secret move / unique skillUltimate technique这是他的独门绝招。; Zhè shì tā de dūmén juézhāo.; This is his unique specialty move.
没招儿; méi zhāor; out of options / at one's wit's endNo moves left我实在没招儿了。; Wǒ shízài méi zhāor le.; I am completely out of ideas.

5. Critical Distinction: 着 (zhe) vs. 在 (zài)​

Many learners confuse the static aspect marker 着 with the dynamic continuous marker 在:

Feature在 (zài) — Dynamic Progression着 (zhe) — Static Continuation
NatureAn active, ongoing dynamic action in motionA continuous state or resting condition
Action Example他穿衣服呢。; Tā zài chuān yīfu ne.; He is putting on clothes.
(He is physically buttoning his shirt)		他穿着衣服。; Tā chuān zhe yīfu.; He is wearing clothes.
(The clothes are already on him)
Door Example他在开门。; Tā zài kāi mén.; He is opening the door.
(His hand is turning the doorknob)		门开着。; Mén kāi zhe.; The door is open.
(The door stands open)

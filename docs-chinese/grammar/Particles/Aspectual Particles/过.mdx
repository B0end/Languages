# 过 (guò / guo)

**过** functions in two distinct ways in Mandarin:
1. **As an Aspect Particle (*guo*, neutral tone):** Attached to a verb to indicate **past life experience** (*"have done before"*).
2. **As a Full Verb (*guò*, 4th tone):** Means **to cross, pass by, or celebrate/spend time**.

---

## 1. Experiential Particle: Verb + 过

When placed directly after a verb, **过 (guo)** indicates that an action has occurred at least once in the past. It highlights personal experience rather than the immediate completion of a task.

### Core Sentence Patterns

| Sentence Form | Chinese Pattern | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Affirmative** | Subj + Verb + **过** + Object | [我去过中国。 Wǒ qùguo Zhōngguó.](wo3qu4guo0zhong1guo2.wav) <br /><small>⤷ I have been to China before.</small><br /><br />[他吃过北京烤鸭。 Tā chīguo Běijīng kǎoyā.](ta1chi1guo0bei3jing1kao3ya1.wav) <br /><small>⤷ He has eaten Peking duck before.</small><br /><br />[我看过这部电影。 Wǒ kànguo zhè bù diànyǐng.](wo3kan4guo0zhe4bu4dian4ying3.wav) <br /><small>⤷ I have seen this movie before.</small> |
| **Negative** | Subj + **没(有)** + Verb + **过** + Object | [我没吃过臭豆腐。 Wǒ méi chīguo chòudòufu.](wo3mei2chi1guo0chou4dou4fu0.wav) <br /><small>⤷ I have never eaten stinky tofu.</small><br /><br />[他没有去过日本。 Tā méiyǒu qùguo Rìběn.](ta1mei2you3qu4guo0ri4ben3.wav) <br /><small>⤷ He has never been to Japan.</small> |
| **Emphatic Never** | Subj + **从来没** + Verb + **过** + Object | [我从来没学过法语。 Wǒ cónglái méi xuéguo Fǎyǔ.](wo3cong2lai2mei2xue2guo0fa3yu3.wav) <br /><small>⤷ I have never studied French in my life.</small> |

## 2. 过 (guo) vs. 了 (le)

> * **过 (*guo*)** = An **experience** that is over and in the past. Focuses on whether it has ever happened.  
> * **了 (*le*)** = A **completed action** connected to a specific event or current state.

| Context | Sentence with 过 | Sentence with 了 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Food** | [你吃过吗？ Nǐ chīguo ma?](ni3chi1guo0ma0.wav)<br />*(Have you ever tasted this food in your life?)* | [你吃了吗？ Nǐ chīle ma?](ni3chi1le0ma0.wav)<br />*(Did you eat lunch/dinner today?)* |
| **Travel** | [他去过中国。 Tā qùguo Zhōngguó.](ta1qu4guo0zhong1guo2.wav)<br />*(He has visited China before — he is back home now).* | [他去了中国。 Tā qùle Zhōngguó.](ta1qu4le0zhong1guo2.wav)<br />*(He went to China — he is likely still there).* |
| **Illness** | [我感冒过。 Wǒ gǎnmàoguo.](wo3gan3mao4guo0.wav)<br />*(I have had colds before in my life).* | [我感冒了。 Wǒ gǎnmào le.](wo3gan3mao4le0.wav)<br />*(I caught a cold — I am sick right now).* |

## 3. Main Verb: To Cross, Pass, or Celebrate (guò)

When pronounced in the 4th tone (**guò**), **过** acts as a full lexical verb rather than an aspect particle. Its meanings fall into three primary categories:

| Category | Common Collocations | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crossing Physical Space** | **过马路** *(cross street)*<br />**过桥** *(cross bridge)*<br />**过河** *(cross river)* | [过马路的时候要小心。 Guò mǎlù de shíhou yào xiǎoxīn.](guo4ma3lu4de0shi2hou0yao4xiao3xin1.wav) <br /><small>⤷ Be careful when crossing the street.</small> |
| **Spending / Celebrating Time** | **过生日** *(celebrate birthday)*<br />**过年** *(celebrate New Year)*<br />**过周末** *(spend weekend)* | [祝你过得愉快！ Zhù nǐ guò de yúkuài!](zhu4ni3guo4de0yu2kuai4.wav) <br /><small>⤷ Have a pleasant time!</small> |
| **Passing Beyond a Threshold** | **过十二点** *(past 12 o'clock)*<br />**过四十岁** *(over 40 years old)*<br />**过期** *(expire / pass limit)* | [现在过了十二点。 Xiànzài guòle shí'èr diǎn.](xian4zai4guo4le0shi2er4dian3.wav) <br /><small>⤷ It is now past 12 o'clock.</small><br /><br />[这盒牛奶过期了。 Zhè hé niúnǎi guòqī le.](zhe4he2niu2nai3guo4qi1le0.wav) <br /><small>⤷ This box of milk has expired.</small> |

## 4. Directional Complements: 过来 (guòlái) vs. 过去 (guòqù)

Attach **过来** or **过去** after a movement verb to indicate the direction of an action crossing space relative to the speaker. They also express metaphorical shifts in consciousness or state:

| Complement | Physical Direction | Metaphorical State | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verb + 过来** | **Toward the speaker**<br /><small>⤷ "Over here"</small> | **Returning to normal / clarity**<br /><small>⤷ Regaining consciousness, fixing an error</small> | [你过来一下！ Nǐ guòlái yíxià!](ni3guo4lai2yi2xia4.wav) <br /><small>⤷ Come over here for a second!</small><br /><br />[病人醒过来了。 Bìngrén xǐng guòlái le.](bing4ren2xing3guo4lai2le0.wav) <br /><small>⤷ The patient regained consciousness.</small> |
| **Verb + 过去** | **Away from the speaker**<br /><small>⤷ "Over there"</small> | **Losing normal state / fading**<br /><small>⤷ Passing out, fainting, drifting off</small> | [请走过去。 Qǐng zǒu guòqù.](qing3zou3guo4qu4.wav) <br /><small>⤷ Please walk over there.</small><br /><br />[他晕过去了。 Tā yūn guòqù le.](ta1yun1guo4qu4le0.wav) <br /><small>⤷ He fainted / passed out.</small> |

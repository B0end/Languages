吗 (ma)

The character 吗 is predominantly pronounced in the neutral tone: ma.

It is the foundational yes/no question particle in Mandarin Chinese. Unlike English, which requires changing word order or adding auxiliary verbs ("Do you...", "Are you..."), Chinese simply appends 吗 to the end of a declarative sentence.

Usage TypeGrammatical FunctionEnglish EquivalentQuick Anchor
1. Yes/No QuestionsConverts a statement into a binary question"...?" / "Is it true that...?"你好吗？; Nǐ hǎo ma?; How are you?
2. Rhetorical QuestionsEmphasizes an obvious fact or reminder"Isn't it...?" / "Don't you...?"这不是很好吗？; Zhè bú shì hěn hǎo ma?; Isn't this great?
3. Tag ConfirmationAsks for consent or agreement"Okay?" / "Can we?"可以吗？; Kěyǐ ma?; Is that okay?
4. Phonetic Loanword (mǎ)Transliteration character (3rd tone)Chemical/medical loanwords吗啡; mǎfēi; morphine

1. Standard Yes/No Questions​

To turn any declarative statement into a yes/no question, keep the exact same word order and add 吗 at the very end.

Pattern: Declarative Statement + 吗？

Base Statement (Hanzi & Pinyin)Question with 吗English Meaning
你是学生。
(Nǐ shì xuésheng.)		你是学生吗？; Nǐ shì xuésheng ma?; Are you a student?Confirms identity
他去过中国。
(Tā qù guò Zhōngguó.)		他去过中国吗？; Tā qù guò Zhōngguó ma?; Has he been to China?Confirms past experience
你喜欢喝茶。
(Nǐ xǐhuan hē chá.)		你喜欢喝茶吗？; Nǐ xǐhuan hē chá ma?; Do you like drinking tea?Confirms preference
你准备好了。
(Nǐ zhǔnbèi hǎo le.)		你准备好了吗？; Nǐ zhǔnbèi hǎo le ma?; Are you ready?Confirms state completion

2. Rhetorical Questions & Reminders​

When combined with negative adverbs like 不是 (bú shì) or rhetorical markers like 难道 (nándào), 吗 forms questions where the speaker already expects agreement.

Pattern A: Subject + 不是 + Predicate + 吗？ ("Isn't it true that...?")
Pattern B: 难道 + Statement + 吗？ ("Could it really be that...?")

Rhetorical FormulaExample SentenceImplied Meaning
不是……吗？今天不是星期天吗？; Jīntiān bú shì Xīngqītiān ma?; Isn't today Sunday?"Today is definitely Sunday; why are you acting otherwise?"
不是……吗？这不是很明显吗？; Zhè bú shì hěn míngxiǎn ma?; Isn't this obvious?"This is completely obvious."
不是……吗？他不是你的朋友吗？; Tā bú shì nǐ de péngyou ma?; Isn't he your friend?"I thought he was your friend."
难道……吗？难道你不知道吗？; Nándào nǐ bù zhīdào ma?; Could it be that you don't know?"You should definitely know this."

3. Short Permission & Tag Requests​

吗 is frequently attached to modal verbs or short predicate phrases at the end of a sentence to seek permission or confirmation.

Pattern: Statement / Proposal + [ Modal Verb + 吗 ]？

Tag PhraseMeaningFull Example Sentence
可以吗？; kěyǐ ma?; may I? / is that okay?Seeking consent我们明天见，可以吗？; Wǒmen míngtiān jiàn, kěyǐ ma?; Let's meet tomorrow, is that okay?
行吗？; xíng ma?; will that work? / alright?Checking viability我用一下你的电脑，行吗？; Wǒ yòng yíxià nǐ de diànnǎo, xíng ma?; May I use your computer for a moment?
好吗？; hǎo ma?; okay? / deal?Friendly agreement晚上一起吃饭，好吗？; Wǎnshang yìqǐ chīfàn, hǎo ma?; Let's eat dinner together tonight, okay?
对吗？; duì ma?; right? / correct?Verifying accuracy这是你的手机，对吗？; Zhè shì nǐ de shǒujī, duì ma?; This is your phone, right?

4. Special Pronunciations: mǎ & má​

While 吗 is overwhelmingly a neutral tone particle, it has two specific exceptions:

WordPinyinMeaningNote
吗啡; mǎfēi; morphinemǎ (3rd tone)Morphine (analgesic drug)Standard transliteration loanword
干吗; gànmá; why / what formá (2nd tone)Spoken colloquial for "Why are you doing that?"Often interchangeably written as 干嘛

5. Critical Pitfalls: When NOT to Use 吗​

A universal rule in Mandarin grammar is that two question methods cannot be combined in one sentence. If a question structure already exists, never add 吗.

Conflict TypeIncorrect StructureCorrect Natural VersionExplanation
Question Words
(什么 / 谁 / 哪儿)		他是谁吗？❌他是谁？; Tā shì shéi?; Who is he?Question pronouns already mark the question.
Question Words
(什么 / 怎么)		你在看什么吗？❌你在看什么？; Nǐ zài kàn shénme?; What are you looking at?Adding 吗 here is ungrammatical.
A-not-A Form
(V-不-V)		你想不想去吗？❌你想不想去？; Nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng qù?; Do you want to go or not?
— or —
你想去吗？; Nǐ xiǎng qù ma?; Do you want to go?		Choose either the A-not-A form or 吗, never both.
Alternative Choice
(还是)		你喝茶还是咖啡吗？❌你喝茶还是喝咖啡？; Nǐ hē chá háishi hē kāfēi?; Do you drink tea or coffee?还是 (or) already sets up an alternative question.

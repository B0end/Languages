呢 (ne / ní)

The character 呢 has two pronunciations: the overwhelmingly common neutral tone particle ne and the 2nd tone noun root ní (referring to woolen fabric):

CharacterPinyinCategoryFunction / Core MeaningQuick Anchor
呢ne (Neutral)Modal Particle"How about...?", locating missing items, ongoing actions你呢？; nǐ ne?; how about you?
呢ní (2nd Tone)Noun RootWoolen material or heavy cloth呢大衣; ní dàyī; woolen overcoat

1. Modal Particle: ne (Neutral Tone)​

When used as a particle, 呢 fulfills several core communicative roles depending on sentence structure and context.

A. Bounce-Back Questions ("How about...?")​

When a topic has already been introduced, 呢 creates an elliptical question by bouncing the query to another subject without repeating the whole sentence.

Pattern: Subject / Topic + 呢？

ContextExample Dialogue / SentenceMeaning
Self-introduction我很好，你呢？; Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?; I'm doing well, how about you?Reciprocates the greeting
Third-Party Inquiry他呢？他去不去？; Tā ne? Tā qù bu qù?; What about him? Is he going?Shifts attention to another person
Availability明天我有空，你呢？; Míngtiān wǒ yǒu kòng, nǐ ne?; I'm free tomorrow, how about you?Checks the other person's schedule
Preference我想喝茶，你呢？; Wǒ xiǎng hē chá, nǐ ne?; I'd like some tea, what about you?Inquires about choice

B. Locating Missing Items ("Where is...?")​

When used immediately after a noun without prior context, 呢 asks about the physical location of someone or something that is missing or expected.

Pattern: Missing Person / Object + 呢？ (Short for: "Where is [noun]?")

TargetPractical ExampleEnglish Meaning
Lost item (Keys)我的钥匙呢？; Wǒ de yàoshi ne?; Where are my keys?"Where did my keys go?"
Missing person小李呢？他怎么还没来？; Xiǎo Lǐ ne? Tā zěnme hái méi lái?; Where is Xiao Li? Why hasn't he arrived?Inquires about an absent person
Misplaced item (Book)书呢？怎么找不到了？; Shū ne? Zěnme zhǎo bu dào le?; Where is the book? Why can't I find it?Searching for a missing object
Wallet / Phone我的手机呢？; Wǒ de shǒujī ne?; Where is my phone?Immediate realization of misplacement

C. Marking Ongoing Actions & Sustained States​

Placed at the end of a declarative sentence, 呢 emphasizes that an action is actively progressing right now or that a particular state still continues.

Pattern: Subject + (在 / 正 / 正在) + Verb + 呢
Pattern: Subject + Verb + 着 + 呢

State / Action TypeExample SentenceImplied Tone
Dynamic Action他在看书呢。; Tā zài kànshū ne.; He is reading right now.Actively in the middle of reading
Weather / Nature外面正下雨呢。; Wàimiàn zhèng xiàyǔ ne.; It is currently raining outside.Weather state in progress
Sustained State门开着呢。; Mén kāi zhe ne.; The door is currently open.Emphasizes open condition
Ongoing Wait大家等你好久了呢。; Dàjiā děng nǐ hǎo jiǔ le ne.; Everyone has been waiting for you for a long time.Friendly reminder of ongoing duration

D. Softening Wh-Questions & Dilemmas​

Adding 呢 to content questions (questions with 什么, 怎么, 哪儿) or alternative choice questions makes the inquiry sound reflective, gentle, and conversational.

Pattern: Question Word / Alternative (还是) + Sentence + 呢？

Query TypeExample SentenceTone Nuance
Preference (What)你想吃什么呢？; Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme ne?; What would you like to eat?Gentle invitation for options
Location (Where)我们去哪儿呢？; Wǒmen qù nǎr ne?; Where shall we go?Friendly deliberation
Dilemma (Choice)去还是不去呢？; Qù háishi bú qù ne?; Should I go or should I not?Internal pondering / hesitation
Method (How)这该怎么办呢？; Zhè gāi zěnme bàn ne?; What should be done about this?Reflective questioning

E. Topical Pause within a Sentence​

Attached after a topic clause at the start of a sentence, 呢 acts as a conversational pause, drawing listener focus to the subject before elaborating.

  • 现在呢，大家都不着急了。; Xiànzài ne, dàjiā dōu bù zháojí le.; As for right now, nobody is anxious anymore.
  • 这个人呢，很有才华。; Zhè ge rén ne, hěn yǒu cáihuá.; As for this person, he is very talented.

2. Noun Root: ní (2nd Tone) — Woolen Cloth​

When pronounced in the 2nd tone, ní refers strictly to heavy woolen fabrics and winter garments.

VocabularyPinyin & MeaningContext Example
呢大衣; ní dàyī; woolen overcoatHeavy overcoat这件呢大衣非常保暖。; Zhè jiàn ní dàyī fēicháng bǎonuǎn.; This woolen overcoat is very warm.
呢子; nízi; woolen cloth / heavy wool fabricWoolen fabric material这套衣服是用呢子做的。; Zhè tào yīfu shì yòng nízi zuò de.; This suit is made of heavy wool.
呢绒; níróng; woolen textilesGeneral woolen textile term呢绒服装很受大家欢迎。; Níróng fúzhuāng hěn shòu dàjiā huānyíng.; Woolen clothing is very popular.

3. Master Particle Contrast: 吗 vs. 吧 vs. 呢​

ParticlePrimary FunctionInformation StateAnchor Example
吗 (ma)True Yes/No question0% knowledge; pure inquiry你去吗？; Nǐ qù ma?; Are you going?
吧 (ba)Suggestion / Confirmation80% guess; seeking validation or proposing你去吧。; Nǐ qù ba.; Why don't you go? / You should go.
呢 (ne)Bounce-back / Ongoing stateInquiring about a new subject or ongoing progress你去，他呢？; Nǐ qù, tā ne?; You're going, what about him?

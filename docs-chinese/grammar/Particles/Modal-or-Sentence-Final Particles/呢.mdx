# 呢 (ne / ní)

The character **呢** has two pronunciations: the overwhelmingly common neutral tone particle **`ne`** and the 2nd tone noun root **`ní`** (referring to woolen fabric):

| Character | Pinyin | Category | Function / Core Meaning | Quick Anchor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **呢** | **`ne`** (Neutral) | Modal Particle | "How about...?", locating missing items, ongoing actions | [你呢？; nǐ ne?; how about you?](ni3ne0.wav) |
| **呢** | **`ní`** (2nd Tone) | Noun Root | Woolen material or heavy cloth | [呢大衣; ní dàyī; woolen overcoat](ni2da4yi1.wav) |

---

## 1. Modal Particle: `ne` (Neutral Tone)

When used as a particle, **呢** fulfills several core communicative roles depending on sentence structure and context.

---

### A. Bounce-Back Questions ("How about...?")
When a topic has already been introduced, **呢** creates an elliptical question by bouncing the query to another subject without repeating the whole sentence.

> **Pattern:** `Subject / Topic + 呢？`

| Context | Example Dialogue / Sentence | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Self-introduction** | [我很好，你呢？; Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?; I'm doing well, how about you?](wo3hen3hao3ni3ne0.wav) | Reciprocates the greeting |
| **Third-Party Inquiry** | [他呢？他去不去？; Tā ne? Tā qù bu qù?; What about him? Is he going?](ta1ne0ta1qu4bu0qu4.wav) | Shifts attention to another person |
| **Availability** | [明天我有空，你呢？; Míngtiān wǒ yǒu kòng, nǐ ne?; I'm free tomorrow, how about you?](ming2tian1wo3you3kong4ni3ne0.wav) | Checks the other person's schedule |
| **Preference** | [我想喝茶，你呢？; Wǒ xiǎng hē chá, nǐ ne?; I'd like some tea, what about you?](wo3xiang3he1cha2ni3ne0.wav) | Inquires about choice |

---

### B. Locating Missing Items ("Where is...?")
When used immediately after a noun without prior context, **呢** asks about the physical location of someone or something that is missing or expected.

> **Pattern:** `Missing Person / Object + 呢？` *(Short for: "Where is [noun]?")*

| Target | Practical Example | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lost item (Keys)** | [我的钥匙呢？; Wǒ de yàoshi ne?; Where are my keys?](wo3de0yao4shi0ne0.wav) | "Where did my keys go?" |
| **Missing person** | [小李呢？他怎么还没来？; Xiǎo Lǐ ne? Tā zěnme hái méi lái?; Where is Xiao Li? Why hasn't he arrived?](xiao3li3ne0ta1zen3me0hai2mei2lai2.wav) | Inquires about an absent person |
| **Misplaced item (Book)** | [书呢？怎么找不到了？; Shū ne? Zěnme zhǎo bu dào le?; Where is the book? Why can't I find it?](shu1ne0zen3me0zhao3bu0dao4le0.wav) | Searching for a missing object |
| **Wallet / Phone** | [我的手机呢？; Wǒ de shǒujī ne?; Where is my phone?](wo3de0shou3ji1ne0.wav) | Immediate realization of misplacement |

---

### C. Marking Ongoing Actions & Sustained States
Placed at the end of a declarative sentence, **呢** emphasizes that an action is **actively progressing right now** or that a particular state **still continues**.

> **Pattern:** `Subject + (在 / 正 / 正在) + Verb + 呢`  
> **Pattern:** `Subject + Verb + 着 + 呢`

| State / Action Type | Example Sentence | Implied Tone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dynamic Action** | [他在看书呢。; Tā zài kànshū ne.; He is reading right now.](ta1zai4kan4shu1ne0.wav) | Actively in the middle of reading |
| **Weather / Nature** | [外面正下雨呢。; Wàimiàn zhèng xiàyǔ ne.; It is currently raining outside.](wai4mian4zheng4xia4yu3ne0.wav) | Weather state in progress |
| **Sustained State** | [门开着呢。; Mén kāi zhe ne.; The door is currently open.](men2kai1zhe0ne0.wav) | Emphasizes open condition |
| **Ongoing Wait** | [大家等你好久了呢。; Dàjiā děng nǐ hǎo jiǔ le ne.; Everyone has been waiting for you for a long time.](da4jia1deng3ni3hao3jiu3le0ne0.wav) | Friendly reminder of ongoing duration |

---

### D. Softening Wh-Questions & Dilemmas
Adding **呢** to content questions (questions with *什么, 怎么, 哪儿*) or alternative choice questions makes the inquiry sound reflective, gentle, and conversational.

> **Pattern:** `Question Word / Alternative (还是) + Sentence + 呢？`

| Query Type | Example Sentence | Tone Nuance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Preference (What)** | [你想吃什么呢？; Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme ne?; What would you like to eat?](ni3xiang3chi1shen2me0ne0.wav) | Gentle invitation for options |
| **Location (Where)** | [我们去哪儿呢？; Wǒmen qù nǎr ne?; Where shall we go?](wo3men0qu4nar3ne0.wav) | Friendly deliberation |
| **Dilemma (Choice)** | [去还是不去呢？; Qù háishi bú qù ne?; Should I go or should I not?](qu4hai2shi4bu2qu4ne0.wav) | Internal pondering / hesitation |
| **Method (How)** | [这该怎么办呢？; Zhè gāi zěnme bàn ne?; What should be done about this?](zhe4gai1zen3me0ban4ne0.wav) | Reflective questioning |

---

### E. Topical Pause within a Sentence
Attached after a topic clause at the start of a sentence, **呢** acts as a conversational pause, drawing listener focus to the subject before elaborating.

* [现在呢，大家都不着急了。; Xiànzài ne, dàjiā dōu bù zháojí le.; As for right now, nobody is anxious anymore.](xian4zai4ne0da4jia1dou1bu4zhao2ji2le0.wav)
* [这个人呢，很有才华。; Zhè ge rén ne, hěn yǒu cáihuá.; As for this person, he is very talented.](zhe4ge0ren2ne0hen3you3cai2hua2.wav)

## 2. Noun Root: `ní` (2nd Tone) — Woolen Cloth

When pronounced in the 2nd tone, **`ní`** refers strictly to heavy woolen fabrics and winter garments.

| Vocabulary | Pinyin & Meaning | Context Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| [呢子; nízi; woolen cloth / heavy wool fabric](ni2zi0.wav) | Woolen fabric material | [这套衣服是用呢子做的。; Zhè tào yīfu shì yòng nízi zuò de.; This suit is made of heavy wool.](zhe4tao4yi1fu0shi4yong4ni2zi0zuo4de0.wav) |

## 3. Master Particle Contrast: 吗 vs. 吧 vs. 呢

| Particle | Primary Function | Information State | Anchor Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **吗 (`ma`)** | True Yes/No question | 0% knowledge; pure inquiry | [你去吗？; Nǐ qù ma?; Are you going?](ni3qu4ma0.wav) |
| **吧 (`ba`)** | Suggestion / Confirmation | 80% guess; seeking validation or proposing | [你去吧。; Nǐ qù ba.; Why don't you go? / You should go.](ni3qu4ba0.wav) |
| **呢 (`ne`)** | Bounce-back / Ongoing state | Inquiring about a new subject or ongoing progress | [你去，他呢？; Nǐ qù, tā ne?; You're going, what about him?](ni3qu4ta1ne0.wav) |

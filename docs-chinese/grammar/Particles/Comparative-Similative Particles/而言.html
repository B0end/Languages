而言 (ér yán)

The construction 而言 (ér yán) is a formal prepositional particle phrase derived from Classical Chinese:

  • 而 (ér) — Conjunction ("and yet / in terms of")
  • 言 (yán) — Classical verb meaning "to speak" or "to state"

Literally translating to "speaking of..." or "in terms of...", 而言 is used in formal writing, speeches, news broadcasts, and professional discourse to define perspectives, scopes, and analytical baselines.

ConstructionGrammar RoleEnglish MeaningQuick Anchor
对……而言Perspective / Stance"As far as [someone] is concerned"对我而言; duì wǒ ér yán; as far as I am concerned
就……而言Scope / Criterion"In terms of [metric/aspect]"就目前而言; jiù mùqián ér yán; as far as the present is concerned
相对而言Set Adverbial Phrase"Relatively speaking"相对而言; xiāngduì ér yán; relatively speaking
简而言之Transitional Connector"To put it simply / in brief"简而言之; jiǎn ér yán zhī; in brief / in short

1. Perspective & Subjectivity: 对 / 对于……而言​

Marks the party or stakeholder from whose vantage point a judgment is being made.

Pattern: 对 / 对于 + Stakeholder + 而言，+ Statement / Evaluation

Target SubjectPhrase with 而言Full Context Example
Self (I / Me)对我而言; duì wǒ ér yán; for me / in my view对我而言，健康是最重要的。; Duì wǒ ér yán, jiànkāng shì zuì zhòngyào de.; As far as I'm concerned, health is the most important thing.
Learners对初学者而言; duì chūxuézhě ér yán; for beginners对初学者而言，发音非常关键。; Duì chūxuézhě ér yán, fāyīn fēicháng guānjiàn.; For beginners, pronunciation is critical.
Enterprise / Business对于公司而言; duìyú gōngsī ér yán; as far as the company is concerned对于公司而言，人才是核心竞争力。; Duìyú gōngsī ér yán, réncái shì héxīn jìngzhēnglì.; For the company, talent is its core competitive strength.
Society对社会而言; duì shèhuì ér yán; for society as a whole环境保护对社会而言意义深远。; Huánjìng bǎohù duì shèhuì ér yán yìyì shēnyuǎn.; Environmental protection has profound significance for society.

2. Scope, Criteria, & Angles: 就……而言​

Restricts an assertion to a specific angle, time boundary, or metric.

Pattern: 就 + Specific Angle / Scope / Metric + 而言，+ Statement

Boundary ScopePhrase with 而言Full Practical Example
Current Situation就目前而言; jiù mùqián ér yán; as far as the present is concerned就目前而言，这是最好的方案。; Jiù mùqián ér yán, zhè shì zuì hǎo de fāng'àn.; As far as the present is concerned, this is the best proposal.
Big Picture / Overall就整体而言; jiù zhěngtǐ ér yán; on the whole / looking at the big picture就整体而言，这次活动非常成功。; Jiù zhěngtǐ ér yán, zhè cì huó dòng fēicháng chénggōng.; On the whole, this event was very successful.
Individual Capacity就个人能力而言; jiù gèrén nénglì ér yán; in terms of personal ability就个人能力而言，他完全胜任这份工作。; Jiù gèrén nénglì ér yán, tā wánquán shèngrèn zhè fèn gōngzuò.; In terms of personal ability, he is fully qualified for this job.

3. High-Yield Formal Transition Connectors​

In formal reports, essays, and debate speeches, 而言 forms standardized introductory and summarizing connectors:

Transition WordMeaningExample Context
相对而言; xiāngduì ér yán; relatively speakingComparative qualification这道题相对而言比较简单。; Zhè dào tí xiāngduì ér yán bǐjiào jiǎndān.; Relatively speaking, this problem is fairly simple.
一般而言; yìbān ér yán; generally speakingBroad baseline statement一般而言，早睡早起对身体好。; Yìbān ér yán, zǎoshuì zǎoqǐ duì shēntǐ hǎo.; Generally speaking, sleeping and rising early is good for one's health.
具体而言; jùtǐ ér yán; specifically speakingNarrowing down to detail具体而言，我们需要增加三项预算。; Jùtǐ ér yán, wǒmen xūyào zēngjiā sān xiàng yùsuàn.; Specifically speaking, we need to add three budget items.
简而言之; jiǎn ér yán zhī; in brief / to put it simplyConcise recap简而言之，这项计划需要更多时间。; Jiǎn ér yán zhī, zhè xiàng jìhuà xūyào gèng duō shíjiān.; In short, this project requires more time.
换而言之; huàn ér yán zhī; in other wordsRestating in another way换而言之，我们必须重新开始。; Huàn ér yán zhī, wǒmen bìxū chóngxīn kāishǐ.; In other words, we must start over.

4. Study Contrast: 而言 (ér yán) vs. 来说 (lái shuō)​

Both constructions express "from the perspective of...", but they belong to different linguistic registers:

Feature……而言 (ér yán)……来说 (lái shuō)
RegisterFormal, literary, journalistic, academicConversational, natural, everyday spoken Chinese
Frequency in SpeechUsed in news reports, presentations, speechesUniversal daily conversation
Preposition MatchPaired with 对 / 对于 or 就Paired with 对 / 对于
Sentence Example对于我而言，这是一个挑战。; Duìyú wǒ ér yán, zhè shì yí gè tiǎozhàn.; For me, this is a challenge.
(Written/formal)		对我来说，这很难。; Duì wǒ lái shuō, zhè hěn nán.; For me, this is hard.
(Standard spoken)

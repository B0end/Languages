似的 (shìde)

The grammatical particle 似的 is an auxiliary word of comparison meaning "as if", "just like", or "seemingly."

Pronunciation Alert​

While the character 似 is pronounced sì in formal compounds (such as 似乎; sìhū; seemingly, 相似; xiāngsì; similar, and 类似; lèisì; analogous), in this specific particle construction it is pronounced shìde (often transcribed as shì de):

Target UnitPinyinPart of SpeechFunctionQuick Anchor
似的shìdeAuxiliary ParticleSimile marker: "as if / just like"好像生病了似的; hǎoxiàng shēngbìng le shìde; as if sick

1. Core Simile Pattern: 像 / 好像……似的​

The most common way to use 似的 is in tandem with comparison words like 像 (xiàng) or 好像 (hǎoxiàng). Together, they form a bracket around the comparison.

Pattern: Subject + (像 / 好像) + Simile Object / Scenario + 似的

Comparison ScenarioPhrase / Sentence with 似的English Meaning
State of Health他脸色苍白，好像生病了似的。; Tā liǎnsè cāngbái, hǎoxiàng shēngbìng le shìde.; His face was pale, as if he were sick.Appears ill
Plausibility他说得像真的一样，像真的似的。; Tā shuō de xiàng zhēn de shìde.; He spoke as if it were completely true.Convincing demeanor
Inattention / Reaction他低着头，好像没听见似的。; Tā dī zhe tóu, hǎoxiàng méi tīngjiàn shìde.; He kept his head down, as if he hadn't heard a thing.Pretending not to hear
Dreamlike Feeling这一切好像做梦似的。; Zhè yíqiè hǎoxiàng zuòmèng shìde.; All of this feels just like a dream.Surreal atmosphere

2. Colloquial Comparison: 跟……似的​

In spoken Mandarin, 跟 (gēn, "with/like") frequently replaces 像 to create conversational comparisons.

Pattern: Subject + 跟 + Reference Standard + 似的

Target ComparisonFull Example SentenceContext Nuance
Festive Mood今天家里很热闹，跟过年似的。; Jīntiān jiālǐ hěn rènao, gēn guònián shìde.; The house is so lively today, just like Chinese New Year.High festive energy
Maturity / Behavior他都三十岁了，还跟小孩子似的。; Tā dōu sānshí suì le, hái gēn xiǎoháizi shìde.; He is already thirty, yet still acts just like a child.Childish demeanor
Comfort / Hospitality别客气，就跟在自己家似的。; Bié kèqi, jiù gēn zài zìjǐ jiā shìde.; Don't be shy; make yourself feel just like at home.Welcoming warmth

3. Fixed Adverbial Expressions: ……似的 + Action​

似的 can attach directly to action phrases or descriptive words to create vivid adverbs describing how an action is performed.

Pattern: Descriptive Chunk + 似的 + Verb Phrase

Idiomatic PhraseMeaningPractical Example Sentence
飞也似的; fēiyěshìde; swift as an arrow / as if flyingExtremely fast pace他飞也似的跑了出去。; Tā fēiyěshìde pǎo le chūqù.; He dashed outside as if flying.
发疯似的; fāfēng shìde; frantically / like crazyRelentless intensity考试前他发疯似地复习。; Kǎoshì qián tā fāfēng shìde fùxí.; Before the exam, he reviewed like crazy.
拼了命似的; pīn le mìng shìde; desperately / risking life and limbMaximum effort选手们拼了命似地向终点跑去。; Xuǎnshǒumen pīn le mìng shìde xiàng zhōngdiǎn pǎo qù.; The athletes ran toward the finish line for dear life.

4. Study Comparison: 似的 (shìde) vs. 一样 (yíyàng)​

In similes, 似的 and 一样 are often interchangeable, but their grammatical boundaries differ:

Feature似的 (shìde)一样 (yíyàng)
Primary Meaning"As if / Just like""Same / Identical / As if"
Used in Similes (像……)Yes: 像真的一样 / 像真的似的; xiàng zhēn de shìde; like it's realYes: 像真的一样; xiàng zhēn de yíyàng; like it's real
Used Alone as Adjective❌ Cannot mean "identical"✅ Can mean identical (这两个手机一样。)
Tone & RegisterMore vivid, expressive, and conversationalStandard, neutral, and universal

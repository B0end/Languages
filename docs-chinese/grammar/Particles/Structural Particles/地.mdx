# 地 (de / dì)

The character **地** is a polyphone with two distinct pronunciations and roles in Mandarin Chinese:

| Character | Pinyin | Part of Speech | Function / Core Meaning | Quick Anchor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **地** | **`de`** (Neutral) | Adverbial Particle | Turns descriptions into adverbs (English **"-ly"**) | [慢慢地走; mànmàn de zǒu; walk slowly](man4man4de0zou3.wav) |
| **地** | **`dì`** (4th Tone) | Noun / Root Word | Physical earth, ground, land, or location | [地上; dìshang; on the ground](di4shang0.wav) |

---

## 1. Adverbial Particle: `de` (Neutral Tone)

When pronounced in the neutral tone, **地** connects a descriptive phrase or adjective to a **verb**. It describes the **attitude, mood, or manner** in which an action is performed.

:::note
Adjective + 地 + Verb
:::

### C. The Single-Syllable Exception

:::tip
When using a **single-syllable** adjective before an action, **地 is omitted**:
* Say: [快跑; kuài pǎo; run fast](kuai4pao3.wav) *(Never say 快地跑)*
* Say: [多吃; duō chī; eat more](duo1chi1.wav) *(Never say 多地吃)*
* Say: [慢走; màn zǒu; take care / walk slowly](man4zou3.wav) *(Never say 慢地走)*
:::

## 3. Study Comparison: 地 (de) vs. 得 (de)

Both particles describe actions, but their **position**, **grammatical order**, and **focus** are completely different:

| Feature | **地 (`de`)** — Manner Marker | **得 (`de`)** — Complement Marker |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Position** | **Before** the verb | **After** the verb |
| **Formula** | `Modifier + 地 + Verb` | `Verb + 得 + Complement` |
| **Focus** | Attitude or state **entering** the action | Outcome, score, or performance **resulting** from the action |
| **Manner Example** | [他慢慢地走。; Tā mànmàn de zǒu.; He slowly walks.](ta1man4man4de0zou3.wav)<br />*(Focus: His pace/attitude as he walks)* | [他走得很慢。; Tā zǒu de hěn màn.; He walks very slowly.](ta1zou3de0hen3man4.wav)<br />*(Focus: The evaluated result/speed of his walk)* |
| **Emotion Example** | [他高兴地跑回家。; Tā gāoxìng de pǎo huí jiā.; He happily ran home.](ta1gao1xing4de0pao3hui2jia1.wav)<br />*(Focus: His emotional state while running)* | [他跑得很快。; Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.; He ran very fast.](ta1pao3de0hen3kuai4.wav)<br />*(Focus: How fast the running turned out to be)* |

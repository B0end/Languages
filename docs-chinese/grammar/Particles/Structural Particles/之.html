之 (zhī)

Spatial & Temporal Boundary Markers​

In standard modern Chinese, 之 regularly combines with positional words to create fixed time and space boundaries.

Pattern: Time / Event / Location + 之 + Directional Word

Boundary MarkerMeaningContext Example
之前; zhīqián; before / prior toPrior to a point in time睡觉之前不要吃东西。; Shuìjiào zhīqián bú yào chī dōngxi.; Don't eat before going to sleep.
之后; zhīhòu; after / afterwardsSubsequent point in time下课之后我给你打电话。; Xiàkè zhīhòu wǒ gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.; I will call you after class.
之间; zhījiān; between / amongSpatial interval or relationship朋友之间要互相帮助。; Péngyou zhījiān yào hùxiāng bāngzhù.; Friends should help one another.
之外; zhīwài; outside / beyond / besidesExclusion or exception除了汉语之外，他还会说法语。; Chúle Hànyǔ zhīwài, tā hái huì shuō Fǎyǔ.; Besides Chinese, he can also speak French.
之中; zhīzhōng; in / among / in the middle ofMidst of a process or state生活在幸福之中; shēnghuó zài xìngfú zhīzhōng; living in happiness
之下; zhīxià; under / beneathUnder a condition or guidance在老师的帮助之下; zài lǎoshī de bāngzhù zhīxià; under the teacher's guidance

Formal & Written Equivalent of 的 (de)​

In formal literature, news reports, and idiomatic expressions, 之 connects a descriptive modifier to a noun, functioning just like 的 but in a much more elevated register.

Pattern: Modifier / Attribute + 之 + Head Noun

Formal ExpressionMeaningModern EquivalentExample Sentence
成功之路; chénggōng zhī lù; road to successPath to accomplishment成功的路坚持是成功之路。; Jiānchí shì chénggōng zhī lù.; Persistence is the road to success.
无价之宝; wújià zhī bǎo; priceless treasurePriceless treasure无价的宝贝时间是无价之宝。; Shíjiān shì wújià zhī bǎo.; Time is a priceless treasure.
解决之道; jiějué zhī dào; solution / way outMethod to resolve an issue解决的办法我们需要找到解决之道。; Wǒmen xūyào zhǎodào jiějué zhī dào.; We need to find a solution.
一念之间; yí niàn zhī jiān; in a single fleeting thoughtSplit-second decision一个念头之间成功与失败往往在一念之间。; Chénggōng yǔ shībài wǎngwǎng zài yí niàn zhī jiān.; Success and failure often hinge on a single momentary thought.

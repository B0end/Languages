地 (de / dì)

The character 地 is a polyphone with two distinct pronunciations and roles in Mandarin Chinese:

CharacterPinyinPart of SpeechFunction / Core MeaningQuick Anchor
地de (Neutral)Adverbial ParticleTurns descriptions into adverbs (English "-ly")慢慢地走; mànmàn de zǒu; walk slowly
地dì (4th Tone)Noun / Root WordPhysical earth, ground, land, or location地上; dìshang; on the ground

1. Adverbial Particle: de (Neutral Tone)​

When pronounced in the neutral tone, 地 connects a descriptive phrase or adjective to a verb. It describes the attitude, mood, or manner in which an action is performed.

note

Adjective + 地 + Verb

A. Common Manner Adverbs​

Manner / EmotionDescriptive Phrase with 地Full Example Sentence
Patience / Pace慢慢地走; mànmàn de zǒu; walk slowly老人慢慢地走过马路。; Lǎorén mànmàn de zǒu guò mǎlù.; The elderly person walked slowly across the road.
Focus / Attention认真地听; rènzhēn de tīng; listen attentively学生们认真地听课。; Xuéshengmen rènzhēn de tīng kè.; The students listened attentively to the class.
Happiness开心地笑; kāixīn de xiào; smile happily孩子们开心地笑了。; Háizimen kāixīn de xiào le.; The children laughed happily.
Anger生气地说; shēngqì de shuō; say angrily他生气地关上了门。; Tā shēngqì de guānshàng le mén.; He shut the door angrily.

B. Vivid Modifiers: Reduplications & Idioms​

Repeating adjectives (AABB / AA pattern) or using four-character idioms creates vivid imagery before 地:

CategoryDescriptive Unit + 地MeaningFull Example
AA Reduplication悄悄地离开; qiāoqiāo de líkāi; quietly leaveStealthily / quietly他悄悄地离开了房间。; Tā qiāoqiāo de líkāi le fángjiān.; He quietly left the room.
AA Reduplication紧紧地抱住; jǐnjǐn de bàozhù; hug tightlyFirmly / tightly妈妈紧紧地抱住了孩子。; Māma jǐnjǐn de bàozhù le háizi.; The mother hugged the child tightly.
4-Character Idiom目不转睛地看着; mù bù zhuǎn jīng de kàn zhe; stare fixatedly / watch without blinkingIntense concentration他目不转睛地看着电视。; Tā mù bù zhuǎn jīng de kàn zhe diànshì.; He stared fixatedly at the TV.

C. The Single-Syllable Exception​

tip

When using a single-syllable adjective before an action, 地 is omitted:

  • Say: 快跑; kuài pǎo; run fast (Never say 快地跑)
  • Say: 多吃; duō chī; eat more (Never say 多地吃)
  • Say: 慢走; màn zǒu; take care / walk slowly (Never say 慢地走)

2. Noun & Root Word: dì (4th Tone)​

When pronounced in the 4th tone, 地 is a content word referring to the Earth, the ground, property, or locations.

A. Ground, Floor, & Physical Space​

TermPinyin & MeaningContext Example
地上; dìshang; on the ground / on the floorFloor / ground level你的钥匙掉在地上了。; Nǐ de yàoshi diào zài dìshang le.; Your keys fell on the floor.
地面; dìmiàn; ground surfacePhysical ground plane地面很滑，小心摔倒。; Dìmiàn hěn huá, xiǎoxīn shuāidǎo.; The ground is slippery; be careful not to fall.
扫地; sǎodì; to sweep the floorChore / cleaning我每天早上扫地。; Wǒ měitiān zǎoshang sǎodì.; I sweep the floor every morning.
地下; dìxià; undergroundBelow ground level地下停车场在负一层。; Dìxià tíngchēchǎng zài fù yī céng.; The underground parking lot is on level B1.

B. Geography, Nature, & the Planet​

TermPinyin & MeaningContext Example
地球; dìqiú; the Earth / the globeThe planet地球是我们的家园。; Dìqiú shì wǒmen de jiāyuán.; The Earth is our home.
地图; dìtú; mapNavigational chart请帮我看一眼地图。; Qǐng bāng wǒ kàn yì yǎn dìtú.; Please take a look at the map for me.
地理; dìlǐ; geographyAcademic subject / terrain他很喜欢学地理。; Tā hěn xǐhuan xué dìlǐ.; He likes studying geography very much.
地震; dìzhèn; earthquakeNatural disaster昨天发生了地震。; Zuótiān fāshēng le dìzhèn.; An earthquake occurred yesterday.
土地; tǔdì; land / soilSoil / agricultural land这片土地非常肥沃。; Zhè piàn tǔdì fēicháng féiwò.; This land is very fertile.

C. Location, Place, & Transportation​

TermPinyin & MeaningContext Example
地方; dìfang; place / locationSpace or area这是什么地方？; Zhè shì shénme dìfang?; What place is this?
地址; dìzhǐ; addressPostal / living address请写下你的地址。; Qǐng xiěxià nǐ de dìzhǐ.; Please write down your address.
目的地; mùdìdì; destinationTarget end point我们到达了目的地。; Wǒmen dàodá le mùdìdì.; We arrived at our destination.
地铁; dìtiě; subway / metroUrban rail transit我坐地铁去上班。; Wǒ zuò dìtiě qù shàngbān.; I take the subway to work.

3. Study Comparison: 地 (de) vs. 得 (de)​

Both particles describe actions, but their position, grammatical order, and focus are completely different:

Feature地 (de) — Manner Marker得 (de) — Complement Marker
PositionBefore the verbAfter the verb
FormulaModifier + 地 + VerbVerb + 得 + Complement
FocusAttitude or state entering the actionOutcome, score, or performance resulting from the action
Manner Example他慢慢地走。; Tā mànmàn de zǒu.; He slowly walks.
(Focus: His pace/attitude as he walks)		他走得很慢。; Tā zǒu de hěn màn.; He walks very slowly.
(Focus: The evaluated result/speed of his walk)
Emotion Example他高兴地跑回家。; Tā gāoxìng de pǎo huí jiā.; He happily ran home.
(Focus: His emotional state while running)		他跑得很快。; Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.; He ran very fast.
(Focus: How fast the running turned out to be)

得 (de / děi / dé)

The character 得 has three distinct pronunciations. Its tone determines its grammatical function and meaning:

CharacterPinyinRoleCore MeaningQuick Anchor
得de (Neutral)Structural ParticleLinks verbs/adjectives to complements (manner, degree, possibility)跑得快; pǎo de kuài; runs fast
得děi (3rd Tone)Modal Verb"Must", "have to", "need to"我得走了; wǒ děi zǒu le; I must go
得dé (2nd Tone)Full Verb"To get", "to obtain", "to contract (illness)"得到; dédào; to obtain

1. Structural Particle​

A. Complement of State (Evaluating Manner & Action)​

  • Structure: Verb (+ Object) + Verb + 得 + Adjective/Adverb
  • Negation: Place 不 (bù) after 得 (Verb + 得 + 不 + Adjective)
Context / TargetAffirmative ExampleNegative Example
Speed / Manner他跑得快。; Tā pǎo de kuài.; He runs fast.他跑得不快。; Tā pǎo de bú kuài.; He does not run fast.
Quality你说得很好。; Nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo.; You speak very well.你说得不好。; Nǐ shuō de bù hǎo.; You don't speak well.
Time / Routine我起得很早。; Wǒ qǐ de hěn zǎo.; I wake up very early.我起得不早。; Wǒ qǐ de bù zǎo.; I don't wake up early.
With Object (Repeat verb)他说中文说得很好。; Tā shuō Zhōngwén shuō de hěn hǎo.; He speaks Chinese very well.他说中文说得不好。; Tā shuō Zhōngwén shuō de bù hǎo.; He does not speak Chinese well.

B. Potential Complements (Ability / Possibility)​

  • Affirmative: Verb + 得 + Result/Direction (Able to...)
  • Negative: Verb + 不 + Result/Direction (Unable to...)
Action & ResultAffirmative (Able to)Negative (Unable to)
See / Perceive看得见; kàn de jiàn; able to see / visible看不见; kàn bu jiàn; unable to see / invisible
Finish / Complete做得完; zuò de wán; able to finish doing做不完; zuò bu wán; unable to finish doing
Capacity / Eat吃得下; chī de xià; able to eat (has room/appetite)吃不下; chī bu xià; cannot eat (too full/sick)
Comprehend听得懂; tīng de dǒng; able to understand by hearing听不懂; tīng bu dǒng; unable to understand by hearing
Afford买得起; mǎi de qǐ; able to afford买不起; mǎi bu qǐ; unable to afford

C. Complement of Degree (Extreme Degree / "So... That...")​

  • Structure: Adjective / State + 得 + Result / Clause
Adjective / StateDegree Expression / SentenceMeaning
Tired (累)我累得不想说话。; Wǒ lèi de bù xiǎng shuōhuà.; I am so tired that I don't want to talk.Fatigue resulting in silence
Happy (高兴)她高兴得跳了起来。; Tā gāoxìng de tiào le qǐlái.; She was so happy that she jumped up.Joy resulting in jumping
Angry (气)气得要命; qì de yàomìng; mad to death / furiously angryExtreme anger
Hot (热)热得不得了; rè de bùdéliǎo; terribly hot / extremely hotUnbearable heat

2. Modal Verb: děi (3rd Tone) — "Must / Have to"​

  • Affirmative Structure: Subject + 得 (děi) + Verb
  • Negation Rule: ⚠️ Never say "不得 (bù děi)". Instead, use 不用 (bùyòng) or 不必 (búbì) ("don't have to").
FunctionAffirmative (得 - děi)Negation (不用 / 不必)
Obligation (Must leave)时间不早了，我得走了。; Shíjiān bù zǎo le, wǒ děi zǒu le.; It's late, I must go.你不用走。; Nǐ búyòng zǒu.; You don't have to leave.
Necessity (Need to do)你得多喝水。; Nǐ děi duō hē shuǐ.; You have to drink more water.你不用多喝水。; Nǐ búyòng duō hē shuǐ.; You don't need to drink more water.
Requirement (Cost/Time)这个工作得花很多时间。; Zhège gōngzuò děi huā hěn duō shíjiān.; This job requires a lot of time.这事不必花很多时间。; Zhè shì búbì huā hěn duō shíjiān.; This doesn't require much time.

3. Full Verb: dé (2nd Tone) — "To Get / To Obtain"​

Used in formal registers, compound words, and idiomatic expressions.

Usage TypeKey VocabularyExample / Set Phrase
Gain / Acquire得到; dédào; to get / to obtain我得到了一个好机会。; Wǒ dédào le yí gè hǎo jīhuì.; I got a good opportunity.
Win an Award得奖; déjiǎng; to win a prize他在比赛中得奖了。; Tā zài bǐsài zhōng déjiǎng le.; He won an award in the competition.
Score Points得分; défēn; to score points这次考试他得了满分。; Zhè cì kǎoshì tā dé le mǎnfēn.; He scored full marks on this exam.
Catch Illness得病; débìng; to fall ill她得了感冒。; Tā dé le gǎnmào.; She caught a cold.
Idiom (Resignation)得了; dé le; that's enough / stop it得了，别说了！; Dé le, bié shuō le!; Enough, stop talking!
Idiom (Dilemma)哭笑不得; kū xiào bù dé; not knowing whether to laugh or cry这件事情真让人哭笑不得。; Zhè jiàn shìqing zhēn ràng rén kū xiào bù dé.; This matter really leaves one not knowing whether to laugh or cry.

# 得 (de / děi / dé)

The character **得** has three distinct pronunciations. Its tone determines its grammatical function and meaning:

| Character | Pinyin | Role | Core Meaning | Quick Anchor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **得** | **`de`** (Neutral) | Structural Particle | Links verbs/adjectives to complements (manner, degree, possibility) | [跑得快; pǎo de kuài; runs fast](pao3de0kuai4.wav) |
| **得** | **`děi`** (3rd Tone) | Modal Verb | "Must", "have to", "need to" | [我得走了; wǒ děi zǒu le; I must go](wo3dei3zou3le0.wav) |
| **得** | **`dé`** (2nd Tone) | Full Verb | "To get", "to obtain", "to contract (illness)" | [得到; dédào; to obtain](de2dao4.wav) |

---

## 1. Structural Particle

### A. Complement of State (Evaluating Manner & Action)
* **Structure:** `Verb (+ Object) + Verb + 得 + Adjective/Adverb`
* **Negation:** Place **不 (`bù`)** *after* **得** (`Verb + 得 + 不 + Adjective`)

| Context / Target | Affirmative Example | Negative Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speed / Manner** | [他跑得快。; Tā pǎo de kuài.; He runs fast.](ta1pao3de0kuai4.wav) | [他跑得不快。; Tā pǎo de bú kuài.; He does not run fast.](ta1pao3de0bu2kuai4.wav) |
| **Quality** | [你说得很好。; Nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo.; You speak very well.](ni3shuo1de0hen3hao3.wav) | [你说得不好。; Nǐ shuō de bù hǎo.; You don't speak well.](ni3shuo1de0bu4hao3.wav) |
| **Time / Routine** | [我起得很早。; Wǒ qǐ de hěn zǎo.; I wake up very early.](wo3qi3de0hen3zao3.wav) | [我起得不早。; Wǒ qǐ de bù zǎo.; I don't wake up early.](wo3qi3de0bu4zao3.wav) |
| **With Object** *(Repeat verb)* | [他说中文说得很好。; Tā shuō Zhōngwén shuō de hěn hǎo.; He speaks Chinese very well.](ta1shuo1zhong1wen2shuo1de0hen3hao3.wav) | [他说中文说得不好。; Tā shuō Zhōngwén shuō de bù hǎo.; He does not speak Chinese well.](ta1shuo1zhong1wen2shuo1de0bu4hao3.wav) |

## 2. Modal Verb: `děi` (3rd Tone) — "Must / Have to"

* **Affirmative Structure:** `Subject + 得 (děi) + Verb`
* **Negation Rule:** ⚠️ **Never** say `"不得 (bù děi)"`. Instead, use **不用 (bùyòng)** or **不必 (búbì)** ("don't have to").

| Function | Affirmative (`得 - děi`) | Negation (`不用 / 不必`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Obligation (Must leave)** | [时间不早了，我得走了。; Shíjiān bù zǎo le, wǒ děi zǒu le.; It's late, I must go.](shi2jian1bu4zao3le0wo3dei3zou3le0.wav) | [你不用走。; Nǐ búyòng zǒu.; You don't have to leave.](ni3bu2yong4zou3.wav) |
| **Necessity (Need to do)** | [你得多喝水。; Nǐ děi duō hē shuǐ.; You have to drink more water.](ni3dei3duo1he1shui3.wav) | [你不用多喝水。; Nǐ búyòng duō hē shuǐ.; You don't need to drink more water.](ni3bu2yong4duo1he1shui3.wav) |
| **Requirement (Cost/Time)** | [这个工作得花很多时间。; Zhège gōngzuò děi huā hěn duō shíjiān.; This job requires a lot of time.](zhe4ge0gong1zuo4dei3hua1hen3duo1shi2jian1.wav) | [这事不必花很多时间。; Zhè shì búbì huā hěn duō shíjiān.; This doesn't require much time.](zhe4shi4bu2bi4hua1hen3duo1shi2jian1.wav) |

所 (suǒ)

The character 所 is pronounced in the 3rd tone: suǒ.

It is one of the most flexible structural components in Mandarin Chinese, functioning as a nominalizing particle, a passive marker, a measure word, and a noun root for physical locations and institutions.

Grammar FunctionRoleEnglish EquivalentQuick Anchor
1. Structural ParticleNominalizes actions (所 + Verb)"that which" / "what is [verbed]"所需要的; suǒ xūyào de; that which is needed
2. Formal Passive VoiceEmphasizes the receiver in passive sentences"is [verbed] by..."为人所知; wéi rén suǒ zhī; known by people
3. Measure WordClassifier for institutions, schools, hospitals"a / an" (institution)一所学校; yì suǒ xuéxiào; a school
4. Location Noun RootBase morpheme meaning "place" or "facility"place / office / station厕所; cèsuǒ; restroom
5. Core ConjunctionsForms foundational connectorstherefore / all / so-called所以; suǒyǐ; therefore / so

1. Structural Particle: 所 + Verb (+ 的)​

When placed directly in front of a transitive verb, 所 turns the verb phrase into the object of the action, meaning "that which is [verbed]" or "what is [verbed]."

Pattern: Subject + 所 + Verb + (的 + Noun)

Particle PhraseMeaningExample Sentence
所需要的; suǒ xūyào de; that which is needed / what is neededThings required这正是我们所需要的。; Zhè zhèng shì wǒmen suǒ xūyào de.; This is precisely what we need.
所说的; suǒ shuō de; what was said / that which was spokenStatements made你所说的事情属实吗？; Nǐ suǒ shuō de shìqing shǔshí ma?; Is what you said true?
所见所闻; suǒ jiàn suǒ wén; what one sees and hearsFirst-hand experience这些都是我的所见所闻。; Zhèxiē dōu shì wǒ de suǒ jiàn suǒ wén.; These are all things I personally saw and heard.
各尽所能; gè jìn suǒ néng; each does what they canGive one's best大家在工作中各尽所能。; Dàjiā zài gōngzuò zhōng gè jìn suǒ néng.; Everyone does their utmost according to their abilities at work.

2. Formal Passive Construction: 为 / 被……所 + Verb​

In formal, literary, and journalistic Chinese, 所 is inserted before the verb in passive sentences to emphasize the action's recipient.

Pattern A: Receiver + 被 + Agent + 所 + Verb
Pattern B (Literary): Receiver + 为 + Agent + 所 + Verb

Passive PatternMeaningComplete Sentence
为大家所熟悉; wéi dàjiā suǒ shúxī; well known by everyoneWidely recognized这个故事为大家所熟悉。; Zhè ge gùshì wéi dàjiā suǒ shúxī.; This story is well known to everyone.
被大家所接受; bèi dàjiā suǒ jiēshòu; accepted by everyoneApproved / welcomed新规定被大家所接受了。; Xīn guīdìng bèi dàjiā suǒ jiēshòu le.; The new rule was accepted by everyone.
为情所困; wéi qíng suǒ kùn; trapped / troubled by loveOvercome by emotion他一直为情所困。; Tā yìzhí wéi qíng suǒ kùn.; He has always been troubled by romantic entanglements.

3. Measure Word for Institutions & Buildings​

所 serves as the standard classifier for schools, hospitals, institutes, and residences.

Pattern: Number + 所 + Institution / Building

Classified NounMeasure Word PhrasePractical Example
School (学校)一所学校; yì suǒ xuéxiào; a school我家附近有一所学校。; Wǒ jiā fùjìn yǒu yì suǒ xuéxiào.; There is a school near my home.
Hospital (医院)一所医院; yì suǒ yīyuàn; a hospital他在一所大医院工作。; Tā zài yì suǒ dà yīyuàn gōngzuò.; He works at a large hospital.
House / Villa (房子)一所房子; yì suǒ fángzi; a house他们在海边买了一所房子。; Tāmen zài hǎibiān mǎi le yì suǒ fángzi.; They bought a house by the sea.
University (大学)一所大学; yì suǒ dàxué; a university这是一所世界著名大学。; Zhè shì yì suǒ shìjiè zhùmíng dàxué.; This is a world-renowned university.

4. Noun Root: Places, Facilities, & Offices​

When acting as a morpheme inside compound nouns, 所 denotes a designated place, public facility, or administrative bureau.

Facility WordPinyin & MeaningExample Sentence
厕所; cèsuǒ; restroom / toiletRestroom请问厕所在哪里？; Qǐngwèn cèsuǒ zài nǎlǐ?; Excuse me, where is the restroom?
场所; chǎngsuǒ; venue / public site / placePublic place or arena公共场所禁止吸烟。; Gōnggòng chǎngsuǒ jìnzhǐ xīyān.; Smoking is prohibited in public places.
派出所; pàichūsuǒ; local police stationLocal police substation他在派出所报了案。; Tā zài pàichūsuǒ bào le àn.; He reported the case at the local police station.
研究所; yánjiūsuǒ; research instituteScientific research center她毕业后进入了一家研究所。; Tā bìyè hòu jìnrù le yì jiā yánjiūsuǒ.; After graduation, she joined a research institute.
诊所; zhěnsuǒ; clinicMedical clinic社区诊所看病很方便。; Shèqū zhěnsuǒ kànbìng hěn fāngbiàn.; Visiting the community clinic is very convenient.

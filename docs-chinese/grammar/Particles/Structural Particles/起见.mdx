# 起见 (qǐjiàn)

**起见 (qǐjiàn)** is a formal grammatical suffix used to express the **underlying motive, purpose, or consideration** behind an action. 

It is best translated as:
* **"For the sake of..."**
* **"Out of consideration for..."**
* **"In view of..."**
* **"As a matter of..."**

It is commonly tested at the **HSK 5 / HSK 6** levels and appears frequently in formal spoken Chinese, business contexts, and written Chinese.

---

### 1. Basic Structure

起见 is almost always placed **at the beginning of a sentence** (or before the main verb) to explain the reason before stating the action taken.

> **(为了 / 为) + [2-Syllable Concept] + 起见，[Main Action]**

*Note: **为了 (wèile)** is optional. Native speakers very often drop it and just start directly with the phrase.*

---

### 2. High-Frequency Fixed Collocations

Unlike the English word "sake" (which can attach to many things: "for God's sake," "for my sake"), **起见 usually pairs with specific two-syllable abstract nouns or adjectives**, especially those related to **caution, fairness, or politeness**.

Here are the most common combinations:

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **安全起见** | *ānquán qǐjiàn* | For safety's sake |
| **保险起见** | *bǎoxiǎn qǐjiàn* | To be on the safe side / To play it safe |
| **慎重起见** | *shènzhòng qǐjiàn* | As a matter of prudence / Out of caution |
| **公平起见** | *gōngpíng qǐjiàn* | For the sake of fairness / To be fair |
| **礼貌起见** | *lǐmào qǐjiàn* | Out of politeness |
| **长远起见** | *chángyuǎn qǐjiàn* | In the long run / Taking the long-term view |

---

### 3. Example Sentences

#### A. 安全起见 (For safety's sake)
* **安全起见，上车后请系好安全带。**  
  *Ānquán qǐjiàn, shàngchē hòu qǐng jì hǎo ānquándài.*  
  For safety's sake, please fasten your seatbelt after getting in the car.

#### B. 保险起见 (To be on the safe side)
* **保险起见，我们还是提前一个小时出发吧。**  
  *Bǎoxiǎn qǐjiàn, wǒmen háishì tíqián yí ge xiǎoshí chūfā ba.*  
  To be on the safe side, we should still leave an hour early.

#### C. 公平起见 (For the sake of fairness)
* **为了公平起见，每个人都有五分钟的发言时间。**  
  *Wèile gōngpíng qǐjiàn, měi ge rén dōu yǒu wǔ fēnzhōng de fāyán shíjiān.*  
  For the sake of fairness, everyone has five minutes of speaking time.

#### D. 慎重起见 (Out of prudence/caution)
* **慎重起见，签合同前我们最好找律师看一下。**  
  *Shènzhòng qǐjiàn, qiān hétong qián wǒmen zuìhǎo zhǎo lǜshī kàn yíxià.*  
  As a matter of caution, we had better have a lawyer look over the contract before signing.

---

### 4. Nuance: "为了..." vs. "为了...起见"

You might wonder: What is the difference between saying **为了安全** and **为了安全起见**?

* **为了安全 (wèile ānquán):** "For safety." (Neutral, direct).
* **为了安全起见 (wèile ānquán qǐjiàn):** "As a matter of safety" or "Out of consideration for safety."

Adding **起见**:
1. Makes the tone **more formal and objective**.
2. Strongly implies a **precautionary mindset**—you are doing something to prevent a problem or maintain a standard principle.

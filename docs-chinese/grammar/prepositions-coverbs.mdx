# Prepositions

Mandarin prepositions (coverbs) front a prepositional phrase before the main verb to establish the spatial, temporal, instrumental, or relational frame of an action. Unlike English prepositions, many coverbs retain verbal roots and strictly govern word order directly preceding the verb phrase.

## Space, Direction & Distance (处所、方向与距离)

### [朝; cháo; Towards / Facing](chao2.wav)
* **Usage:** `朝 + Direction / Location / Target + Verb` (emphasizes physical orientation or movement heading towards a point).
* **Example:** [这列高铁朝终点站疾驰而去。; Zhè liè gāotiě cháo zhōngdiǎnzhàn jíchí ér qù.; This high-speed train speeds towards the terminal station.](zhe4_lie4_gao1tie3_chao2_zhong1dian3zhan4_ji2chi2_er2_qu4.wav)

---

### [从; cóng; From / Starting from](cong2.wav)
* **Usage:** `从 + Spatial Starting Point + Verb / 往 / 到` (marks geographic or physical point of departure).
* **Example:** [外交官从那座使馆步行到会议厅。; Wàijiāoguān cóng nà zuò shǐguǎn bùxíng dào huìyìtīng.; The diplomat walked from that embassy to the conference hall.](wai4jiao1guan1_cong2_na4_zuo4_shi3guan3_bu4xing2_dao4_hui4yi4ting1.wav)

---

### [离; lí; Away from / Distant from](li2.wav)
* **Usage:** `Location A + 离 + Location B + Adverb + 远 / 近 / Metric Distance` (measures static separation between two reference points).
* **Example:** [这家新客栈离风景区只有两公里。; Zhè jiā xīn kèzhàn lí fēngjǐngqū zhǐ yǒu liǎng gōnglǐ.; This new inn is only two kilometers away from the scenic area.](zhe4_jia1_xin1_ke4zhan4_li2_feng1jing3qu1_zhi3_you3_liang3_gong1li3.wav)

---

### [往; wǎng; Toward / In direction of](wang3.wav)
* **Usage:** `往 + Direction / Target + Action Verb` (directs motion along a specific trajectory; commonly pairs with directional monosyllabic verbs).
* **Example:** [邮递员骑着一辆摩托车往东拐去。; Yóudìyuán qí zhe yī liàng mótuōchē wǎng dōng guǎi qù.; The courier rode a motorcycle and turned toward the east.](you2di4yuan2_qi2_zhe0_yi1_liang4_mo2tuo1che1_wang3_dong1_guai3_qu4.wav)

---

### [向; xiàng; Towards / To (a target)](xiang4.wav)
* **Usage:** `向 + Person / Cardinal Direction / Abstract Target + Verb` (focuses on intended recipient or purposeful directionality).
* **Example:** [记者向那位著名的教授提出了三个疑问。; Jìzhě xiàng nà wèi zhùmíng de jiàoshòu tíchū le sān gè yíwèn.; The reporter posed three questions to that renowned professor.](ji4zhe3_xiang4_na4_wei4_zhu4ming2_de0_jiao4shou4_ti2chu1_le0_san1_ge4_yi2wen4.wav)

---

### [沿; yán; Along / Following](yan2.wav)
* **Usage:** `沿 + Route / Perimeter / Waterway / Street + Movement Verb` (specifies linear path adherence).
* **Example:** [巡逻队沿那条蜿蜒的河流搜寻失踪人员。; Xúnluóduì yán nà tiáo wānyán de héliú sōuxún shīzōng rényuán.; The patrol team searched along that winding river for missing personnel.](xun2luo2dui4_yan2_na4_tiao2_wan1yan2_de0_he2liu2_sou1xun2_shi1zong1_ren2yuan2.wav)

---

### [在; zài; At / In / On](zai4.wav)
* **Usage:** `在 + Physical Location + Action Verb` (establishes the locative scene where an action occurs).
* **Example:** [建筑师在这间安静的画室里绘制图纸。; Jiànzhùshī zài zhè jiān ānjìng de huàshì lǐ huìzhì túzhǐ.; The architect draws blueprints in this quiet studio.](jian4zhu4shi1_zai4_zhe4_jian1_an1jing4_de0_hua4shi4_li3_hui4zhi4_tu2zhi3.wav)

---

### [自; zì; From / Originating from](zi4.wav)
* **Usage:** `自 + Spatial Origin + Verb` (formal/written register equivalent of 从).
* **Example:** [这批珍贵的文物自欧洲海运抵达港口。; Zhè pī zhēnguì de wénwù zì Ōuzhōu hǎiyùn dǐdá gǎngkǒu.; This batch of precious cultural relics arrived at the port via sea freight from Europe.](zhe4_pi1_zhen1gui4_de0_wen2wu4_zi4_ou1zhou1_hai3yun4_di3da2_gang3kou3.wav)

## Time & Opportunity (时间与时机)

### [趁; chèn; Seizing / Taking advantage of](chen4.wav)
* **Usage:** `趁 + Temporal Window / Opportunity / Fleeting State + Action Verb` (acts before conditions change).
* **Example:** [工程师打算趁天气晴朗检修那架风力发电机。; Gōngchéngshī dǎsuàn chèn tiānqì qínglǎng jiǎnxiū nà jià fēnglì fādiànjī.; The engineer plans to seize the clear weather to inspect and repair that wind turbine.](gong1cheng2shi1_da3suan4_chen4_tian1qi4_qing2lang3_jian3xiu1_na4_jia4_feng1li4_fa1dian4ji1.wav)

---

### [从; cóng; From / Since](cong2.wav)
* **Usage:** `从 + Temporal Starting Point + 起 / 开始 / 到...` (designates temporal origin).
* **Example:** [这间实验室从下周一开始实行封闭管理。; Zhè jiān shíyànshì cóng xià zhōu yī kāishǐ shíxíng fēngbì guǎnlǐ.; This laboratory will implement closed-off management starting next Monday.](zhe4_jian1_shi2yan4shi4_cong2_xia4_zhou1_yi1_kai1shi3_shi2xing2_feng1bi4_guan3li3.wav)

---

### [当; dāng; When / At the time of](dang1.wav)
* **Usage:** `当 + Event / Temporal Anchor + (的时 / 的时候)，Main Clause` (synchronizes primary event with a specific moment).
* **Example:** [当警报声响起时，值班医生迅速赶往急诊室。; Dāng jǐngbàoshēng xiǎngqǐ shí, zhíbān yīshēng xùnsù gǎnwǎng jízhěnshì.; When the alarm sounded, the on-duty doctor rushed swiftly to the emergency room.](dang1_jing3bao4sheng1_xiang3qi3_shi2_zhi2ban1_yi1sheng1_xun4su4_gan3wang3_ji2zhen3shi4.wav)

---

### [随着; suízhe; Along with / In the wake of](sui2zhe0.wav)
* **Usage:** `随着 + Evolving Situation / Trend, Main Clause` (governs concurrent gradual developments).
* **Example:** [随着经济结构优化，这家企业逐步拓展了海外市场。; Suízhe jīngjì jiégòu yōuhuà, zhè jiā qǐyè zhúbù tuòzhǎn le hǎiwài shìchǎng.; Along with the optimization of the economic structure, this enterprise gradually expanded its overseas market.](sui2zhe0_jing1ji4_jie2gou4_you1hua4_zhe4_jia1_qi3ye4_zhu2bu4_tuo4zhan3_le0_hai3wai4_shi4chang3.wav)

---

### [在; zài; In / During (a time frame)](zai4.wav)
* **Usage:** `在 + Temporal Duration / Period + 期间 / 之中 / 内` (frames action within a bounded period).
* **Example:** [代表团在整个访问期间签署了一项合作备忘录。; Dàibiǎotuán zài zhěnggè fǎngwèn qījiān qiānshǔ le yī xiàng hézuò bèiwànglù.; The delegation signed a memorandum of cooperation during the entire visit.](dai4biao3tuan2_zai4_zheng3ge4_fang3wen4_qi1jian1_qian1shu3_le0_yi1_xiang4_he2zuo4_bei4wang4lu4.wav)

---

### [自; zì; Since / From (formal)](zi4.wav)
* **Usage:** `自 + Historical Point / Date + 以来 / 起` (formal temporal point of inception).
* **Example:** [自去年改革以来，该部门的工作效率显著提高。; Zì qùnián gǎigé yǐlái, gāi bùmén de gōngzuò xiàolǜ xiǎnzhù tígāo.; Since last year's reform, this department's work efficiency has improved noticeably.](zi4_qu4nian2_gai3ge2_yi3lai2_gai1_bu4men2_de0_gong1zuo4_xiao4lv4_xian3zhu4_ti2gao1.wav)

## Target, Recipient & Accompaniment (对象、受益者与伴随)

### [对; duì; To / Toward / Regarding](dui4.wav)
* **Usage:** `对 + Person / Entity / Issue + Verb / Attitudinal Adjective` (marks recipient of attitudes, evaluations, or actions).
* **Example:** [导师对这篇论文的论证逻辑感到满意。; Dǎoshī duì zhè piān lùnwén de lùnzhèng luóji gǎndào mǎnyì.; The advisor felt satisfied with the argumentative logic of this thesis.](dao3shi1_dui4_zhe4_pian1_lun4wen2_de0_lun4zheng4_luo2ji0_gan3dao4_man3yi4.wav)

---

### [给; gěi; To / For (beneficiary / recipient)](gei3.wav)
* **Usage:** `给 + Recipient + Verb` or `Verb + 给 + Recipient` (marks transference recipient or service beneficiary).
* **Example:** [前台秘书给那位应聘者递上一杯热茶。; Qiántái mìshū gěi nà wèi yìngpìnzhě dì shang yī bēi rèchá.; The front-desk receptionist handed a cup of hot tea to that applicant.](qian2tai2_mi4shu1_gei3_na4_wei4_ying4pin4zhe3_di4_shang0_yi1_bei1_re4cha2.wav)

---

### [跟; gēn; With / And / From](gen1.wav)
* **Usage:** `跟 + Interactive Partner + Verb` (interactive accompaniment or comparative baseline).
* **Example:** [会计师跟那家银行的业务经理核对了账户账目。; Kuàijìshī gēn nà jiā yínháng de yèwù jīnglǐ héduì le zhànghù zhàngmù.; The accountant verified the account entries with that bank's operations manager.](kuai4ji4shi1_gen1_na4_jia1_yin2hang2_de0_ye4wu4_jing1li3_he2dui4_le0_zhang4hu4_zhang4mu4.wav)

---

### [和; hé; With / Together with](he2.wav)
* **Usage:** `和 + Companion + 一起 + Verb` (joint participation in an action).
* **Example:** [地质学家和考察队员一起攀登了那座险峻的山峰。; Dìzhìxuéjiā hé kǎochá duìyuán yīqǐ pāndēng le nà zuò xiǎnjùn de shānfēng.; The geologist climbed that precipitous mountain peak together with the expedition team members.](di4zhi4xue2jia1_he2_kao3cha2_dui4yuan2_yi1qi3_pan1deng1_le0_na4_zuo4_xian3jun4_de0_shan1feng1.wav)

---

### [同; tóng; With / And (formal)](tong2.wav)
* **Usage:** `同 + Diplomatic / Formal Partner + Verb` (mid-to-high register coverb for bilateral interactions).
* **Example:** [外交部长同外宾就两国经贸合作举行了会谈。; Wàijiāo bùzhǎng tóng wàibīn jiù liǎng guó jīngmào hézuò jǔxíng le huìtán.; The foreign minister held talks with the foreign guests on bilateral economic and trade cooperation.](wai4jiao1_bu4zhang3_tong2_wai4bin1_jiu4_liang3_guo2_jing1mao4_he2zuo4_ju3xing2_le0_hui4tan2.wav)

---

### [与; yǔ; With / And (formal / high-register)](yu3.wav)
* **Usage:** `与 + Counterpart + Verb (Formal / Written)` (establishes high-register relational binding).
* **Example:** [投资方与研发团队签订了一份具有约束力的合同。; Tóuzīfāng yǔ yánfā tuánduì qiāndìng le yī fèn jùyǒu yuēshùlì de hétong.; The investor signed a binding contract with the research and development team.](tou2zi1fang1_yu3_yan2fa1_tuan2dui4_qian1ding4_le0_yi1_fen4_ju4you3_yue1shu4li4_de0_he2tong0.wav)

---

### [为; wèi; For / On behalf of](wei4.wav)
* **Usage:** `为 + Beneficiary / Client + Verb` (indicates intentional service or action done for someone's sake).
* **Example:** [社区志愿者为独居老人提供了一项免费理发服务。; Shèqū zhìyuànzhě wèi dújū lǎorén tígōng le yī xiàng miǎnfèi lǐfà fúwù.; Community volunteers provided a free haircut service for solitary seniors.](she4qu1_zhi4yuan4zhe3_wei4_du2ju1_lao3ren2_ti2gong1_le0_yi1_xiang4_mian3fei4_li3fa4_fu2wu4.wav)

* **Usage:** `针对 + Specific Issue / Target Problem + Action Verb` (focuses an intervention or countermeasure).
* **Example:** [专家组针对城市内涝制定了一套排水改造方案。; Zhuānjiāzǔ zhēnduì chéngshì nèilào zhìdìng le yī tào páishuǐ gǎizào fāng'àn.; The expert panel formulated a drainage renovation plan targeted at urban waterlogging.](zhuan1jia1zu3_zhen1dui4_cheng2shi4_nei4lao4_zhi4ding4_le0_yi1_tao4_pai2shui3_gai3zao4_fang1an4.wav)

## Reason, Purpose & Role (原因、目的与身份)

### [鉴于; jiànyú; In view of / Considering that](jian4yu2.wav)

Conjunctions

Mandarin conjunctions link linguistic units—words, phrases, or full clauses—to make syntactic cohesion and logical relationships explicit. While parallel coordinators connect grammatically equal arguments, correlative subordinators operate across paired clauses to mark transition, conditionality, causality, or progression.

Coordination (并列连词)​

并且; bìngqiě; And / Furthermore / Moreover​

  • Usage: Clause 1 / VP 1 + 并且 + VP 2 (coordinates two concurrent or sequential actions/predicates in a shared subject context).
  • Example: 委员会通过了那项预算方案，并且立即启动了招标程序。; Wěiyuánhuì tōngguò le nà xiàng yùsuàn fāng'àn, bìngqiě lìjí qǐdòng le zhāobiāo chéngxù.; The committee approved that budget proposal and immediately initiated the bidding procedure.

而; ér; And / As well as​

  • Usage: Adjective / VP 1 + 而 + Adjective / VP 2 (links coordinated descriptive attributes or balanced actions).
  • Example: 这座新建的体育馆外观宏伟而典雅。; Zhè zuò xīn jiàn de tǐyùguǎn wàiguān hóngwěi ér diǎnyǎ.; The exterior of this newly built stadium is majestic as well as elegant.

和; hé; And / As well as​

  • Usage: Noun Phrase 1 + 和 + Noun Phrase 2 (neutral coordinator linking parallel nominal constituents; rarely joins verb phrases).
  • Example: 班主任核对了毕业生的档案和推荐信。; Bānzhǔrèn héduì le bìyèshēng de dàng'àn hé tuījiànxìn.; The homeroom teacher verified the graduates' dossiers and letters of recommendation.

以及; yǐjí; As well as / Along with / And​

  • Usage: Primary Noun Phrases + 以及 + Secondary / Appended Noun Phrase (formal written coordinator introducing supplementary or ancillary entities).
  • Example: 该条约详细规定了各方的领海边界、渔业权以及航行准则。; Gāi tiáoyuē xiángxì guīdìng le gè fāng de lǐnghǎi biānjiè, yúyèquán yǐjí hángxíng zhǔnzé.; The treaty stipulated in detail each party's territorial sea boundaries, fishing rights, as well as navigation guidelines.

与; yǔ; And / With (formal)​

  • Usage: Noun Phrase 1 + 与 + Noun Phrase 2 (high-register, literary coordinator linking parallel nouns, concepts, or titles).
  • Example: 历史学家潜心考证古代贸易路线与货币制度的演变。; Lìshǐxuéjiā qiǎnxīn kǎozhèng gǔdài màoyì lùxiàn yǔ huòbì zhìdù de yǎnbiàn.; Historians dedicated themselves to investigating the evolution of ancient trade routes and monetary systems.

Progression & Addition (递进连词)​

不但; búdàn; Not only​

  • Usage: 不但 + Clause 1，而且 / 还 + Clause 2 (introduces baseline proposition before escalating in scale, severity, or benefit).
  • Example: 这款新型电池不但重量大幅减轻，使用寿命还延长了五年。; Zhè kuǎn xīnxíng diànchí búdàn zhòngliàng dàfú jiǎnqīng, shǐyòng shòumìng hái yáncháng le wǔ nián.; Not only is the weight of this new battery drastically reduced, but its service life is also extended by five years.

不仅; bùjǐn; Not only / Not just​

  • Usage: 不仅 + Clause 1，也 / 而且 + Clause 2 (signals that the initial clause is merely a subset of a broader scope).
  • Example: 举办这场国际博览会不仅提振了当地旅游业，也吸引了数家跨国企业的关注。; Jǔbàn zhè chǎng guójì bólǎnhuì bùjǐn tízhèn le dāngdì lǚyóuyè, yě xīyǐn le shù jiā kuàguó qǐyè de guānzhù.; Hosting this international expo not only boosted local tourism, but also attracted the attention of several multinational enterprises.

此外; cǐwài; Besides / In addition / Moreover​

  • Usage: Clause 1。此外，Clause 2 (sentence-initial transition adding an independent supplemental argument or dimension).
  • Example: 施工队修缮了那座倾斜的钟楼；此外，他们还加固了周围的石制围栏。; Shīgōngduì xiūshàn le nà zuò qīngxié de zhōnglóu; cǐwài, tāmen hái jiāgù le zhōuwéi de shízhì wéilán.; The construction team renovated that leaning bell tower; furthermore, they reinforced the surrounding stone fences.

而且; érqiě; Furthermore / And also​

  • Usage: Clause 1 + 而且 + Clause 2 (escalating connector; frequently pairs with 不但 or 不仅 to reinforce progressive degree).
  • Example: 质检员发现这批布料质地粗糙，而且存在严重的褪色缺陷。; Zhìjiǎnyuán fāxiàn zhè pī bùliào zhìdì cūcāo, érqiě cúnzài yánzhòng de tuìsè quēxiàn.; The quality inspector discovered that this batch of fabric had a rough texture, and furthermore suffered from severe fading defects.

况且; kuàngqiě; Moreover / Besides​

  • Usage: Clause 1，况且 + Clause 2 (injects an additional, often decisive factual argument that cements the preceding claim).
  • Example: 翻越这道陡峭的雪山太危险，况且暴风雪随时可能封锁唯一的山口。; Fānyuè zhè dào dǒuqiào de xuěshān tài wēixiǎn, kuàngqiě bàofēngxuě suíshí kěnéng fēngsuǒ wéiyī de shānkǒu.; Scaling this steep snow mountain is too dangerous, moreover a blizzard could seal off the only pass at any time.

Alternative & Choice (选择与取舍连词)​

不然; bùrán; Otherwise / Or else​

  • Usage: Imperative / Warning Clause，不然 + Adverse Consequence (states the negative outcome if the condition is unmet).
  • Example: 护士必须立即给病患更换药液，不然注射针头会发生堵塞。; Hùshi bìxū lìjí gěi bìnghuàn gēnghuàn yàoyè, bùrán zhùshè zhēntóu huì fāshēng dǔsè.; The nurse must replace the patient's medicinal liquid immediately, otherwise the injection needle will become clogged.

还是; háishì; Or (in questions)​

  • Usage: Alternative A + 还是 + Alternative B? (strictly restricted to interrogative options or indirect embedded choice inquiries).
  • Example: 航天局决定先发射那枚探测卫星，还是优先安排载人货运飞船？; Hángtiānjú juédìng xiān fāshè nà méi tàncè wèixīng, háishì yōuxiān ānpái zàirén huòyùn fēichuán?; Will the space agency decide to launch that exploration satellite first, or give priority to arranging the manned cargo spacecraft?

或者; huòzhě; Or (in statements)​

  • Usage: Option A + 或者 + Option B (presents interchangeable or non-exclusionary possibilities in declarative sentences).
  • Example: 客户可以下载那份电子对账单，或者亲自前往营业网点打印凭条。; Kèhù kěyǐ xiàzài nà fèn diànzǐ duìzhàngdān, huòzhě qīnzì qiánwǎng yíngyè wǎngdiǎn dǎyìn píngtiáo.; Customers can download that electronic statement or visit a service branch in person to print a voucher.

要么; yàome; Either... or​

  • Usage: 要么 + Option A，要么 + Option B (sets up an exclusive disjunctive dilemma forcing a choice between two courses).
  • Example: 被告要么全额赔偿原告的经济损失，要么依照判决接受强制执行。; Bèigào yàome quán'é péicháng yuángào de jīngjì sǔnshī, yàome yīzhào pànjué jiēshòu qiángzhì zhíxíng.; The defendant must either compensate the plaintiff's financial loss in full or accept mandatory enforcement according to the ruling.

与其; yǔqí; Rather than / Better than​

  • Usage: 与其 + Rejected Path，不如 + Preferred Alternative (evaluates and dismisses an inferior plan in favor of a superior one).
  • Example: 与其盲目扩大产能，不如投资引进一套尖端净化设备。; Yǔqí mángmù kuòdà chǎnnéng, bùrú tóuzī yǐnjìn yī tào jiānduān jìnghuà shèbèi.; Rather than blindly expanding production capacity, it is better to invest in introducing a set of cutting-edge purification equipment.

Adversative & Transition (转折连词)​

不过; búguò; However / But / Only that​

  • Usage: Clause 1，不过 + Clause 2 (mild adversative modifying, supplementing, or qualifying the scope of the preceding remark).
  • Example: 这批古董瓷器保存得相当完好，不过底座边缘有一处细微的裂痕。; Zhè pī gǔdǒng cíqì bǎocún de xiāngdāng wánhǎo, búguò dǐzuò biānyuán yǒu yī chù xìwēi de lièhén.; This batch of antique porcelain was preserved quite well; however, there is a tiny fissure along the edge of the base.

但是; dànshì; But / However / Yet​

  • Usage: Clause 1，但是 + Clause 2 (standard direct adversative expressing explicit, emphatic logical contrast).
  • Example: 侦探搜集了许多零散的线索，但是凶手依然没有留下任何指纹。; Zhēntàn sōují le xǔduō língsǎn de xiànsuǒ, dànshì xiōngshǒu yīrán méiyǒu liúxià rènhé zhǐwén.; The detective gathered many fragmented clues, but the killer still left no fingerprints behind.

而; ér; But / Yet / On the other hand​

  • Usage: Clause 1，而 + Clause 2 (written adversative contrasting two comparative viewpoints, metrics, or entities).
  • Example: 传统燃油汽车销量逐渐下滑，而混合动力车型的市场份额却稳步攀升。; Chuántǒng rányóu qìchē xiàoliàng zhújiàn xiàhuá, ér hùnhé dònglì chèxíng de shìchǎng fèn'é què wěnbù pānshēng.; Traditional fuel vehicle sales gradually declined, while the market share of hybrid models climbed steadily.

尽管; jǐnguǎn; Although / In spite of​

  • Usage: 尽管 + Objective Reality / Obstacle，(但是 / 还是 / 却) + Outcome (concessive marker acknowledging facts that fail to impede the result).
  • Example: 尽管气温骤降至零下三十度，地质队员依然按时完成了那处岩层的钻探任务。; Jǐnguǎn qìwēn zhòujiàng zhì língxià sānshí dù, dìzhì duìyuán yīrán ànshí wánchéng le nà chù yáncéng de zuàntàn rènwu.; Even though the temperature plummeted to thirty degrees below zero, the geological team members still finished the drilling task on that stratum on time.

可是; kěshì; But / However​

  • Usage: Clause 1，可是 + Clause 2 (spoken-to-written adversative indicating unexpected contradiction or emotional friction).
  • Example: 采购员跑遍了城里几家药房，可是始终没有买到那种特效退烧药水。; Cǎigòuyuán pǎo biàn le chéng lǐ jǐ jiā yàofáng, kěshì shǐzhōng méiyǒu mǎi dào nà zhǒng tèxiào tuìshāo yàoshuǐ.; The purchasing agent visited several pharmacies across the city, yet never managed to buy that specific fever-reducing liquid.

然而; rán'ér; However / Yet / But​

  • Usage: Clause 1。然而，Clause 2 (formal, high-register adversative placed clause-initially to reverse a narrative expectation).
  • Example: 探险家克服重重险阻登上了峰顶，然而一场罕见的暴风将营地彻底摧毁了。; Tànxiǎnjiā kèfú chóngchóng xiǎnzǔ dēng shàng le fēngdǐng, rán'ér yī cháng hǎnjiàn de bàofēng jiāng yíngdì chèdǐ cuīhuǐ le.; The explorer overcame myriad obstacles to reach the summit; however, a rare gale completely destroyed the camp.

虽然; suīrán; Although / Even though​

  • Usage: 虽然 + Concessive Clause，但是 / 可是 + Main Clause (standard concessive marker conceding a point before asserting a primary counterpoint).
  • Example: 虽然这位老工匠视力有所衰退，但他雕刻的那尊木雕依然栩栩如生。; Suīrán zhè wèi lǎo gōngjiàng shìlì yǒu suǒ shuāituì, dàn tā diāokè de nà zūn mùdiāo yīrán xǔxǔ-rúshēng.; Although this veteran craftsman's eyesight has declined somewhat, that wooden sculpture he carved is still vivid and lifelike.

则; zé; But / In contrast / While​

  • Usage: Subject 1 + VP 1，Subject 2 + 则 + VP 2 (formal contrastive connective placed immediately after the second subject to mark parallel comparison).
  • Example: 主教练带领主力队员进行力量训练，替补球员则在一旁练习防守战术。; Zhǔjiàoliàn dàilǐng zhǔlì duìyuán jìnxíng lìliàng xùnliàn, tìbǔ qiúyuán zé zài yīpáng liànxí fángshǒu zhànshù.; The head coach led the main players through strength training, while the substitute players practiced defensive tactics on the sidelines.

Cause, Effect & Inference (因果与推论连词)​

从而; cóng'ér; Thus / Thereby / As a result​

  • Usage: Action / Policy Clause + 从而 + Organic Result (formal marker uniting an implemented method directly to its ensuing consequence).
  • Example: 农场引进了滴灌节水农业技术，从而大幅降低了水资源的消耗率。; Nóngchǎng yǐnjìn le dīguàn jiéshuǐ nóngyè jìshù, cóng'ér dàfú jiàngdī le shuǐzīyuán de xiāohàolǜ.; The farm introduced drip irrigation water-saving agricultural technology, thereby substantially decreasing the consumption rate of water resources.

既然; jìrán; Since / Now that​

  • Usage: 既然 + Established Fact / Premise，就 / 那 + Inference / Directive (takes an established premise as justification for a logical deduction or prompt).
  • Example: 既然合约条款已经达成共识，双方代表就应该尽快举办签字仪式。; Jìrán héyuē tiáokuǎn yǐjīng dáchéng gòngshí, shuāngfāng dàibiǎo jiù yīnggāi jǐnkuài jǔbàn qiānzì yíshì.; Since the terms of the contract have reached a consensus, both delegations should organize the signing ceremony as soon as possible.

可见; kějiàn; It can clearly be seen that / So​

  • Usage: Factual Evidence / Observation。可见，Deductive Conclusion (draws an analytical inference directly from demonstrated evidence).
  • Example: 仅过去一个季度就有数万名游客退订船票，可见这场传染病对邮轮产业的冲击极大。; Jǐn guòqù yī gè jīdù jiù yǒu shù wàn míng yóukè tuìdìng chuánpiào, kějiàn zhè chǎng chuánrǎnbìng duì yóulún chǎnyè de chōngjī jí dà.; Tens of thousands of tourists refunded their ship tickets in just the past quarter; thus it can be seen that this infectious disease had an enormous impact on the cruise industry.

然后; ránhòu; Then / After that / Afterwards​

  • Usage: 先 + Action 1，然后 + Action 2 (articulates chronological step-by-step procedure).
  • Example: 烘焙师先称量好那袋面粉的重量，然后往搅拌缸里缓慢注入温水。; Hōngbèishī xiān chēngliáng hǎo nà dài miànfěn de zhòngliàng, ránhòu wǎng jiǎobàngāng lǐ huǎnmàn zhùrù wēnshuǐ.; The baker first weighed that bag of flour accurately, and then slowly poured warm water into the mixing vat.

所以; suǒyǐ; Therefore / As a result / So​

  • Usage: (因为 + Cause)，所以 + Result (explicitly introduces the resulting consequence in causal constructions).
  • Example: 仓储部未能按时交付核心零配件，所以整个装配车间不得不暂时停工。; Cāngchǔbù wèinéng ànshí jiāofù héxīn língpèijiàn, suǒyǐ zhěnggè zhuāngpèi chējiān bùdébù zànshí tínggōng.; The warehouse department failed to deliver the core spare parts on schedule, so the entire assembly workshop had to suspend work temporarily.

因此; yīncǐ; Therefore / For this reason / Thus​

  • Usage: Context Cause。因此，Resulting Action / Decision (formal objective conclusion marker placed clause-initially).
  • Example: 该沿海城市地下水过度开采，因此市政当局出台了一套严格的限水政策。; Gāi yánhǎi chéngshì dìxiàshuǐ guòdù kāicǎi, yīncǐ shìzhèng dāngjú chūtái le yī tào yángé de xiànshuǐ zhèngcè.; Groundwater in this coastal city was excessively extracted; therefore, the municipal authorities enacted a strict water rationing policy.

因而; yīn'ér; Therefore / As a result / Thus​

  • Usage: Causal Factor，因而 + Organic Outcome (literary coordinator establishing intrinsic causal entailment between two propositions).
  • Example: 那位青年学者提出的崭新假说缺乏确凿的实证支撑，因而遭到了学界的广泛质疑。; Nà wèi qīngnián xuézhě tíchū de zhǎnxīn jiǎshuō quēfá quèzáo de shízhèng zhīchēng, yīn'ér zāodào le xuéjiè de guǎngfàn zhìyí.; The brand-new hypothesis proposed by that young scholar lacked solid empirical support, and thus met with widespread skepticism in academia.

因为; yīnwèi; Because / On account of​

  • Usage: 因为 + Objective Cause，(所以) + Consequence (states primary causal etiology; pairs optionally with 所以 / 因此).
  • Example: 因为发动机突发机械故障，机长果断决定将那架客机迫降在备降机场。; Yīnwèi fādòngjī tūfā jīxiè gùzhàng, jīzhǎng guǒduàn juédìng jiāng nà jià kèjī pòjiàng zài bèijiàng jīchǎng.; Because the engine suffered a sudden mechanical failure, the captain decisively resolved to make a forced landing with that airliner at an alternate airport.

于是; yúshì; Thereupon / Consequently / As a result​

  • Usage: Clause 1 (Development)，于是 + Clause 2 (Prompt Reactive Measure) (narrative causality marking an immediate subsequent action).
  • Example: 大雾笼罩了整条高速公路，于是交警封锁了各个收费站的入口通道。; Dàwù lǒngzhào le zhěng tiáo gāosù gōnglù, yúshì jiāojǐng fēngsuǒ le gè gè shòufèizhàn de rùkǒu tōngdào.; Heavy fog engulfed the entire expressway, whereupon the traffic police blocked the entrance lanes of every toll booth.

Condition & Requirement (条件连词)​

不管; bùguǎn; No matter / Regardless of​

  • Usage: 不管 + Variable Condition / Interrogative Pronoun，都 / 也 + Unaltered Outcome (colloquial unconditional marker indicating total invariance).
  • Example: 不管外界舆论如何评价那部电影，这位年轻导演都坚持走实验艺术路线。; Bùguǎn wàijiè yúlùn rúhé píngjià nà bù diànyǐng, zhè wèi niánqīng dǎoyǎn dōu jiānchí zǒu shíyàn yìshù lùxiàn.; No matter how external public opinion evaluates that movie, this young director insists on taking the experimental art path.

除非; chúfēi; Only if / Unless​

  • Usage: 除非 + Sole Condition，才 + Outcome or 除非 + Condition，否则 + Failure (isolates an exclusive prerequisite).
  • Example: 除非承租人提前结清欠缴的三个月租金，房东才会退还那笔押金。; Chúfēi chéngzūrén tíqián jiéqīng qiànjiǎo de sān gè yuè zūjīn, fángdōng cái huì tuìhuán nà bǐ yājīn.; Only if the tenant clears the three months of overdue rent in advance will the landlord return that deposit.

无论; wúlùn; Regardless of / No matter what​

  • Usage: 无论 + Choice A 还是 Choice B / Wh- Word，都 / 也 + Predicate (formal unconditional marker governing all contingencies).
  • Example: 无论国际金融市场的汇率怎样波动，这家外贸企业的外汇储备规模都保持稳定。; Wúlùn guójì jīnróng shìchǎng de huìlǜ zěnyàng bōdòng, zhè jiā wàimào qǐyè de wàihuì chǔbèi guīmó dōu bǎochí wěndìng.; Regardless of how exchange rates in international financial markets fluctuate, this foreign trade enterprise's foreign exchange reserve volume remains stable.

只要; zhǐyào; As long as / Provided that​

  • Usage: 只要 + Sufficient Condition，就 + Result (identifies an adequate trigger guaranteed to fulfill the main predicate).
  • Example: 只要救援直升机成功空投那箱急救药品，受困伤员的病情就能得到初步缓解。; Zhǐyào jiùyuán zhíshēngjī chénggōng kōngtóu nà xiāng jíjiù yàopǐn, shòukùn shāngyuán de bìngqíng jiù néng dédào chūbù huǎnjiě.; As long as the rescue helicopter successfully air-drops that box of first-aid medication, the stranded casualties' conditions can be initially alleviated.

只有; zhǐyǒu; Only if / Only when​

  • Usage: 只有 + Indispensable Condition，才 + Outcome (sets a strict necessary requirement without which the result cannot occur).
  • Example: 只有通读了整部古代典籍的注解，学者才能准确破解那些残缺的石刻铭文。; Zhǐyǒu tōngdú le zhěng bù gǔdài diǎnjí de zhùjiě, xuézhě cái néng zhǔnquè pòjiě nàxiē cánquē de shíkè míngwén.; Only by reading through the annotations of the entire ancient classic can the scholar accurately decipher those fragmented stone inscriptions.

Hypothesis & Concession (假设与让步连词)​

即使; jíshǐ; Even if / Even though​

  • Usage: 即使 + Extreme Hypothesis，也 + Sustained Conclusion (grants a hypothetical worst-case or extreme scenario that leaves the outcome unchanged).
  • Example: 即使对方辩友抛出了极其尖锐的反驳论据，主辩手也丝毫没有慌张。; Jíshǐ duìfāng biànyǒu pāochū le jíqí jiānruì de fǎnbó lùnjù, zhǔbiànshǒu yě sīháo méiyǒu huāngzhāng.; Even if the opposing debater tossed out an extremely incisive counterargument, the lead debater was not flustered in the slightest.

假如; jiǎrú; If / Supposing / In case​

  • Usage: 假如 + Hypothetical Proposition，(就 / 便) + Consequent Action (formal speculative condition marker).
  • Example: 假如这家造船厂无法按期交付那艘万吨货轮，他们将面临巨额违约赔偿。; Jiǎrú zhè jiā zàochuánchǎng wúfǎ ànqī jiāofù nà sōu wàn dūn huòlún, tāmen jiāng miànlín jù'é wéiyuē péicháng.; Supposing this shipyard is unable to deliver that ten-thousand-ton cargo ship on schedule, they will face colossal breach-of-contract indemnities.

哪怕; nǎpà; Even if / No matter how​

  • Usage: 哪怕 + Concessive Minimum / Extremity，也 + Unyielding Clause (emphasizes resilient determination against extreme odds).
  • Example: 哪怕只剩下一线微弱的希望，搜救犬训导员也不会放弃在废墟中搜寻幸存者。; Nǎpà zhǐ shèng xià yī xiàn wēiruò de xīwàng, sōujiùquǎn xùndǎoyuán yě bú huì fàngqì zài fèixū zhōng sōuxún xìngcúnzhě.; Even if only a faint thread of hope remains, the search-and-rescue canine trainer will not give up searching for survivors amidst the ruins.

如果; rúguǒ; If / In the event of​

  • Usage: 如果 + Hypothetical Condition，(就 / 便) + Result (neutral, standard conditional marker across spoken and written registers).
  • Example: 如果实验室培育出的新品种小麦能够抵御干旱，粮食产量便会大幅递增。; Rúguǒ shíyànshì péiyù chū de xīn pǐnzhǒng xiǎomài nénggòu dǐyù gānhàn, liángshi chǎnliàng biàn huì dàfú dìzēng.; If the new variety of wheat cultivated by the laboratory can withstand drought, grain yield will increase substantially.

万一; wànyī; In case / If by any chance​

  • Usage: (以防 / 倘若) + 万一 + Unforeseen Contingency，(就) + Countermeasure (frames low-probability risks or accidental emergencies).
  • Example: 保安队随身携带着防毒面具，以防万一化工厂车间发生有毒气体泄漏事故。; Bǎo'ānduì suíshēn xiédài zhe fángdú miànjù, yǐ fáng wànyī huàgōngchǎng chējiān fāshēng yǒudú qìtǐ xièlòu shìgù.; The security team carried gas masks on them to guard against the unlikely event of a toxic gas leakage accident occurring in the chemical plant workshop.

要是; yàoshi; If / In case / Supposing​

  • Usage: 要是 + Conversational Hypothesis，就 + Result (informal, spoken-register hypothetical marker).
  • Example: 要是今晚航班没有因为雷阵雨延误，我们就能赶上那场精彩的芭蕾舞剧首演。; Yàoshi jīnwǎn hángbān méiyǒu yīnwèi léizhènyǔ yánwù, wǒmen jiù néng gǎn shàng nà chǎng jīngcǎi de bālěiwǔjù shǒuyǎn.; If tonight's flight had not been delayed due to thunderstorms, we would have been able to make it to the premiere of that wonderful ballet drama.

Purpose & Prevention (目的连词)​

以便; yǐbiàn; So that / In order to​

  • Usage: Preparatory Action，以便 + Facilitated Target Goal (formal purpose conjunction facilitating an intended subsequent operation).
  • Example: 档案馆将所有破损的历史报刊录入了数字化系统，以便研究人员随时检索。; Dàng'ànguǎn jiāng suǒyǒu pòsǔn de lìshǐ bàokān lùrù le shùzìhuà xìtǒng, yǐbiàn yánjiū rényuán suíshí jiǎnsuǒ.; The archives entered all damaged historical newspapers and periodicals into a digital system so that researchers could retrieve them at any time.

以免; yǐmiǎn; In order to avoid / Lest​

  • Usage: Preventive Measure，以免 + Avoided Negative Consequence (formal preventive purpose marker indicating risk deterrence).
  • Example: 搬运工在那台贵重的显微镜外箱上缠绕了数层气泡垫，以免运输途中遭受震裂。; Bānyùngōng zài nà tái guìzhòng de xiǎnwéijìng wàixiāng shang chánrào le shù céng qìpàodiàn, yǐmiǎn yùnshū túzhōng zāoshòu zhènliè.; The movers wrapped several layers of bubble wrap around the outer case of that valuable microscope to avoid shock cracking during transit.

免得; miǎnde; So as not to / Lest​

  • Usage: Precautionary Action，免得 + Avoided Mishap (colloquial preventive purpose equivalent of 以免).
  • Example: 徒步向导提醒大家随手关好帐篷的防蚊纱网，免得森林里的毒飞虫钻入睡袋。; Túbù xiàngdǎo tíxǐng dàjiā suíshǒu guān hǎo zhàngpeng de fángwén shāwǎng, miǎnde sēnlín lǐ de dú fēichóng zuān rù shuìdài.; The hiking guide reminded everyone to shut the tent's mosquito netting conveniently behind them, lest venomous flying insects from the forest crawl into the sleeping bags.

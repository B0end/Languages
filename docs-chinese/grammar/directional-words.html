Directional Words

Directional words (方位词) function as locative nouns that attach immediately after reference nouns (Noun + Direction) without prepositions to create locative phrases, or stand independently as subjects, objects, and attributive modifiers (Direction + 的 + Noun).

Vertical Spatial Relations (上下)​

上; shàng; On / Above / Up​

  • Usage: Bound postposition attached directly to nouns (Noun + 上) indicating physical surface contact or abstract scope.
  • Example: 那一本词典在这一张桌子上。; Nà yī běn cídiǎn zài zhè yī zhāng zhuōzi shàng.; That dictionary is on this desk.

上面; shàngmiàn; Top / Above / Surface​

  • Usage: Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 上边 (shàngbiān) and colloquial 上头 (shàngtou). Slots as subject, object, or attributive modifier.
  • Example: 上面那一层货架上摆着三瓶红酒。; Shàngmiàn nà yī céng huòjià shàng bǎizhe sān píng hóngjiǔ.; Three bottles of red wine are arranged on that upper shelf.

下; xià; Under / Below / Beneath​

  • Usage: Bound postposition affixed directly to nouns (Noun + 下) signifying physical position underneath or an environmental condition.
  • Example: 两只小猫躲在这一棵大树下乘凉。; Liǎng zhī xiǎomāo duǒ zài zhè yī kē dàshù xià chéngliáng.; Two kittens are hiding under this big tree to enjoy the shade.

下面; xiàmiàn; Below / Bottom / Underneath​

  • Usage: Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 下边 (xiàbiān) and colloquial 下头 (xiàtou). Functions as subject, object, or attributive modifier.
  • Example: 这一把椅子下面放着一只皮箱。; Zhè yī bǎ yǐzi xiàmiàn fàngzhe yī zhī píxiāng.; A leather suitcase is placed underneath this chair.

Sagittal Spatial Relations (前后)​

前; qián; Front / Before​

  • Usage: Bound postposition affixed directly to nouns (Noun + 前) indicating spatial position facing outward or temporal precedence.
  • Example: 门前停着一辆崭新的自行车。; Mén qián tíngzhe yī liàng zhǎnxīn de zìxíngchē.; A brand-new bicycle is parked in front of the door.

前面; qiánmiàn; In front / Ahead​

  • Usage: Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 前边 (qiánbiān) and spoken 前头 (qiántou). Slots as subject, object, or directional modifier.
  • Example: 那一所学校前面有一家大型超市。; Nà yī suǒ xuéxiào qiánmiàn yǒu yī jiā dàxíng chāoshì.; There is a large supermarket in front of that school.

后; hòu; Behind / Back / After​

  • Usage: Bound postposition affixed directly to nouns (Noun + 后) indicating physical position at the rear or temporal succession.
  • Example: 房后有一片郁郁葱葱的竹林。; Fáng hòu yǒu yī piàn yùyùcōngcōng de zhúlín.; There is a lush bamboo grove behind the house.

后面; hòumiàn; Behind / Rear / Back​

  • Usage: Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 后边 (hòubiān) and colloquial 后头 (hòutou). Functions as subject, object, or modifier.
  • Example: 后面那一辆黑色轿车开得太快了。; Hòumiàn nà yī liàng hēisè jiàochē kāi de tài kuài le.; That black sedan behind is driving too fast.

Lateral Spatial Relations (左右)​

左边; zuǒbiān; Left side​

  • Usage: Independent locative noun; interchangeable with 左面 (zuǒmiàn). Expresses position to the left of a focal reference point (Noun + 左边).
  • Example: 坐在我左边的那一位医生是李大夫。; Zuò zài wǒ zuǒbiān de nà yī wèi yīshēng shì Lǐ dàifu.; The doctor sitting on my left side is Dr. Li.

右边; yòubiān; Right side​

  • Usage: Independent locative noun; interchangeable with 右面 (yòumiàn). Expresses position to the right of a focal reference point (Noun + 右边).
  • Example: 那一座红色的邮局就在图书馆右边。; Nà yī zuò hóngsè de yóujú jiù zài túshūguǎn yòubiān.; That red post office is right on the right side of the library.

Interior and Exterior Spatial Relations (内外)​

里; lǐ; In / Inside​

  • Usage: Bound postposition attached to common nouns (Noun + 里) to create a locative; never attaches directly to proper geographic country or city names.
  • Example: 这一部手机在她的手提包里。; Zhè yī bù shǒujī zài tā de shǒutíbāo lǐ.; This mobile phone is inside her handbag.

里面; lǐmiàn; Inside / Interior​

  • Usage: Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 里边 (lǐbiān) and colloquial 里头 (lǐtou). Operates independently as subject, object, or modifier.
  • Example: 这一间会议室里面摆着二十把新椅子。; Zhè yī jiān huìyìshì lǐmiàn bǎizhe èrshí bǎ xīn yǐzi.; Twenty new chairs are arranged inside this conference room.

外; wài; Outside / Beyond​

  • Usage: Bound postposition affixed to nouns (Noun + 外) designating space exterior to an established boundary or limit.
  • Example: 窗外正下着一场倾盆大雨。; Chuāng wài zhèng xiàzhe yī cháng qīngpén dàyǔ.; A torrential downpour is falling outside the window.

外面; wàimiàn; Outside / Exterior​

  • Usage: Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 外边 (wàibiān) and colloquial 外头 (wàitou). Operates as subject, object, or modifier.
  • Example: 外面停着两辆空载的出租车。; Wàimiàn tíngzhe liǎng liàng kōngzài de chūzūchē.; Two vacant taxis are parked outside.

Proximity and Relative Spatial Relations (中·旁·对面)​

中间; zhōngjiān; Middle / Between / Center​

  • Usage: Locative noun designating the intermediate interval between two coordinates (A 和 B 中间) or the interior center of a defined perimeter.
  • Example: 在两座大厦中间有一条狭窄的小巷。; Zài liǎng zuò dàshà zhōngjiān yǒu yī tiáo xiázhǎi de xiǎoxiàng.; There is a narrow alley between the two skyscrapers.

旁边; pángbiān; Beside / Side / Next to​

  • Usage: Locative noun designating immediate lateral adjacency (Noun + 旁边).
  • Example: 那一栋教学楼旁边有一片宽阔的操场。; Nà yī dòng jiàoxuélóu pángbiān yǒu yī piàn kuānkuò de cāochǎng.; Beside that classroom building is an expansive sports field.

对面; duìmiàn; Opposite / Across from​

  • Usage: Locative noun designating an opposing location facing the reference entity directly across a dividing axis (Noun + 对面).
  • Example: 那一家银行对面新开了一家花店。; Nà yī jiā yínháng duìmiàn xīn kāi le yī jiā huādiàn.; A new flower shop opened across from that bank.

Cardinal Directions (东南西北)​

东边; dōngbiān; East side / East​

  • Usage: Directional noun denoting easterly coordinate; interchangeable with 东面 (dōngmiàn). Follows reference nouns or functions as subject/object.
  • Example: 城市东边新建起了一座现代化的体育馆。; Chéngshì dōngbiān xīn jiànqǐ le yī zuò xiàndàihuà de tǐyùguǎn.; A modern stadium was recently built on the east side of the city.

西边; xībiān; West side / West​

  • Usage: Directional noun denoting westerly coordinate; interchangeable with 西面 (xīmiàn). Follows reference nouns or functions as subject/object.
  • Example: 村庄西边有一条清澈见底的小溪。; Cūnzhuāng xībiān yǒu yī tiáo qīngchè jiàndǐ de xiǎoxī.; There is a crystal-clear stream on the west side of the village.

南边; nánbiān; South side / South​

  • Usage: Directional noun denoting southerly coordinate; interchangeable with 南面 (nánmiàn). Follows reference nouns or functions as subject/object.
  • Example: 公园南边坐落着一座保存完好的古寺。; Gōngyuán nánbiān zuòluòzhe yī zuò bǎocún wánhǎo de gǔsì.; A well-preserved ancient temple sits on the south side of the park.

北边; běibiān; North side / North​

  • Usage: Directional noun denoting northerly coordinate; interchangeable with 北面 (běimiàn). Follows reference nouns or functions as subject/object.
  • Example: 办公楼北边整齐地停着一排大客车。; Bàngōnglóu běibiān zhěngqí de tíngzhe yī pái dàkèchē.; A row of tour buses is parked neatly on the north side of the office building.

Environmental Scope and Vicinity (周围·附近)​

周围; zhōuwéi; Surroundings / Perimeter / Around​

  • Usage: Locative noun denoting the circumambient boundary or peripheral area encircling a core target (Noun + 周围).
  • Example: 那一座水库周围栽种了数千棵柳树。; Nà yī zuò shuǐkù zhōuwéi zāizhòng le shùqiān kē liǔshù.; Thousands of willow trees are planted around the perimeter of that reservoir.

附近; fùjìn; Vicinity / Nearby area​

  • Usage: Locative noun denoting the immediate geographic area adjacent to a landmark (Noun + 附近), or functioning independently as a spatial noun.
  • Example: 火车站附近有三家二十四小时营业的药店。; Huǒchēzhàn fùjìn yǒu sān jiā èrshísì xiǎoshí yíngyè de yàodiàn.; There are three 24-hour pharmacies in the vicinity of the railway station.

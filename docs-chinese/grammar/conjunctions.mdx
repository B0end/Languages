# Conjunctions

Mandarin conjunctions link linguistic units—words, phrases, or full clauses—to make syntactic cohesion and logical relationships explicit. While parallel coordinators connect grammatically equal arguments, correlative subordinators operate across paired clauses to mark transition, conditionality, causality, or progression.

## Coordination (并列连词)

### [并且; bìngqiě; And / Furthermore / Moreover](bing4qie3.wav)
* **Usage:** `Clause 1 / VP 1 + 并且 + VP 2` (coordinates two concurrent or sequential actions/predicates in a shared subject context).
* **Example:** [委员会通过了那项预算方案，并且立即启动了招标程序。; Wěiyuánhuì tōngguò le nà xiàng yùsuàn fāng'àn, bìngqiě lìjí qǐdòng le zhāobiāo chéngxù.; The committee approved that budget proposal and immediately initiated the bidding procedure.](wei3yuan2hui4_tong1guo4_le0_na4_xiang4_yu4suan4_fang1an4_bing4qie3_li4ji2_qi3dong4_le0_zhao1biao1_cheng2xu4.wav)

### [而; ér; And / As well as](er2.wav)
* **Usage:** `Adjective / VP 1 + 而 + Adjective / VP 2` (links coordinated descriptive attributes or balanced actions).
* **Example:** [这座新建的体育馆外观宏伟而典雅。; Zhè zuò xīn jiàn de tǐyùguǎn wàiguān hóngwěi ér diǎnyǎ.; The exterior of this newly built stadium is majestic as well as elegant.](zhe4_zuo4_xin1_jian4_de0_ti3yu4guan3_wai4guan1_hong2wei3_er2_dian3ya3.wav)

### [和; hé; And / As well as](he2.wav)
* **Usage:** `Noun Phrase 1 + 和 + Noun Phrase 2` (neutral coordinator linking parallel nominal constituents; rarely joins verb phrases).
* **Example:** [班主任核对了毕业生的档案和推荐信。; Bānzhǔrèn héduì le bìyèshēng de dàng'àn hé tuījiànxìn.; The homeroom teacher verified the graduates' dossiers and letters of recommendation.](ban1zhu3ren4_he2dui4_le0_bi4ye4sheng1_de0_dang4an4_he2_tui1jian4xin4.wav)

### [以及; yǐjí; As well as / Along with / And](yi3ji2.wav)
* **Usage:** `Primary Noun Phrases + 以及 + Secondary / Appended Noun Phrase` (formal written coordinator introducing supplementary or ancillary entities).
* **Example:** [该条约详细规定了各方的领海边界、渔业权以及航行准则。; Gāi tiáoyuē xiángxì guīdìng le gè fāng de lǐnghǎi biānjiè, yúyèquán yǐjí hángxíng zhǔnzé.; The treaty stipulated in detail each party's territorial sea boundaries, fishing rights, as well as navigation guidelines.](gai1_tiao2yue1_xiang2xi4_gui1ding4_le0_ge4_fang1_de0_ling3hai3_bian1jie4_yu2ye4quan2_yi3ji2_hang2xing2_zhun3ze2.wav)

### [与; yǔ; And / With (formal)](yu3.wav)
* **Usage:** `Noun Phrase 1 + 与 + Noun Phrase 2` (high-register, literary coordinator linking parallel nouns, concepts, or titles).
* **Example:** [历史学家潜心考证古代贸易路线与货币制度的演变。; Lìshǐxuéjiā qiǎnxīn kǎozhèng gǔdài màoyì lùxiàn yǔ huòbì zhìdù de yǎnbiàn.; Historians dedicated themselves to investigating the evolution of ancient trade routes and monetary systems.](li4shi3xue2jia1_qian3xin1_kao3zheng4_gu3dai4_mao4yi4_lu4xian4_yu3_huo4bi4_zhi4du4_de0_yan3bian4.wav)

## Progression & Addition (递进连词)

### [不但; búdàn; Not only](bu2dan4.wav)
* **Usage:** `不但 + Clause 1，而且 / 还 + Clause 2` (introduces baseline proposition before escalating in scale, severity, or benefit).
* **Example:** [这款新型电池不但重量大幅减轻，使用寿命还延长了五年。; Zhè kuǎn xīnxíng diànchí búdàn zhòngliàng dàfú jiǎnqīng, shǐyòng shòumìng hái yáncháng le wǔ nián.; Not only is the weight of this new battery drastically reduced, but its service life is also extended by five years.](zhe4_kuan3_xin1xing2_dian4chi2_bu2dan4_zhong4liang4_da4fu2_jian3qing1_shi3yong4_shou4ming4_hai2_yan2chang2_le0_wu3_nian2.wav)

### [不仅; bùjǐn; Not only / Not just](bu4jin3.wav)
* **Usage:** `不仅 + Clause 1，也 / 而且 + Clause 2` (signals that the initial clause is merely a subset of a broader scope).
* **Example:** [举办这场国际博览会不仅提振了当地旅游业，也吸引了数家跨国企业的关注。; Jǔbàn zhè chǎng guójì bólǎnhuì bùjǐn tízhèn le dāngdì lǚyóuyè, yě xīyǐn le shù jiā kuàguó qǐyè de guānzhù.; Hosting this international expo not only boosted local tourism, but also attracted the attention of several multinational enterprises.](ju3ban4_zhe4_chang3_guo2ji4_bo2lan3hui4_bu4jin3_ti2zhen4_le0_dang1di4_lv3you2ye4_ye3_xi1yin3_le0_shu4_jia1_kua4guo2_qi3ye4_de0_guan1zhu4.wav)

### [此外; cǐwài; Besides / In addition / Moreover](ci3wai4.wav)
* **Usage:** `Clause 1。此外，Clause 2` (sentence-initial transition adding an independent supplemental argument or dimension).
* **Example:** [施工队修缮了那座倾斜的钟楼；此外，他们还加固了周围的石制围栏。; Shīgōngduì xiūshàn le nà zuò qīngxié de zhōnglóu; cǐwài, tāmen hái jiāgù le zhōuwéi de shízhì wéilán.; The construction team renovated that leaning bell tower; furthermore, they reinforced the surrounding stone fences.](shi1gong1dui4_xiu1shan4_le0_na4_zuo4_qing1xie2_de0_zhong1lou2_ci3wai4_ta1men0_hai2_jia1gu4_le0_zhou1wei2_de0_shi2zhi4_wei2lan2.wav)

---

### [而且; érqiě; Furthermore / And also](er2qie3.wav)
* **Usage:** `Clause 1 + 而且 + Clause 2` (escalating connector; frequently pairs with 不但 or 不仅 to reinforce progressive degree).
* **Example:** [质检员发现这批布料质地粗糙，而且存在严重的褪色缺陷。; Zhìjiǎnyuán fāxiàn zhè pī bùliào zhìdì cūcāo, érqiě cúnzài yánzhòng de tuìsè quēxiàn.; The quality inspector discovered that this batch of fabric had a rough texture, and furthermore suffered from severe fading defects.](zhi4jian3yuan2_fa1xian4_zhe4_pi1_bu4liao4_zhi4di4_cu1cao1_er2qie3_cun2zai4_yan2zhong4_de0_tui4se4_que1xian4.wav)

### [况且; kuàngqiě; Moreover / Besides](kuang4qie3.wav)
* **Usage:** `Clause 1，况且 + Clause 2` (injects an additional, often decisive factual argument that cements the preceding claim).
* **Example:** [翻越这道陡峭的雪山太危险，况且暴风雪随时可能封锁唯一的山口。; Fānyuè zhè dào dǒuqiào de xuěshān tài wēixiǎn, kuàngqiě bàofēngxuě suíshí kěnéng fēngsuǒ wéiyī de shānkǒu.; Scaling this steep snow mountain is too dangerous, moreover a blizzard could seal off the only pass at any time.](fan1yue4_zhe4_dao4_dou3qiao4_de0_xue3shan1_tai4_wei1xian3_kuang4qie3_bao4feng1xue3_sui2shi2_ke3neng2_feng1suo3_wei2yi1_de0_shan1kou3.wav)

## Alternative & Choice (选择与取舍连词)

### [不然; bùrán; Otherwise / Or else](bu4ran2.wav)
* **Usage:** `Imperative / Warning Clause，不然 + Adverse Consequence` (states the negative outcome if the condition is unmet).
* **Example:** [护士必须立即给病患更换药液，不然注射针头会发生堵塞。; Hùshi bìxū lìjí gěi bìnghuàn gēnghuàn yàoyè, bùrán zhùshè zhēntóu huì fāshēng dǔsè.; The nurse must replace the patient's medicinal liquid immediately, otherwise the injection needle will become clogged.](hu4shi0_bi4xu1_li4ji2_gei3_bing4huan4_geng1huan4_yao4ye4_bu4ran2_zhu4she4_zhen1tou2_hui4_fa1sheng1_du3se4.wav)

---

### [还是; háishì; Or (in questions)](hai2shi4.wav)
* **Usage:** `Alternative A + 还是 + Alternative B?` (strictly restricted to interrogative options or indirect embedded choice inquiries).
* **Example:** [航天局决定先发射那枚探测卫星，还是优先安排载人货运飞船？; Hángtiānjú juédìng xiān fāshè nà méi tàncè wèixīng, háishì yōuxiān ānpái zàirén huòyùn fēichuán?; Will the space agency decide to launch that exploration satellite first, or give priority to arranging the manned cargo spacecraft?](hang2tian1ju2_jue2ding4_xian1_fa1she4_na4_mei2_tan4ce4_wei4xing1_hai2shi4_you1xian1_an1pai2_zai4ren2_huo4yun4_fei1chuan2.wav)

---

### [或者; huòzhě; Or (in statements)](huo4zhe3.wav)
* **Usage:** `Option A + 或者 + Option B` (presents interchangeable or non-exclusionary possibilities in declarative sentences).
* **Example:** [客户可以下载那份电子对账单，或者亲自前往营业网点打印凭条。; Kèhù kěyǐ xiàzài nà fèn diànzǐ duìzhàngdān, huòzhě qīnzì qiánwǎng yíngyè wǎngdiǎn dǎyìn píngtiáo.; Customers can download that electronic statement or visit a service branch in person to print a voucher.](ke4hu4_ke3yi3_xia4zai4_na4_fen4_dian4zi3_dui4zhang4dan1_huo4zhe3_qin1zi4_qian2wang3_ying2ye4_wang3dian3_da3yin4_ping2tiao2.wav)

---

### [要么; yàome; Either... or](yao4me0.wav)
* **Usage:** `要么 + Option A，要么 + Option B` (sets up an exclusive disjunctive dilemma forcing a choice between two courses).
* **Example:** [被告要么全额赔偿原告的经济损失，要么依照判决接受强制执行。; Bèigào yàome quán'é péicháng yuángào de jīngjì sǔnshī, yàome yīzhào pànjué jiēshòu qiángzhì zhíxíng.; The defendant must either compensate the plaintiff's financial loss in full or accept mandatory enforcement according to the ruling.](bei4gao4_yao4me0_quan2e2_pei2chang2_yuan2gao4_de0_jing1ji4_sun3shi1_yao4me0_yi1zhao4_pan4jue2_jie1shou4_qiang2zhi4_zhi2xing2.wav)

---

### [与其; yǔqí; Rather than / Better than](yu3qi2.wav)
* **Usage:** `与其 + Rejected Path，不如 + Preferred Alternative` (evaluates and dismisses an inferior plan in favor of a superior one).
* **Example:** [与其盲目扩大产能，不如投资引进一套尖端净化设备。; Yǔqí mángmù kuòdà chǎnnéng, bùrú tóuzī yǐnjìn yī tào jiānduān jìnghuà shèbèi.; Rather than blindly expanding production capacity, it is better to invest in introducing a set of cutting-edge purification equipment.](yu3qi2_mang2mu4_kuo4da4_chan3neng2_bu4ru2_tou2zi1_yin3jin4_yi1_tao4_jian1duan1_jing4hua4_she4bei4.wav)

## Adversative & Transition (转折连词)

### [不过; búguò; However / But / Only that](bu2guo4.wav)
* **Usage:** `Clause 1，不过 + Clause 2` (mild adversative modifying, supplementing, or qualifying the scope of the preceding remark).
* **Example:** [这批古董瓷器保存得相当完好，不过底座边缘有一处细微的裂痕。; Zhè pī gǔdǒng cíqì bǎocún de xiāngdāng wánhǎo, búguò dǐzuò biānyuán yǒu yī chù xìwēi de lièhén.; This batch of antique porcelain was preserved quite well; however, there is a tiny fissure along the edge of the base.](zhe4_pi1_gu3dong3_ci2qi4_bao3cun2_de0_xiang1dang1_wan2hao3_bu2guo4_di3zuo4_bian1yuan2_you3_yi1_chu4_xi4wei1_de0_lie4hen2.wav)

* **Usage:** `Clause 1，但是 + Clause 2` (standard direct adversative expressing explicit, emphatic logical contrast).

## Cause, Effect & Inference (因果与推论连词)

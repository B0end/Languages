Negation

Mandarin negation adverbs modulate propositional truth along two grammatical axes: aspectual realization (habitual/stative denial vs. perfective non-completion) and pragmatic force (objective refutation vs. deontic prohibition vs. epistemic reservation). Syntactically, negators occupy the immediate pre-verbal or pre-adjectival slot, strictly barring perfective aspect particles like 了 from co-occurring in the predicate.

Core Aspectual & Stative Negation (时态与性状基础否定)​

不; bù; Not / Non-​

  • Usage: Default negator for timeless facts, habitual behaviors, volitional intent, future projections, and gradable adjectives (不 + Verb/Adj); incompatible with completed aspect (*不 + Verb + 了).
  • Example: 那家跨国制药企业坚决不妥协，拒绝签署一份附带不合理条款的授权合同。; Nà jiā kuàguó zhìyào qǐyè jiānjué bù tuǒxié, jùjué qiānshǔ yí fèn fùdài bù hélǐ tiáokuǎn de shòuquán hétong.; That multinational pharmaceutical enterprise resolutely refuses to compromise, declining to sign a licensing contract attached with unreasonable clauses.

没; méi; Have not / Did not​

  • Usage: Aspectual negator indicating the non-realization or non-completion of an action in the past or present (没 + Verb), or the absence of an entity (没有 + Noun).
  • Example: 质检员在彻底核验前没在那批精密轴承的出厂合格证上盖章。; Zhìjiǎnyuán zài chèdǐ héyàn qián méi zài nà pī jīngmì zhóuchéng de chūchǎng hégézhèng shàng gàizhāng.; The quality inspector did not stamp the factory conformity certificate of that batch of precision bearings prior to a thorough verification.

未; wèi; Not yet / Have not​

  • Usage: Formal and written aspectual negator denoting "not yet occurred" or "not having reached"; substitutes for 没有 in administrative, legal, and academic texts (未 + Verb).
  • Example: 城市规划委员会至今尚未批准那一套极具争议的高架桥拓宽方案。; Chéngshì guīhuà wěiyuánhuì zhìjīn shàngwèi pīzhǔn nà yí tào jí jù zhēngyì de gāojiàqiáo tuòkuān fāng'àn.; The urban planning committee has not yet approved that highly controversial viaduct widening scheme to this day.

Prohibitive & Negative Imperatives (祈使禁止与告诫)​

别; bié; Don't / Do not​

  • Usage: Conversational prohibitive adverb used to issue negative commands, dissuasions, or preventative warnings (别 + Verb (+ 了)).
  • Example: 领队严厉警告队员别靠近那一处极易发生坍塌的陡峭冰裂缝。; Lǐngduì yánlì jǐnggào duìyuán bié kàojìn nà yí chù jí yì fāshēng tāntā de dǒuqiào bīnglièfèng.; The team leader sternly warned members not to approach that steep crevasse that is extremely prone to collapsing.

勿; wù; Do not / Never​

  • Usage: Formal and classical prohibitive particle used in official notices, legal statutes, and public cautionary signs (请勿 / 切勿 + Verb).
  • Example: 博物馆在展厅入口设立警示牌，提醒观众切勿抚摸那尊珍贵的北魏石雕佛像。; Bówùguǎn zài zhǎntīng rùkǒu shèlì jǐngshìpái, tíxǐng guānzhòng qièwù fǔmō nà zūn zhēnguì de Běiwèi shídiāo fóxiàng.; The museum erected a warning sign at the exhibition entrance, reminding visitors under no circumstances to touch that precious Northern Wei stone Buddha sculpture.

Absolute & Resolute Negation (决意与极致否定)​

决不; juébù; Under no circumstances / Definitely not​

  • Usage: Emphatic modal negator expressing uncompromising personal resolution, formal defiance, or non-negotiable rejection (决不 + Verb).
  • Example: 恪尽职守的船长立下誓言，在所有乘客撤离前决不离开那艘受损的客轮。; Kèjìnzhíshǒu de chuánzhǎng lìxià shìyán, zài suǒyǒu chéngkè chèlí qián juébù líkāi nà sōu shòusǔn de kèlún.; The dutiful captain made a vow that under no circumstances would he leave that damaged passenger ship before all passengers had evacuated.

毫不; háobù; Not in the least / Without an iota of​

  • Usage: Scalar intensifier negating an attribute down to zero magnitude (毫不 + Two-syllable Verb/Adj); frequently heads psychological and action verbs like 犹豫 (hesitate) or 动摇 (waver).
  • Example: 年轻的消防战士毫不犹豫地冲进那栋浓烟滚滚的木质居民楼展开搜救。; Niánqīng de xiāofáng zhànshì háobù yóuyù de chōngjìn nà dòng nóngyān gǔngǔn de mùzhì jūmínlóu zhǎnkāi sōujiù.; The young firefighter rushed into that wooden apartment building billowing dense smoke without the slightest hesitation to launch a rescue.

无; wú; Without / Lack / Non-existent​

  • Usage: Classical and formal negator asserting the total absence of a property or condition; directly governs abstract noun objects (无 + Noun) or acts within four-character compounds (毫无偏私).
  • Example: 这位公正的审判长在做出最终裁决时毫无偏私，充分体现了法律的尊严。; Zhè wèi gōngzhèng de shěnpànzhǎng zài zuòchū zuìzhōng cáijué shí háowú piānsī, chōngfèn tǐxiànle fǎlǜ de zūnyán.; When making the final ruling, this impartial presiding judge was completely devoid of personal bias, fully exemplifying the dignity of the law.

Epistemic & Essential Negation (推测与条件否定)​

未必; wèibì; Not necessarily / May not​

  • Usage: Epistemic modal negator that qualifies an absolute assumption, indicating uncertainty or rejecting categorical determinism without asserting a direct opposite (未必 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 企业即便斥巨资引进这一批自动化设备，其实际产能也未必能立即翻倍。; Qǐyè jíbiàn chì jùzī yǐnjìn zhè yì pī zìdònghuà shèbèi, qí shíjì chǎnnéng yě wèibì néng lìjí fānbèi.; Even if the enterprise spends massive capital to introduce this batch of automated equipment, its actual production capacity may not necessarily double immediately.

非; fēi; Is not / Non- / Must (in double negative)​

  • Usage: Classical copular negator meaning "is not"; primarily functions in modern grammar within the correlative double-negative construction 非...不可 to express categorical obligation or inescapable outcome.
  • Example: 宇航员非经历这一套严酷的高原低氧适应性特训不可，方能适应空间站环境。; Yǔhángyuán fēi jīnglì zhè yí tào yánkù de gāoyuán dīyǎng shìyìngxìng tèxùn bùkě, fāng néng shìyìng kōngjiānzhàn huánjìng.; The astronauts must undergo this rigorous set of high-altitude hypoxic adaptation training in order to adapt to the space station environment.

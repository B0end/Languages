# Degree & Approximation

Degree and approximation adverbs modify adjectives, psych-verbs, and numeric structures to calibrate intensity, comparative rank, or quantitative precision. Selection depends on syntactic positioning (pre-verbal vs. post-verbal complement), register (colloquial vs. formal), and whether the comparison is relative, absolute, or dynamic.

## Absolute & High Degree (绝对程度与极度)

### [很; hěn; Very / Quite](hen3.wav)
* **Usage:** Unmarked, baseline intensifier placed before adjectives or psychological verbs (`很 + Adj/Verb`); serves as the standard structural placeholder in non-comparative descriptive predicates.
* **Example:** [那个博物馆珍藏着一件历史价值很高的青铜器。; Nàge bówùguǎn zhēncángzhe yí jiàn lìshǐ jiàzhí hěn gāo de qīngtóngqì.; That museum preserves a bronze vessel of very high historical value.](na4ge0_bo2wu4guan3_zhen1cang2zhe0_yi2_jian4_li4shi3_jia4zhi2_hen3_gao1_de0_qing1tong2qi4.wav)

### [非常; fēicháng; Very / Exceptionally](fei1chang2.wav)
* **Usage:** Standard high-intensity degree adverb preceding adjectives, modal verbs, or cognitive-emotional verbs (`非常 + Adj/Verb`); conveys greater intensity than `很`.
* **Example:** [营养师推荐了一款对糖尿病患者非常有益的健康食谱。; Yíngyǎngshī tuījiànle yì kuǎn duì tángniàobìng huànzhě fēicháng yǒuyì de jiànkāng shípǔ.; The nutritionist recommended a healthy recipe that is exceptionally beneficial for diabetic patients.](ying2yang3shi1_tui1jian4le0_yi4_kuan3_dui4_tang2niao4bing4_huan4zhe3_fei1chang2_you3yi4_de0_jian4kang1_shi2pu3.wav)

### [特别; tèbié; Especially / Particularly](te4bie2.wav)
* **Usage:** Precedes predicates to express exceptional prominence or distinctive intensity; can also serve as an adjective meaning "unique".

### [十分; shífēn; Fully / Utterly / Extremely](shi2fen1.wav)
* **Usage:** Formal intensifier denoting a comprehensive, near-total degree (literally "ten tenths"); modifies gradable adjectives and stative verbs.

### [极; jí; Extremely / Exceedingly](ji2.wav)
* **Usage:** Highest-tier degree marker; functions predominantly as a post-adjectival complement with `了` (`Adj + 极了`), or directly pre-modifies select monosyllabic adjectives in literary styles.

## Comparative & Relative Degree (相对程度与比较)

### [更; gèng; Even more / Further](geng4.wav)
* **Usage:** Relative degree adverb indicating escalation on top of an existing baseline; used in comparative sentences (`比...更...`) or implicit comparative contexts.
* **Example:** [新提出的这套节能方案比原计划更能节省开支。; Xīn tíchū de zhè tào jiénéng fāng'àn bǐ yuán jìhuà gèng néng jiéshěng kāizhī.; This newly proposed energy-saving scheme can cut expenses even more than the original plan.](xin1_ti2chu1_de0_zhe4_tao4_jie2neng2_fang1'an4_bi3_yuan2_ji4hua4_geng4_neng2_jie2sheng3_kai1zhi1.wav)

---

### [更加; gèngjiā; Even more / Still more](geng4jia1.wav)
* **Usage:** Formal, multi-syllable equivalent of `更`; primarily modifies two-syllable adjectives and complex verbal constructions to signify amplified progression.
* **Example:** [政府颁布的这项新法规让企业减排的标准更加明确。; Zhèngfǔ bānbù de zhè xiàng xīn fǎguī ràng qǐyè jiǎnpái de biāozhǔn gèngjiā míngquè.; This new regulation promulgated by the government makes emission reduction standards for enterprises even more explicit.](zheng4fu3_ban1bu4_de0_zhe4_xiang4_xin1_fa3gui1_rang4_qi3ye4_jian3pai2_de0_biao1zhun3_geng4jia1_ming2que4.wav)

---

### [最; zuì; Most / -est (Superlative)](zui4.wav)
* **Usage:** Superlative degree adverb expressing the supreme or terminal point within a referenced comparative set (`最 + Adj/Verb`).
* **Example:** [这座国际机场是全国起降货运航班最繁忙的枢纽。; Zhè zuò guójì jīchǎng shì quánguó qǐjiàng huòyùn hángbān zuì fánmáng de shūniǔ.; This international airport is the most active hub in the entire country for landing and departing cargo flights.](zhe4_zuo4_guo2ji4_ji1chang3_shi4_quan2guo2_qi3jiang4_huo4yun4_hang2ban1_zui4_fan2mang2_de0_shu1niu3.wav)

---

### [比较; bǐjiào; Comparatively / Relatively](bi3jiao4.wav)
* **Usage:** Attenuates assertions by marking moderate comparative intensity ("fairly / relatively"); modifies adjectives and stative verbs without needing a direct comparator.
* **Example:** [技术人员建议挑选那款性能比较稳定的操作系统。; Jìshù rényuán jiànyì tiāoxuǎn nà kuǎn xìngnéng bǐjiào wěndìng de cāozuò xìtǒng.; Technicians suggest selecting that operating system with relatively stable performance.](ji4shu4_ren2yuan2_jian4yi4_tiao1xuan3_na4_kuan3_xing4neng2_bi3jiao4_wen3ding4_de0_cao1zuo4_xi4tong3.wav)

---

### [尤其; yóuqí; Especially / Particularly](you2qi2.wav)
* **Usage:** Singling-out adverb that isolates a specific item within a broader class to show a heightened degree; regularly paired with copula `是` (`尤其/特别是 + Focal Element`).
* **Example:** [展厅里陈列的现代艺术品吸引了大量观众，尤其是那一幅抽象油画。; Zhǎntīng lǐ chénliè de xiàndài yìshùpǐn xīyǐnle dàliàng guānzhòng, yóuqí shì nà yì fú chōuxiàng yóuhuà.; The modern artworks displayed in the exhibition hall attracted vast audiences, especially that abstract oil painting.](zhan3ting1_li3_chen2lie4_de0_xian4dai4_yi4shu4pin3_xi1yin3le0_da4liang4_guan1zhong4_you2qi2_shi4_na4_yi4_fu2_chou1xiang4_you2hua4.wav)

### [稍微; shāowēi; Slightly / A little bit](shao1wei1.wav)
* **Usage:** Designates minimal degree or slight adjustment; commonly coordinates with post-adjectival diminutive markers (`稍微 + Adj/Verb + 一点儿 / 一些`).
* **Example:** [调琴师把那根琴弦稍微调紧了一点儿。; Tiáoqínshī bǎ nà gēn qínxián shāowēi tiáojǐn le yìdiǎnr.; The piano tuner tightened that musical string slightly.](tiao2qin2shi1_ba3_na4_gen1_qin2xian2_shao1wei1_tiao2jin3_le0_yi4dian3r.wav)

## Progressive & Dynamic Degree (渐进程度与动态)

### [日益; rìyì; Day by day / Increasingly](ri4yi4.wav)
* **Usage:** Written, progressive adverb meaning "more with each passing day"; exclusively modifies dynamic gradable adjectives and trend-oriented verbs (`日益 + Adj/Verb`).
* **Example:** [跨国制造企业必须面对一场日益激烈的全球人才竞争。; Kuàguó zhìzào qǐyè bìxū miànduì yì chǎng rìyì jīliè de quánqiú réncái jìngzhēng.; Multinational manufacturing enterprises must confront an increasingly fierce global talent competition.](kua4guo2_zhi4zao4_qi3ye4_bi4xu1_mian4dui4_yi4_chang3_ri4yi4_ji1lie4_de0_quan2qiu2_ren2cai2_jing4zheng1.wav)

### [越; yuè; The more... / Increasingly](yue4.wav)
* **Usage:** Correlative progressive marker indicating scalar increase proportionally tied to time or conditions (`越 A 越 B`, or temporally anchored `越来越 + Adj`).

## Approximation & Thresholds (概数估测与逼近)

### [几乎; jīhū; Almost / Nearly](ji1hu1.wav)
* **Usage:** Denotes close approximation to a critical threshold, total state, or absolute limit without reaching it (`几乎 + Verb/Adj/Quantifier`); focuses on proximity to a whole.
* **Example:** [昨晚席卷沿海的那场强台风几乎摧毁了整座果园。; Zuówǎn xíjuǎn yánhǎi de nà chǎng qiáng táifēng jīhū cuīhuǐle zhěng zuò guǒyuán.; That severe typhoon that swept the coast last night almost destroyed the entire orchard.](zuo2wan3_xi2juan3_yan2hai3_de0_na4_chang3_qiang2_tai2feng1_ji1hu1_cui1hui3le0_zheng3_zuo4_guo3yuan2.wav)

### [差不多; chàbuduō; Almost / About the same](cha4bu0duo1.wav)
* **Usage:** Flexible approximation marker; operates as a pre-predicate adverb ("nearly"), a predicative adjective ("comparable / almost alike"), or modifies numerical amounts.
* **Example:** [翻译团队历时三年，差不多完成了那部哲学巨著的中文译稿。; Fānyì tuánduì lìshí sān nián, chàbuduō wánchéngle nà bù zhéxué jùzhù de zhōngwén yìgǎo.; Spending three years, the translation team has almost completed the Chinese draft of that monumental philosophical work.](fan1yi4_tuan2dui4_li4shi2_san1_nian2_cha4bu0duo1_wan2cheng2le0_na4_bu4_zhe2xue2_ju4zhu4_de0_zhong1wen2_yi4gao3.wav)

* **Usage:** Objective approximation adverb used explicitly with quantities, numerical estimates, or factual intervals (`大约 + Numeral-Classifier Phrase`).
* **Example:** [那艘满载矿石的货轮大约需要航行四十五天才能抵达目的港。; Nà sōu mǎnzǎi kuàngshí de huòlún dàyuē xūyào hángxíng sìshíwǔ tiān cái néng dǐdá mùdìgǎng. ; That freighter fully loaded with ore needs to sail for approximately forty-five days to arrive at the destination port.](na4_sou1_man3zai4_kuang4shi2_de0_huo4lun2_da4yue1_xu1yao4_hang2xing2_si4shi2wu3_tian1_cai2_neng2_di3da2_mu4di4gang3.wav)

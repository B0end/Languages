# Manner & State

Manner and state adverbs delineate how an action is executed, characterizing personal agency, deliberate intent, interpersonal reciprocity, temporal succession, or stealth. They occupy the pre-verbal adverbial position (`Adverb + Verb`), qualifying the dynamics of the predicate without altering its core argument structure.

## Intentionality & Purpose (主观意图与专向)

### [故意; gùyì; Deliberately / On purpose](gu4yi4.wav)
* **Usage:** Modifies actions executed with conscious premeditation, often carrying a negative pragmatic nuance of provocation, concealment, or calculated rule-breaking (`故意 + Verb`).
* **Example:** [证人在法庭上故意隐瞒了一条能够证明被告清白的关键线索。; Zhèngrén zài fǎtíng shàng gùyì yǐnmánle yì tiáo nénggòu zhèngmíng bèigào qīngbái de guānjiàn xiànsuǒ.; The witness deliberately concealed a critical clue in court that could prove the defendant's innocence.](zheng4ren2_zai4_fa3ting2_shang4_gu4yi4_yin3man2le0_yi4_tiao2_neng2gou4_zheng4ming2_bei4gao4_qing1bai2_de0_guan1jian4_xian4suo3.wav)

### [特意; tèyì; Specially / Purposefully](te4yi4.wav)
* **Usage:** Highlights dedicated effort, special thoughtfulness, or targeted accommodation made specifically for a positive or courteous purpose (`特意 + 为/给... + Verb`).

### [专门; zhuānmén; Exclusively / Specifically](zhuan1men2.wav)

### [轻易; qīngyì; Lightly / Rashly / Easily](qing1yi4.wav)
* **Usage:** Characterizes actions performed casually, without due reflection or resistance; overwhelmingly negated (`不轻易` / `切勿轻易`) to signal prudent restraint.

### [顺便; shùnbiàn; In passing / Conveniently](shun4bian4.wav)

## Personal Execution & Autonomy (亲历实施与独处)

### [亲自; qīnzì; In person / Personally](qin1zi4.wav)
* **Usage:** Emphasizes first-hand involvement, on-site presence, or personal leadership by a person of status, ruling out surrogates or subordinates (`亲自 + Verb`).
* **Example:** [总建筑师冒着严寒亲自视察了那一处位于悬崖边缘的施工工地。; Zǒng jiànzhùshī màozhe yánhán qīnzì shìchále nà yí chù wèiyú xuányá biānyuán de shīgōng gōngdì.; Braving the severe cold, the chief architect personally inspected that construction site perched on the edge of the cliff.](zong3_jian4zhu4shi1_mao4zhe0_yan2han2_qin1zi4_shi4cha2le0_na4_yi2_chu4_wei4yu2_xuan2ya2_bian1yuan2_de0_shi1gong1_gong1di4.wav)

### [单独; dāndú; Alone / By oneself / Independently](dan1du2.wav)

## Stealth & Covert Manner (隐秘情状与潜抑)

### [悄悄; qiāoqiāo; Quietly / Stealthily](qiao1qiao1.wav)
* **Usage:** Depicts physical actions carried out with minimal sound or overt motion to avoid detection or prevent disturbing others (`悄悄 + Motion/Physical Verb`).
* **Example:** [值班护士替病人掖好被角，然后悄悄关上了那扇沉重的病房木门。; Zhíbān hùshi tì bìngrén yē hǎo bèijiǎo, ránhòu qiāoqiāo guānshàngle nà shàn chénzhòng de bìngfáng mùmén.; The nurse on duty tucked the patient in, then quietly closed that heavy wooden ward door.](zhi2ban1_hu4shi0_ti4_bing4ren2_ye1_hao3_bei4jiao3_ran2hou4_qiao1qiao1_guan1shang4le0_na4_shan4_chen2zhong4_de0_bing4fang2_mu4men2.wav)

### [暗暗; àn'àn; Inwardly / Secretly to oneself](an4'an4.wav)

## Reciprocity & Collective Action (相互关系与协同)

### [互相; hùxiāng; Mutually / Each other](hu4xiang1.wav)
* **Usage:** Pre-verbal reciprocal adverb signaling two-way interactive feedback between plural subjects; directly precedes transitive action verbs (`互相 + Verb`); cannot be modified by degree adverbs.
* **Example:** [两家新兴科技企业在市场竞争中互相借鉴了一套高效的管理经验。; Liǎng jiā xīnxīng kējì qǐyè zài shìchǎng jìngzhēng zhōng hùxiāng jièjiànle yí tào gāoxiào de guǎnlǐ jīngyàn.; The two emerging technology companies mutually borrowed an efficient set of management experiences amidst market competition.](liang3_jia1_xin1xing1_ke1ji4_qi3ye4_zai4_shi4chang3_jing4zheng1_zhong1_hu4xiang1_jie4jian4le0_yi2_tao4_gao1xiao4_de0_guan3li3_jing1yan4.wav)

### [相互; xiānghù; Reciprocally / Mutually](xiang1hu4.wav)
* **Usage:** Formal reciprocal marker functioning flexibly as a pre-verbal adverb (`相互支持`), an attributive noun modifier (`相互关系`), or an abstract relational noun.
* **Example:** [两国边界部队依据那份防务条约相互通报了近期的巡逻动态。; Liǎng guó biānjiè bùduì yījù nà fèn fángwù tiáoyuē xiānghù tōngbàole jìnqī de xúnduó dòngtài.; The border forces of both nations mutually briefed each other on recent patrol developments in accordance with that defense treaty.](liang3_guo2_bian1jie4_bu4dui4_yi1ju4_na4_fen4_fang2wu4_tiao2yue1_xiang1hu4_tong1bao4le0_jin4qi1_de0_xun2luo2_dong4tai4.wav)

### [一起; yìqǐ; Together / In company](yi4qi3.wav)
* **Usage:** Standard connective adverb indicating joint spatial co-presence or simultaneous collaborative effort (`A 与/跟 B 一起 + Verb`).
* **Example:** [青年学者同几位资深勘探队员一起绘制了一幅精密的地层剖面图。; Qīngnián xuézhě tóng jǐ wèi zīshēn kāntàn duìyuán yìqǐ huìzhìle yì fú jīngmì de dìcéng pōumiàntú.; The young scholar, together with several veteran prospectors, plotted a precise geological cross-section diagram.](qing1nian2_xue2zhe3_tong2_ji3_wei4_zi1shen1_kan1tan4_dui4yuan2_yi4qi3_hui4zhi4le0_yi4_fu2_jing1mi4_de0_di4ceng2_pou1mian4tu2.wav)

* **Usage:** Formal and literary counterpart of `一起`; predominantly employed in ceremonial, diplomatic, or institutional accounts depicting joint participation.
* **Example:** [文化部长与外国使节一同出席了那一套珍稀非遗织锦的捐赠仪式。; Wénhuà bùzhǎng yǔ wàiguó shǐjié yìtóng chūxíle nà yí tào zhēnxī fēiyí zhījǐn de juānzèng yíshì.; The Minister of Culture, together with foreign envoys, attended the donation ceremony for that set of rare intangible heritage brocades.](wen2hua4_bu4zhang3_yu3_wai4guo2_shi3jie2_yi4tong2_chu1xi2le0_na4_yi2_tao4_zhen1xi1_fei1yi2_zhi1jin3_de0_juan1zeng4_yi2shi4.wav)

## Succession & Collective Flow (接续态势与群体动向)

### [纷纷; fēnfēn; One after another / In succession](fen1fen1.wav)
* **Usage:** Descriptive plural manner adverb depicting numerous diverse actors taking action in rapid, spontaneous succession or widespread wave-like concurrence (`Plural Subject + 纷纷 + Verb`).
* **Example:** [灾情发生后，沿海市民纷纷向慈善机构捐献了一批急需的御寒衣物。; Zāiqíng fāshēng hòu, yánhǎi shìmín fēnfēn xiàng císhàn jīgòu juānxiànle yì pī jíxū de yùhán yīwù.; After the disaster occurred, coastal citizens one after another donated a batch of urgently needed cold-weather clothing to charity organizations.](zai1qing2_fa1sheng1_hou4_yan2hai3_shi4min2_fen1fen1_xiang4_ci2shan4_ji1gou4_juan1xian4le0_yi4_pi1_ji2xu1_de0_yu4han2_yi1wu4.wav)

### [陆续; lùxù; Successively / One after another](lu4xu4.wav)
* **Usage:** Objective sequential adverb denoting an orderly, staged continuation where entities arrive or actions transpire intermittently across a timeline (`陆续 + Verb`).
* **Example:** [各国代表团在清晨陆续登上了那艘停泊在港湾内的科考母船。; Gè guó dàibiǎotuán zài qīngchén lùxù dēngshàngle nà sōu tíngbó zài gǎngwān nèi de kěkǎo mǔchuán.; The delegations of various countries stepped one after another in succession onto that scientific research mothership moored in the harbor at dawn.](ge4_guo2_dai4biao3tuan2_zai4_qing1chen2_lu4xu4_deng1shang4le0_na4_sou1_ting2bo2_zai4_gang3wan1_nei4_de0_ke1kao3_mu3chuan2.wav)

## Persistence & Unchanged Manner (沿袭状态与依旧)

### [照样; zhàoyàng; Still / As usual / All the same](zhao4yang4.wav)

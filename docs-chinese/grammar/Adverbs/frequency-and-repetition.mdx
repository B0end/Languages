# Frequency & Repetition

Adverbs of frequency and repetition occupy the pre-verbal adverbial position to define occurrence intervals, habitual tendencies, and iterative actions. Selection hinges on temporal aspect (realized past vs. projected future), empirical regularity versus absolute invariance, and pragmatic intent.

## Habitual & General Frequency (日常频率与常态)

### [总是; zǒngshì; Always / Invariably](zong3shi4.wav)
* **Usage:** Placed before verbs or adjectives to denote an unchanging, persistent state or universal occurrence across all relevant scenarios (`总是 + Verb/Adj`).
* **Example:** [这趟航班总是因为恶劣的天气延误。; Zhè tàng hángbān zǒngshì yīnwèi èliè de tiānqì yánwù.; This flight is always delayed due to severe weather.](zhe4_tang4_hang2ban1_zong3shi4_yin1wei4_e4lie4_de0_tian1qi4_yan2wu4.wav)

---

### [经常; jīngcháng; Often / Frequently](jing1chang2.wav)
* **Usage:** Modifies actions with high recurring frequency; can be directly negated by `不` to indicate infrequency (`不经常`), and can function as an attributive adjective modifying nouns.
* **Example:** [这位律师经常处理一宗复杂的商业诉讼。; Zhè wèi lǜshī jīngcháng chǔlǐ yì zōng fùzá de shāngyè sùsòng.; This lawyer frequently handles complex commercial lawsuits.](zhe4_wei4_lu:4shi1_jing1chang2_chu3li3_yi4_zong1_fu4za2_de0_shang1ye4_su4song4.wav)

---

### [常常; chángcháng; Frequently / Often](chang2chang2.wav)
* **Usage:** Pre-verbal frequency adverb indicating habitual recurrence; cannot be modified by `不` (negated via `不常`), cannot modify nouns, and focuses on routine actions.
* **Example:** [建筑师常常加班修改一张关键的设计图纸。; Jiànzhùshī chángcháng jiābān xiūgǎi yì zhāng guānjiàn de shèjì túzhǐ.; Architects often work overtime to revise a crucial design drawing.](jian4zhu4shi1_chang2chang2_jia1ban1_xiu1gai3_yi4_zhang1_guan1jian4_de0_she4ji4_tu2zhi3.wav)

---

### [往往; wǎngwǎng; Often / As a rule](wang3wang3.wav)
* **Usage:** Highlights an empirical regularity, predictable outcome, or natural tendency under specific conditions; requires a prerequisite context or conditional clause.
* **Example:** [缺乏沟通的团队往往会引发一场严重的信任危机。; Quēfá gōutōng de tuánduì wǎngwǎng huì yǐnfā yì chǎng yánzhòng de xìnrèn wēijī.; Teams lacking communication often trigger a severe crisis of trust.](que1fa2_gou1tong1_de0_tuan2dui4_wang3wang3_hui4_yin3fa1_yi4_chang3_yan2zhong4_de0_xin4ren4_wei1ji1.wav)

---

### [通常; tōngcháng; Usually / Normally](tong1chang2.wav)
* **Usage:** Marks general conventions, standard operating procedures, or baseline conditions; can occupy sentence-initial position or function as a noun-modifier.
* **Example:** [大使馆通常需要两周时间审批一份签证申请。; Dàshǐguǎn tōngcháng xūyào liǎng zhōu shíjiān shěnpī yí fèn qiānzhèng shēnqǐng.; The embassy usually takes two weeks to process a visa application.](da4shi3guan3_tong1chang2_xu1yao4_liang3_zhou1_shi2jian1_shen3pi1_yi2_fen4_qian1zheng4_shen1qing3.wav)

---

### [偶尔; ǒu'ěr; Occasionally / Once in a while](ou3'er3.wav)
* **Usage:** Signals low, non-systematic occurrence frequency; acts as the direct antonym of `经常` (`偶尔 + Verb`).
* **Example:** [这位外科医生偶尔会弹奏一架古老的钢琴来缓解压力。; Zhè wèi wàikē yīshēng ǒu'ěr huì tánzòu yí jià gǔlǎo de gāngqín lái huǎnjiě yālì.; The surgeon occasionally plays an antique piano to relieve stress.](zhe4_wei4_wai4ke1_yi1sheng1_ou3'er3_hui4_tan2zou4_yi2_jia4_gu3lao3_de0_gang1qin2_lai2_huan3jie3_ya1li4.wav)

---

### [从来; cónglái; Always / Never (with negative)](cong2lai2.wav)
* **Usage:** Denotes absolute continuity from past to present; predominantly coupled with negative particles `不` (habitual refusal: `从来不`) or `没` (lack of experiential occurrence: `从来没`).
* **Example:** [这家谨慎的企业从来不参与任何一项高风险的投机交易。; Zhè jiā jǐnshèn de qǐyè cónglái bù cānyù rènhé yí xiàng gāo-fēngxiǎn de tóujī jiāoyì.; This prudent enterprise never engages in any high-risk speculative transactions.](zhe4_jia1_jin3shen4_de0_qi3ye4_cong2lai2_bu4_can1yu4_ren4he2_yi2_xiang4_gao1_feng1xian3_de0_tou2ji1_jiao1yi4.wav)

## Linear Continuance & State Invariance (持续频率与始终)

### [一直; yìzhí; Continuously / All along](yi4zhi2.wav)
* **Usage:** Specifies an unbroken temporal span or sustained action without interruption (`一直 + Verb/Predicate`).
* **Example:** [监督员一直密切监测那座钢铁厂的污水排放指标。; Jiāndūyuán yìzhí mìqiè jiāncè nà zuò gāngtiěchǎng de wūshuǐ páifàng zhǐbiāo.; Inspectors have consistently and closely monitored the wastewater emission metrics of that steel plant.](jian1du1yuan2_yi4zhi2_mi4qie4_jian1ce4_na4_zuo4_gang1tie3chang3_de0_wu1shui3_pai2fang4_zhi3biao1.wav)

---

### [始终; shǐzhōng; From beginning to end / Throughout](shi3zhong1.wav)
* **Usage:** Formal adverb indicating persistent adherence to an orientation, principle, or factual truth across an entire duration despite external shifts.
* **Example:** [那个科研团队在实验中始终保持着一种严谨的治学态度。; Nàge kēyán tuánduì zài shíyàn zhōng shǐzhōng bǎochízhe yì zhǒng yánjǐn de zhìxué tàidu.; The research team maintained a rigorous scholarly attitude throughout their experiments.](na4ge0_ke1yan2_tuan2dui4_zai4_shi2yan4_zhong1_shi3zhong1_bao3chi2zhe0_yi4_zhong3_yan2jin3_de0_zhi4xue2_tai4du0.wav)

## Event Recurrence & Successive Actions (事件复发与再现)

### [又; yòu; Again (realized) / Both... and...](you4.wav)
* **Usage:** Marks the recurrence of an event that has *already taken place* (past/factual), or cyclical actions occurring by regular schedule.
* **Example:** [这个苹果又大又甜。; Zhè ge píngguǒ yòu dà yòu tián.;This apple is both big and sweet](zhe4ge0ping2guo3you4da4you4tian2.wav)

---

### [再; zài; Again (future) / Once more](zai4.wav)
* **Usage:** Directs repetition toward *future, unfulfilled, or prospective* events; commonly marks sequential completion (`先...再...`).
* **Example:** [委员会建议他先补充一份财务证明，然后再递交申请表。; Wěiyuánhuì jiànyì tā xiān bǔchōng yí fèn cáiwù zhèngmíng, ránhòu zài dìjiāo shēnqǐngbiǎo.; The committee suggested that he supplement a financial certificate first before submitting the application form.](wei3yuan2hui4_jian4yi4_ta1_xian1_bu3chong1_yi2_fen4_cai2wu4_zheng4ming2_ran2hou4_zai4_di4jiao1_shen1qing3biao3.wav)

---

### [重新; chóngxīn; Anew / Again from the start](chong2xin1.wav)
* **Usage:** Denotes initiating an operation from the beginning, typically to revise, correct, or adjust previous unsatisfactory outcomes (`重新 + Verb`).
* **Example:** [董事会要求专家组重新评估那一套投资方案的可行性。; Dǒngshìhuì yāoqiú zhuānjiāzǔ chóngxīn pínggū nà yí tào tóuzī fāng'àn de kěxíngxìng.; The board of directors requested the expert group to re-evaluate the feasibility of that investment scheme.](dong3shi4hui4_yao1qiu2_zhuan1jia1zu3_chong2xin1_ping2gu1_na4_yi2_tao4_tou2zi1_fang1'an4_de0_ke3xing2xing4.wav)

## Repeated Iteration & Insistence (反复施加与多次强调)

### [反复; fǎnfù; Repeatedly / Over and over](fan3fu4.wav)
* **Usage:** Highlights cyclical iterations, back-and-forth behavioral processes, or exhaustive deliberation before reaching a conclusion (`反复 + Verb`).
* **Example:** [发言人反复推敲了一篇外交演讲稿中的用词细节。; Fāyánrén fǎnfù tuīqiāo le yì piān wàijiāo yǎnjiǎnggǎo zhōng de yòngcí xìjié.; The spokesperson repeatedly scrutinized the wording details in a diplomatic speech draft.](fa1yan2ren2_fan3fu4_tui1qiao1_le0_yi4_pian1_wai4jiao1_yan3jiang3gao3_zhong1_de0_yong4ci2_xi4jie2.wav)

---

### [再三; zàisān; Over and over / Repeatedly](zai4san1.wav)
* **Usage:** Emphasizes high-stakes deliberate intent, urging, or consideration; restricted almost exclusively to communicative, cognitive, or intentional verbs (`再三考虑`, `再三叮嘱`).
* **Example:** [经理再三叮嘱出纳员务必妥善保管那一枚公章。; Jīnglǐ zàisān dīngzhǔ chūnàyuán wùbì tuǒshàn bǎoguǎn nà yì méi gōngzhāng.; The manager repeatedly instructed the cashier to properly safe-keep that official seal.](jing1li3_zai4san1_ding1zhu3_chu1na4yuan2_wu4bi4_tuo3shan4_bao3guan3_na4_yi4_mei2_gong1zhang1.wav)

---

### [一再; yízài; Repeatedly / Time and again](yi2zai4.wav)
* **Usage:** Stresses ongoing reiteration of an action, particularly in contexts where advice is unheeded or where an undesirable situation is repeatedly delayed or ignored.
* **Example:** [组委会因为突发情况一再推迟了那一届博览会的开幕日期。; Zǔwěihuì yīnwèi tūfā qíngkuàng yízài tuīchí le nà yí jiè bólǎnhuì de kāimù rìqī.; Due to unexpected circumstances, the organizing committee repeatedly postponed the opening date of that expo.](zu3wei3hui4_yin1wei4_tu1fa1_qing2kuang4_yi2zai4_tui1chi2_le0_na4_yi2_jie4_bo2lan3hui4_de0_kai1mu4_ri4qi1.wav)

### [屡次; lǚcì; Time and again / Repeatedly](lu:3ci4.wav)
* **Usage:** Formal adverb indicating repeated occurrences of an event, commonly applied to infractions, regulatory violations, or uncorrected errors.
* **Example:** [那个司机屡次违反交通信号灯，最终被吊销了一本驾驶证。; Nàge sījī lǚcì wéifǎn jiāotōng xìnhàodēng, zuìzhōng bèi diàoxiāo le yì běn jiàshǐzhèng.; That driver repeatedly violated traffic lights and ultimately had his driver's license revoked.](na4ge0_si1ji1_lu:3ci4_wei2fan3_jiao1tong1_xin4hao4deng1_zui4zhong1_bei4_diao4xiao1_le0_yi4_ben3_jia4shi3zheng4.wav)

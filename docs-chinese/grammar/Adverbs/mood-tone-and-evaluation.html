Mood, Tone & Evaluation

Modal, tone, and evaluation adverbs establish the speaker's epistemic commitment, subjective stance, emotional evaluation, or rhetorical orientation toward a proposition. Syntactically, they function either sententially (preceding the subject to frame the entire utterance) or pre-verbally (modifying the predicate to modulate certainty, contrast, or necessity).

Certainty, Affirmation & Factuality (确定、肯定与实情)​

确实; quèshí; Indeed / Truly / Reliably​

  • Usage: Confirms the indisputable truth, authenticity, or factual basis of a situation (确实 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 这部纪录片确实揭露了当年那场金融危机背后的制度漏洞。; Zhè bù jìlùpiàn quèshí jiēlùle dāngnián nà chǎng jīnróng wēijī bèihòu de zhìdù lòudòng.; This documentary film indeed exposed the institutional loopholes behind that financial crisis of that year.

其实; qíshí; Actually / In fact​

  • Usage: Clarifies underlying objective reality, correcting a false impression, surface assumption, or misconception (其实 + Clause/Predicate).
  • Example: 双方代表其实早已就那份补充协议的核心条款达成了共识。; Shuāngfāng dàibiǎo qíshí zǎoyǐ jiù nà fèn bǔchōng xiéyì de héxīn tiáokuǎn dáchéngle gòngshí.; Both delegations had actually reached a consensus on the core terms of that supplemental agreement long ago.

显然; xiǎnrán; Obviously / Evidently​

  • Usage: Marks an inference or conclusion as self-evident and readily discernible from observable facts (显然 + Predicate/Clause).
  • Example: 这份审计报告显然证明管理层故意隐瞒了上个季度的巨额亏损。; Zhè fèn shěnjì bàogào xiǎnrán zhèngmíng guǎnlǐcéng gùyì yǐnmánle shàng gè jìdù de jù'é kuīsǔn.; This audit report evidently proves that management intentionally concealed the massive losses of the previous quarter.

自然; zìrán; Naturally / As a matter of course​

  • Usage: Expresses that an outcome follows logically, organically, and inevitably from precedent conditions (自然 + Verb/Predicate).
  • Example: 研发团队成功斩获这项国际专利后，自然会吸引来众多风投资金。; Yánfā tuánduì chénggōng zhǎnhuò zhè xiàng guójì zhuānlì hòu, zìrán huì xīyǐn lái zhòngduō fēngtóu zījīn.; After the R&D team successfully clinched this international patent, it naturally attracted substantial venture capital funds.

当然; dāngrán; Of course / Certainly​

  • Usage: Emphasizes that an assertion is beyond dispute or logically unquestionable; can also concede a secondary point (当然，...但是...).
  • Example: 作为一位资深的辩护律师，他当然要全力维护当事人的合法权益。; Zuòwéi yí wèi zīshēn de biànhù lǜshī, tā dāngrán yào quánlì wéihù dāngshìrén de héfǎ quányì.; As a veteran defense attorney, he of course must defend the lawful rights of his client with all his might.

果然; guǒrán; Sure enough / As expected​

  • Usage: Confirms that a preceding prediction, forecast, or apprehension has been fulfilled by reality (果然 + Verb/Predicate).
  • Example: 气象台预报的那场特大暴风雪，今天清晨果然导致了全城交通瘫痪。; Qìxiàngtái yùbào de nà chǎng tèdà bàofēngxuě, jīntiān qīngchén guǒrán dǎozhìle quánchéng jiāotōng tānhuàn.; That severe blizzard forecast by the meteorological station sure enough caused city-wide traffic paralysis this morning.

本来; běnlái; Originally / By nature​

  • Usage: Contrasts a past baseline, initial intention, or intrinsic property with an altered reality (本来 + Predicate/Clause).
  • Example: 旅客们本来预订了这趟直飞航班，却因机械故障不得不更改行程。; Lǚkèmén běnlái yùdìngle zhè tàng zhífēi hángbān, què yīn jīxiè gùzhàng bùdébù gēnggǎi xíngchéng.; The passengers had originally booked this direct flight, but were compelled to change itineraries due to a mechanical malfunction.

Epistemic Modality, Probability & Evidentiality (推测、或然与察知)​

也许; yěxǔ; Perhaps / Probably​

  • Usage: Expresses tentative conjecture, low-to-moderate probability, or polite non-committal speculation (也许 + Verb/Clause).
  • Example: 这一剂改良的中药方剂也许能够彻底缓解困扰他多年的慢性胃炎。; Zhè jì gǎiliáng de zhōngyào fāngjì yěxǔ nénggòu chèdǐ huǎnjiě kùnrǎo tā duō nián de mànxìng wèiyán.; This modified dose of herbal medicine perhaps can completely alleviate the chronic gastritis that has plagued him for years.

大概; dàgài; Probably / Likely​

  • Usage: Denotes a high probability based on general estimates, empirical likelihood, or approximate judgment (大概 + Predicate).
  • Example: 考古学家推断这批出土的青铜器皿大概制作于西周早期。; Kǎogǔxuéjiā tuīduàn zhè pī chūtǔ de qīngtóng qìmǐn dàgài zhìzuò yú Xīzhōu zǎoqī.; Archaeologists infer that this batch of unearthed bronze vessels was likely fabricated during the early Western Zhou period.

看来; kànlai; Apparently / It seems​

  • Usage: Sentential evidential adverb signaling an inductive deduction derived from circumstantial evidence or visible patterns (看来 + Clause).
  • Example: 防汛指挥部看来需要重新启动那套针对突发山洪的紧急疏散预案。; Fángxùn zhǐhuībù kànlai xūyào chóngxīn qǐdòng nà tào zhēnduì tūfā shānhóng de jǐnjí shūsàn yù'àn.; The flood prevention headquarters apparently needs to reboot that emergency evacuation protocol targeting flash floods.

不见得; bújiànde; Not necessarily / Unlikely​

  • Usage: Softens a categorical assertion or expresses polite skepticism regarding an alleged certainty (不见得 + Adj/Verb).
  • Example: 驾驶一辆价值不菲的豪华跑车不见得就能带来真正的内心充实。; Jiàshǐ yí liàng jiàzhí bùfěi de háohuá pǎochē bújiànde jiù néng dàilái zhēnzhèng de nèixīn chōngshí.; Driving a high-value luxury sports car does not necessarily bring genuine internal fulfillment.

难怪; nánguài; No wonder / Understandably​

  • Usage: Indicates sudden realization of why an anomaly occurred once the underlying cause or condition is revealed (难怪 + Clause).
  • Example: 他每天在泳池里高强度训练十个小时，难怪能摘得这枚奥运金牌。; Tā měitiān zài yǒngchí lǐ gāo-qiángdù xùnliàn shí gè xiǎoshí, nánguài néng zhāidé zhè méi Àoyùn jīnpái.; He trains with high intensity in the pool ten hours daily; no wonder he was able to capture this Olympic gold medal.

Deontic Modality, Obligation & Admonition (必然、叮嘱与诫勉)​

必须; bìxū; Must / Compulsorily​

  • Usage: Deontic modal adverb signaling mandatory operational requirement, legal compulsion, or absolute necessity (必须 + Verb).
  • Example: 所有新入职的核心工程师都必须签署一份严格的技术保密协议。; Suǒyǒu xīn rùzhí de héxīn gōngchéngshī dōu bìxū qiānshǔ yí fèn yángé de jìshù bǎomì xiéyì.; All newly recruited core engineers must sign a strict technical confidentiality agreement.

务必; wùbì; Be sure to / By all means​

  • Usage: Formal imperative adverb indicating urgent admonition, strict managerial decree, or non-negotiable instruction (务必 + Verb).
  • Example: 离开办公室前请务必把那叠机密图纸锁入保险柜中。; Líkāi bàngōngshì qián qǐng wùbì bǎ nà dié jīmì túzhǐ suǒrù bǎoxiǎnguì zhōng.; Before departing the office, please be sure to lock that stack of classified blueprints into the safe.

千万; qiānwàn; Above all / By all means​

  • Usage: Emphatic cautionary adverb used to plead, warn, or prohibit, heavily concentrated in negative commands (千万别... / 千万不可...).
  • Example: 施工人员在挖掘作业时千万不可触碰地下那根高压电缆。; Shīgōng rényuán zài wājué zuòyè shí qiānwàn bùkě chùpèng dìxià nà gēn gāoyā diànlǎn.; Construction personnel must under no circumstances disturb that high-voltage underground cable during excavation work.

一定; yídìng; Certainly / Firmly​

  • Usage: Expresses strong personal resolve ("definitely will"), firm epistemic certainty ("bound to"), or assertive instruction (一定 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 建设兵团下定决心在年底前一定要让那座大型水电站顺利并网发电。; Jiànshè bīngtuán xiàdìng juéxīn zài niándǐ qián yídìng yào ràng nà zuò dàxíng shuǐdiànzhàn shùnlì bìngwǎng fādiàn.; The construction corps made up its mind that it must connect that large hydroelectric station to the power grid by year-end.

不妨; bùfáng; Might as well / There is no harm in​

  • Usage: Diplomatic, constructive suggestion marker proposing an alternative path of action without imposing pressure (不妨 + Verb).
  • Example: 如果您经常需要在嘈杂环境中办公，不妨试戴一下这款无线降噪耳机。; Rúguǒ nín jīngcháng xūyào zài cáozá huánjìng zhōng bàngōng, bùfáng shìdài yíxià zhè kuǎn wúxiàn jiàngzào ěrjī.; If you frequently need to work in noisy environments, there is no harm in trying on this wireless noise-cancelling headset.

Rhetorical Inquest & Probing (反诘、追究与探询)​

究竟; jiūjìng; Exactly / In the final analysis​

  • Usage: Deepens an interrogation, probing for ultimate bottom-line truth in questions (究竟 + Wh-word/V-not-V); formal equivalent of 到底.
  • Example: 历史学家们试图弄清楚这桩尘封半个世纪的悬案究竟隐藏了什么内幕。; Lìshǐxuéjiāmén shìtú nòng qīngchu zhè zhuāng chénfēng bàn gè shìjì de xuán'àn jiūjìng yǐncángle shénme nèimù.; Historians are striving to clarify what inside secrets this unsettled case shelved for half a century actually hides.

到底; dàodǐ; On earth / Down to the root​

  • Usage: Presses for an explicit, definitive resolution in interrogatives, or marks an ultimate realization after prolonged delays.
  • Example: 工程师花了整整三天排查，也没查出这台激光雕刻机到底出了什么故障。; Gōngchéngshī huāle zhěngzhěng sān tiān páichá, yě méi cháchū zhè tái jīguāng diāokèjī dàodǐ chūle shénme gùzhàng.; The engineer spent three full days troubleshooting and still could not figure out what on earth went wrong with this laser engraver.

难道; nándào; Could it be that...? / Don't tell me...​

  • Usage: Signals a rhetorical question that challenges an absurd proposition, expecting a vigorous negative assent (难道...吗/不成?).
  • Example: 难道企业花费重金打造的这道网络防火墙连一个初级黑客都防不住吗？; Nándào qǐyè huāfèi zhòngjīn dǎzào de zhè dào wǎngluò fánghuǒqiáng lián yí gè chūjí hēikè dōu fáng bú zhù ma?; Could it be that this network firewall the enterprise spent a fortune building cannot even defend against an entry-level hacker?

何必; hébì; Why should...? / There is no need​

  • Usage: Rhetorical adverb negating necessity or criticizing unwarranted exertion/conflict (何必 + Verb/Clause (+ 呢)?).
  • Example: 邻里之间何必为了争夺一处临街摊位而闹得不可开交呢？; Línlǐ zhījiān hébì wèile zhēngduó yí chù línjiē tānwèi ér nào de bùkě kāijiāo ne?; Why should neighbors create such an irreconcilable scene merely to contest a street-facing vendor stall?

Counter-Expectation, Contrast & Concession (反预期、转折与让步)​

倒; dào; Contrarily / Actually / On the contrary​

  • Usage: Signals unexpected reversal against expectations, mild concession with an underlying contrast, or softened conversational critique (倒 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 这碗中草药熬制的热汤闻着有些刺鼻，尝起来味道倒挺鲜美的。; Zhè wǎn zhōngcǎoyào áozhì de rètāng wénzhe yǒuxiē cìbí, cháng qǐlái wèidào dào tǐng xiānměi de.; This hot broth brewed with herbal medicine smells somewhat pungent, but tasting it, the flavor is actually quite savory.

反而; fǎn'ér; On the contrary / Instead​

  • Usage: Connective adverb showing that the ensuing outcome developed in exact opposition to logical anticipation (Condition, 反而 + Result).
  • Example: 央行宣布降息后股市没有上涨，反而引发了一轮恐慌性的抛售潮。; Yāngháng xuānbù jiàngxī hòu gǔshì méiyǒu shàngzhǎng, fǎn'ér yǐnfāle yì lún kǒnghuāngxìng de pāoshòucháo.; After the central bank announced an interest rate cut, the stock market did not rise, but instead triggered a round of panic selling.

偏偏; piānpiān; Perversely / Contrary to wishes​

  • Usage: Highlights frustrating counter-coincidence ("as luck would have it") or willful contrariness against expectations or advice (偏偏 + Verb).
  • Example: 戏剧即将开演，他偏偏把那张千辛万苦抢到的VIP门票落在了出租车上。; Xìjù jíjiāng kāiyǎn, tā piānpiān bǎ nà zhāng qiānxīnwànkǔ qiǎngdào de VIP ménpiào là zài le chūzūchē shàng.; Just as the drama was about to start, contrary to all wishes he left that VIP ticket he scrambled to acquire in the taxi.

毕竟; bìjìng; After all / In the final analysis​

  • Usage: Appeals to a fundamental truth, foundational constraint, or overriding reality to explain, rationalize, or contextualize an imperfect state (毕竟 + Clause).
  • Example: 请不要对这位初出茅庐的陶瓷手艺人过于苛求，他毕竟只学了三年陶艺。; Qǐng búyào duì zhè wèi chūchūmáolú de táocí shǒuyìrén guòyú kēqiú, tā bìjìng zhǐ xuéle sān nián táoyì.; Please do not be overly demanding of this novice ceramic artisan; after all, he has only studied ceramics for three years.

反正; fǎnzhèng; In any case / Regardless​

  • Usage: Expresses unconditional resolve or irrelevance of antecedent variables ("no matter what happens, the outcome remains fixed") (反正 + Clause).
  • Example: 反正今晚已经错过了最后一趟城际动车，我们索性在附近找家客栈住下吧。; Fǎnzhèng jīnwǎn yǐjīng cuòguòle zuìhòu yí tàng chéngjì dòngchē, wǒmen suǒxìng zài fùjìn zhǎo jiā kèzhàn zhùxià ba.; In any case we have already missed the final intercity bullet train tonight; we might as well find an inn nearby to stay.

Coincidence, Timing & Fortuity (机缘、巧合与庆幸)​

刚好; gānghǎo; Just / By coincidence​

  • Usage: Denotes exact temporal, spatial, or numerical alignment without discrepancy; emphasizes opportune coincidence (刚好 + Verb/Numeral).
  • Example: 贵宾走进餐厅时，大堂靠窗的位置刚好腾出了一张精致的八仙桌。; Guìbīn zǒujìn cāntīng shí, dàtáng kàochuāng de wèizhì gānghǎo téngchūle yì zhāng jīngzhì de bāxiānzhuō.; When the VIP walked into the restaurant, a refined square dining table had just opened up by the window in the lobby.

正好; zhènghǎo; Coincidentally / Just right​

  • Usage: Interchangeable with 刚好, signifying precise confluence of conditions, timing, or dimensions (正好 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 门卫递过来的那把折叠雨伞，正好挡住了迎面泼来的冰冷雨水。; Ménwèi dì guòlái de nà bǎ zhédié yǔsǎn, zhènghǎo dǎngzhùle yíngmiàn pō lái de bīnglěng yǔshuǐ.; That folding umbrella handed over by the doorman just happened to block the freezing rain splashing from ahead.

幸亏; xìngkuī; Fortunately / Luckily​

  • Usage: Highlights that a fortunate intervention averted an imminent calamity or adverse development (幸亏...，才/不然...).
  • Example: 幸亏那名地质勘探员戴了一顶坚固的安全帽，才没被滚落的碎石击伤头部。; Xìngkuī nà míng dìzhì kāntànyuán dàile yì dǐng jiāngù de ānquánmào, cái méi bèi gǔnluò de suìshí jīshāng tóubù.; Fortunately that geological prospector was wearing a sturdy hard hat, preventing him from suffering a head injury from rolling gravel.

多亏; duōkuī; Thanks to / Luckily​

  • Usage: Attentive emotional gratitude attributing deliverance from misfortune to a specific agent, entity, or action (多亏了 + Noun/Clause).
  • Example: 多亏那艘破浪赶来的海警搜救艇，遇险的渔民才能脱离那片风暴漩涡。; Duōkuī nà sōu pòlàng gǎnlái de hǎijǐng sōujiùtǐng, yùxiǎn de yúmín cái néng tuōlí nà piàn fēngbào xuánwō.; Thanks to that coast guard rescue boat arriving through the waves, the imperiled fishermen were able to escape that storm vortex.

Resolution, Relief & Culmination (终了、释然与决意)​

总算; zǒngsuàn; At long last / Finally​

  • Usage: Conveys profound subjective relief that a desired objective has materialized after long delays, arduous setbacks, or intense anticipation (总算 + Verb).
  • Example: 历经十四个小时的艰苦跋涉，搜救队员总算在深夜抵达了那座被困的山顶营地。; Lìjīng shísì gè xiǎoshí de jiānkǔ báshè, sōujiù duìyuán zǒngsuàn zài shēnyè dǐdále nà zuò bèikùn de shāndǐng yíngdì.; After undergoing fourteen hours of grueling trekking, the search and rescue team at long last reached that stranded mountaintop camp late at night.

索性; suǒxìng; Might as well / Simply​

  • Usage: Marks a decisive, radical choice to adopt a direct, straightforward approach, abandoning tedious interim compromises (索性 + Verb).
  • Example: 既然那台机械打字机彻底坏了，作家索性换用了一台全新的轻薄笔记本电脑。; Jìrán nà tái jīxiè dǎzìjī chèdǐ huàile, zuòjiā suǒxìng huànyòngle yì tái quánxīn de qīngbáo bǐjìběn diànnǎo.; Since that mechanical typewriter broke completely, the author simply switched to a brand-new ultrathin laptop.

只好; zhǐhǎo; Have no choice but to​

  • Usage: Indicates that a course of action is chosen reluctantly as the sole remaining, unideal alternative due to external constraints (只好 + Verb).
  • Example: 由于前方铁路线遭遇严重积雪，站长只好让这列国际货运班列暂时滞留在站台。; Yóuyú qiánfāng tiělùxiàn zāoyù yánzhòng jīxuě, zhànzhǎng zhǐhǎo ràng zhè liè guójì huòyùn bānliè zànshí zhìliú zài zhàntái.; Because the rail line ahead suffered severe snow accumulation, the stationmaster had no choice but to let this international cargo freight train remain stranded at the platform.

不得已; bùdéyǐ; Forced to / Against one's will​

  • Usage: Formal expression meaning "having no alternative"; functions as an adjective, predicative complement, or inside the formula 在不得已的情况下 ("under forced circumstances").
  • Example: 董事会在不得已的情况下关闭了那间持续亏损三年的海外旗舰店。; Dǒngshìhuì zài bùdéyǐ de qíngkuàng xià guānbìle nà jiān chíxù kuīsǔn sān nián de hǎiwài qíjiàndiàn.; Under circumstances of having no other choice, the board of directors closed that overseas flagship store that had run losses for three consecutive years.

宁可; nìngkě; Would rather / Prefer to​

  • Usage: Signals an intentional trade-off where the speaker consciously accepts an agonizing cost to avert an intolerable dishonor or worse disaster (宁可 A，也不/也要 B).
  • Example: 这位严谨的学者宁可放弃那笔丰厚的商业赞助，也绝不篡改任何一条实验数据。; Zhè wèi yánjǐn de xuézhě nìngkě fàngqì nà bǐ fēnghòu de shāngyè zànzhù, yě jué bù cuàngǎi rènhé yì tiáo shíyàn shùjù.; This rigorous scholar would rather forfeit that generous commercial sponsorship than tamper with a single piece of experimental data.

Inevitability & Critical Evaluation (难免、评骘与委婉)​

难免; nánmiǎn; Hard to avoid / Inevitable​

  • Usage: Denotes an unavoidable objective tendency or natural consequence under specific empirical dynamics (难免 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 传统制造业在推进这一场深刻的数字化转型时，难免会遭遇局部的阵痛。; Chuántǒng zhìzàoyè zài tuījìn zhè yì chǎng shēnkè de shùzìhuà zhuǎnxíng shí, nánmiǎn huì zāoyù júbù de zhèntòng.; When traditional manufacturing advances this profound digital transformation, it is hard to avoid localized growing pains.

不免; bùmiǎn; Unavoidably / Cannot help but​

  • Usage: Descriptive tone adverb emphasizing spontaneous emotional reactions or psychological states that naturally erupt under situational catalysts (心中不免...).
  • Example: 游子在异国他乡捧读那一封发黄的旧家书，心中不免泛起阵阵乡愁。; Yóuzǐ zài yìguótāxiāng pěngdú nà yì fēng fāhuáng de jiù jiāshū, xīnzhōng bùmiǎn fànqǐ zhènzhèn xiāngchóu.; The wanderer holding and reading that yellowed old family letter in a foreign land could not help but feel waves of homesickness welling up.

未免; wèimiǎn; Rather / Truly a bit too​

  • Usage: Softens an evaluative criticism, expressing polite disagreement while implying that someone has crossed an acceptable threshold (未免 + 太/过于...).
  • Example: 校方因为学生迟到一次就给予记过处分，这项决定未免太苛刻了。; Xiàofāng yīnwèi xuésheng chídào yí cì jiù jǐyǔ jìguò chǔfèn, zhè xiàng juédìng wèimiǎn tài kēkè le.; The school giving a demerit sanction because a student was late once—this decision is truly a bit too harsh.

Emphatic Stance, Scope & Progression (极端强调、递进与语势)​

根本; gēnběn; At all / Fundamentally​

  • Usage: Expresses unconditional, radical negation from the very root (根本 + 不/没/无); rejects the validity of the premise entirely.
  • Example: 质监局证实那家小作坊出产的这一批劣质零件根本不符合国家工业安全标准。; Zhìjiānjú zhèngshí nà jiā xiǎozuòfang chūchǎn de zhè yì pī lièzhì língjiàn gēnběn bù fúhé guójiā gōngyè ānquán biāozhǔn.; The quality supervision bureau confirmed that this batch of substandard parts produced by that small workshop does not conform to national industrial safety standards at all.

简直; jiǎnzhí; Simply / Practically​

  • Usage: Intensifier used to introduce hyperbole, vivid characterizations, or startling descriptions as if they were literal truth (简直 + Verb/Predicate).
  • Example: 青年画家创作的这幅全景水墨画气势磅礴，技艺简直堪称鬼斧神工。; Qīngnián huàjiā chuàngzuò de zhè fú quánjǐng shuǐmòhuà qìshì pángbó, jìyì jiǎnzhí kānchēng guǐfǔshéngōng.; This panoramic ink wash painting created by the young painter is breathtakingly grand; the artistry can simply be hailed as superhuman perfection.

甚至; shènzhì; Even / So much that​

  • Usage: Marks an extreme, unexpected illustrative case that extends the baseline, highlighting radical progression (甚至 + Verb/Noun).
  • Example: 电信公司推进数字基建，甚至在那座悬崖上的偏远山村架设了一座5G基站。; Diànxìn gōngsī tuījìn shùzì jījiàn, shènzhì zài nà zuò xuányá shàng de piānyuǎn shāncūn jiàshèle yí zuò 5G jīzhàn.; The telecom company advanced digital infrastructure, even erecting a 5G base station in that remote mountain village perched upon a cliff.

何况; hékuàng; Much less / Let alone​

  • Usage: Introduces an a fortiori argument; points out a more obvious, compelling subsequent condition that reinforces the preceding statement (..., 何况...呢).
  • Example: 资深文史研究员翻译这本古籍尚且感到吃力，何况是缺乏基础的初学者呢？; Zīshēn wénshǐ yánjiūyuán fānyì zhè běn gǔjí shàngqiě gǎndào chīlì, hékuàng shì quēfá jīchǔ de chūxuézhě ne?; Even veteran literature and history researchers find translating this ancient tome arduous, let alone beginners lacking foundational training?

就; jiù; Directly / As early as / Right away​

  • Usage: Imparts rapid immediacy, swift realization, or assertive focal alignment between condition and outcome (Condition + 就 + Result).
  • Example: 天才少年看了一眼黑板上的那道奥数难题，当场就在草稿纸上写出了答案。; Tiāncái shàonián kànle yì yǎn hēibǎn shàng de nà dào àoshù nántí, dāngchǎng jiù zài cǎogǎozhǐ shàng xiěchūle dá'àn.; The prodigy glanced once at that mathematical olympiad problem on the blackboard, and wrote down the solution on his scratch paper right on the spot.

还; hái; Still / Even yet​

  • Usage: Confirms that a historic state or persistent functionality remains unbroken despite elapsed time, obstacles, or deterioration (还 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 那座历经数百年风雨侵蚀的石拱桥，如今还承担着两岸村民的日常通行需求。; Nà zuò lìjīng shù bǎi nián fēngyǔ qīnshí de shí gǒngqiáo, rújīn hái chéngdānzhe liǎng'àn cūnmín de rìcháng tōngxíng xūqiú.; That stone arch bridge that weathered centuries of wind and rain erosion still serves the everyday commuting needs of villagers on both banks today.

Scope & Restriction

Scope and restriction adverbs delimit the semantic domain of predicates, quantifying participant totality, imposing exclusive boundaries, or aggregating spatial and numerical breadths. Syntactically, they occupy the pre-verbal or pre-quantifier slot, frequently anchoring correlative constructions that summarize preceding subjects or conditional clauses.

Totality & Universal Scope (总括与全称范围)​

都; dōu; All / Both / Entirely​

  • Usage: Left-associative universal quantifier summarizing preceding plural subjects, fronted objects, or conditional scopes (Subject(Plural) + 都 + Predicate).
  • Example: 参与会诊的几位主治医师都赞同这套手术方案。; Cānyù huìzhěn de jǐ wèi zhǔzhì yīshī dōu zàntóng zhè tào shǒushù fāng'àn.; The several attending physicians participating in the consultation all endorsed this surgical plan.

统统; tǒngtǒng; All / Entirely / Completely​

  • Usage: Colloquial and emphatic totality adverb meaning "all without exception"; frequently summarizes multi-item direct objects in 把-disposition constructions or fronted topicalized lists.
  • Example: 档案管理员把这批过期的合同文件统统归入了一间地下储藏室。; Dàng'ǎn guǎnlǐyuán bǎ zhè pī guòqī de hétong wénjiàn tǒngtǒng guīrù le yì jiān dìxià chǔcángshì.; The archivist sorted all these expired contract documents without exception into an underground storage room.

全部; quánbù; Whole / Entire / Completely​

  • Usage: Dual-function word acting as an exhaustive noun-modifier or an adverbial marker denoting 100% completion or participation across an entire inventory.
  • Example: 工程队在上周末已经全部完成了那条跨江大桥的铺轨工作。; Gōngchéngduì zài shàng zhōumò yǐjīng quánbù wánchéngle nà tiáo kuàjiāng dàqiáo de pūguǐ gōngzuò.; The engineering team had completely finished the track-laying work of that cross-river bridge last weekend.

一律; yílǜ; Without exception / Universally​

  • Usage: Prescriptive scope adverb meaning "treated uniformly alike"; standard in legal codes, institutional regulations, policy enforcement, and administrative guidelines (一律 + Verb).
  • Example: 这家连锁酒店规定所有客房在午夜后一律停止供应酒精饮料。; Zhè jiā liánsuǒ jiǔdiàn guīdìng suǒyǒu kèfáng zài wǔyè hòu yílǜ tíngzhǐ gōngyìng jiǔjīng yǐnliào.; This hotel chain stipulates that all guest rooms without exception must cease alcoholic beverage service after midnight.

凡是; fánshì; Every / Any / All​

  • Usage: Universal distributive marker placed at the beginning of noun phrases or conditional clauses to define an open class ("whatever / every single"); obligatorily pairs with 都 or 一律 in the matrix clause (凡是...都/一律...).
  • Example: 凡是获得国家基金资助的科研项目都必须提交一份详细的结题报告。; Fánshì huòdé guójiā jījīn zīzhù de kēyán xiàngmù dōu bìxū tíjiāo yí fèn xiángxì de jiétí bàogào.; Every scientific research project funded by the national fund must submit a detailed completion report.

完全; wánquán; Completely / Entirely / Totally​

  • Usage: Marks absolute fulfillment, totality of scope, or unconditional degree; directly precedes predicates or negative particles (完全不 / 完全没) to denote absolute non-existence or categorical disagreement.
  • Example: 专家组确认那座在地震中受损的古桥结构已经完全恢复了稳定。; Zhuānjiāzǔ quèrèn nà zuò zài dìzhèn zhōng shòusǔn de gǔqiáo jiégòu yǐjīng wánquán huìfù le wěndìng.; The expert panel confirmed that the structure of that ancient bridge damaged in the earthquake has completely recovered its stability.

Summation & Aggregation (总计与累计范围)​

一共; yígòng; Altogether / In total​

  • Usage: Summation adverb placed immediately before numeral-classifier phrases to aggregate individuals, inventory, or temporal units into a combined total (一共 + Numeral-Classifier).
  • Example: 出版社为这套科普丛书一共设计了十二册独立的专题绘本。; Chūbǎnshè wèi zhè tào kēpǔ cóngshū yígòng shèjìle shí'èr cè dúlì de zhuāntí huìběn.; The publishing house designed a total of twelve independent thematic picture books for this popular science series.

总共; zǒnggòng; In total / Altogether​

  • Usage: Formal aggregation adverb synonymous with 一共, predominantly used in administrative accounts, macroeconomic statistics, corporate auditing, and budgetary audits.
  • Example: 审计局核算出开发商总共挪用了一笔高达五千万元的专项改造基金。; Shěnjìjú hésuàn chū kāifāshāng zǒnggòng nuóyòngle yì bǐ gāodá wǔqiānwàn yuán de zhuānxiàng gǎizào jījīn.; The audit bureau calculated that the developer misappropriated a total of fifty million yuan in special renovation funds.

Restriction & Exclusivity (限定与排他范围)​

只; zhǐ; Only / Merely / Solely​

  • Usage: Primary restrictive adverb establishing strict exclusivity by eliminating all alternative candidates outside the designated target (只 + Verb/Predicate).
  • Example: 巡护员在那片原始森林里只发现了一株极其罕见的野生兰花。; Xúnhùyuán zài nà piàn yuánshǐ sēnlín lǐ zhǐ fāxiànle yì zhū jíqí hǎnjiàn de yěshēng lánhuā.; The patrol ranger found only a single, extremely rare wild orchid in that primeval forest.

光; guāng; Solely / Barely / Merely​

  • Usage: Spoken restrictive adverb emphasizing that a solitary action, entity, or factor is singled out; carries the pragmatic implication that the isolated element is insufficient by itself.
  • Example: 教练提醒学员光依靠节食而不配合一套科学的训练计划很难保持健康。; Jiàoliàn tíxǐng xuéyuán guāng yīkào jiéshí ér bù pèihé yí tào kēxué de xùnliàn jìhuà hěn nán bǎochí jiànkāng.; The coach reminded the trainees that merely relying on dieting without accompanying a scientific training plan makes it difficult to maintain health.

仅仅; jǐnjǐn; Merely / Barely / Only​

  • Usage: Formal restrictive modifier highlighting small numerical quantities, restricted scales, or minimal durations; frequently coordinates with restrictive post-verbal structures like 而已 or 不到.
  • Example: 这列新型磁悬浮列车仅仅花费了十五分钟就跨越了整座海峡。; Zhè liè xīnxíng cíxuánfú lièchē jǐnjǐn huāfèile shíwǔ fēnzhōng jiù kuàyuèle zhěng zuò hǎixiá.; This new type of maglev train took merely fifteen minutes to cross the entire strait.

Spatial & Situational Distribution (空间与常态分布)​

到处; dàochù; Everywhere / In all places​

  • Usage: Locative scope adverb meaning "in every place"; precedes predicates without requiring prepositions, denoting pervasive spatial distribution across an entire setting (到处 + 都 + Verb).
  • Example: 春季的清晨，游客漫步在那座植物园里到处都能闻到泥土与花草的芬芳。; Chūnjì de qīngchén, yóukè mànbù zài nà zuò zhíwùyuán lǐ dàochù dōu néng wéndào nítǔ yǔ huācǎo de fēnfāng.; On a spring morning, tourists strolling in that botanical garden can smell the fragrance of soil and flowers everywhere.

大致; dàzhì; Roughly / More or less / In general​

  • Usage: Delimits the broad conceptual boundaries or essential contours of an action, inventory, or proposition, bypassing trivial operational details (大致 + Verb/Predicate).
  • Example: 测绘员在现场大致勾勒出了一张反映山体滑坡范围的地形草图。; Cèhuìyuán zài xiànchǎng dàzhì gōulè chūle yì zhāng fǎnyìng shāntǐ huápō fànwéi de dìxíng cǎotú.; On site, the surveyor roughly sketched a topographical outline reflecting the scope of the landslide.

Parallelism & Inclusion (类同与递进关联)​

也; yě; Also / Too / Either​

  • Usage: Connective scope adverb indicating semantic parallel, shared state, or inclusive concurrence with a prior referent; must precede other auxiliary verbs, prepositions, or negation words (也 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 经过数月磋商，那支欧洲代表团也正式签署了这份涉及绿色能源的合作备忘录。; Jīngguò shù yuè cuōshāng, nà zhī Ōuzhōu dàibiǎotuán yě zhèngshì qiānshǔle zhè fèn shèjí lǜsè néngyuán de hézuò bèiwànglù.; After months of deliberations, that European delegation also formally signed this memorandum of cooperation regarding green energy.

Boundary & Utmost Limit (界限与最大幅度)​

尽量; jǐnliàng; To the best of one's ability / To the utmost​

  • Usage: Boundary-setting adverb indicating operating within the absolute upper or lower practical thresholds of capacity; modifies dynamic action verbs aiming to maximize or minimize an outcome (尽量 + Verb).
  • Example: 研发人员正努力改进配方，尽量降低这剂新型抗生素引发的过敏反应。; Yánfā rényuán zhèng nǔlì gǎijìn pèifāng, jǐnliàng jiàngdī zhè jì xīnxíng kàngshēngsù yǐnfā de guòmǐn fǎnyìng.; The research personnel are striving to refine the formula to minimize as much as possible the allergic reactions triggered by this dose of novel antibiotic.

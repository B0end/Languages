# Time & Aspect

Time adverbs establish temporal location relative to the moment of speech, while aspectual adverbs delineate the phase, duration, and continuity of an event. These markers precede verbs or adjectives to indicate whether an action is completed, ongoing, imminent, or progressing incrementally.

## 1. Past & Completed Aspect (过去与完成)

### [曾经; céngjīng; Once / Previously / Ever](ceng2jing1.wav)
* **Usage:** `曾经 + Verb (+ 过)`: Preverbal modifier indicating an action was experienced in the past and is no longer ongoing.
* **Example:** [他曾经在这家跨国企业担任过市场部经理。; Tā céngjīng zài zhè jiā kuàguó qǐyè dānrèn guo shìchǎngbù jīnglǐ.; He once served as the marketing manager in this multinational enterprise.](ta1_ceng2jing1_zai4_zhe4_jia1_kua4guo2_qi3ye4_dan1ren4_guo0_shi4chang3bu4_jing1li3.wav)

Frequency & Repetition

Adverbs of frequency and repetition occupy the pre-verbal adverbial position to define occurrence intervals, habitual tendencies, and iterative actions. Selection hinges on temporal aspect (realized past vs. projected future), empirical regularity versus absolute invariance, and pragmatic intent.

Habitual & General Frequency (日常频率与常态)​

总是; zǒngshì; Always / Invariably​

  • Usage: Placed before verbs or adjectives to denote an unchanging, persistent state or universal occurrence across all relevant scenarios (总是 + Verb/Adj).
  • Example: 这趟航班总是因为恶劣的天气延误。; Zhè tàng hángbān zǒngshì yīnwèi èliè de tiānqì yánwù.; This flight is always delayed due to severe weather.

经常; jīngcháng; Often / Frequently​

  • Usage: Modifies actions with high recurring frequency; can be directly negated by 不 to indicate infrequency (不经常), and can function as an attributive adjective modifying nouns.
  • Example: 这位律师经常处理一宗复杂的商业诉讼。; Zhè wèi lǜshī jīngcháng chǔlǐ yì zōng fùzá de shāngyè sùsòng.; This lawyer frequently handles complex commercial lawsuits.

常常; chángcháng; Frequently / Often​

  • Usage: Pre-verbal frequency adverb indicating habitual recurrence; cannot be modified by 不 (negated via 不常), cannot modify nouns, and focuses on routine actions.
  • Example: 建筑师常常加班修改一张关键的设计图纸。; Jiànzhùshī chángcháng jiābān xiūgǎi yì zhāng guānjiàn de shèjì túzhǐ.; Architects often work overtime to revise a crucial design drawing.

往往; wǎngwǎng; Often / As a rule​

  • Usage: Highlights an empirical regularity, predictable outcome, or natural tendency under specific conditions; requires a prerequisite context or conditional clause.
  • Example: 缺乏沟通的团队往往会引发一场严重的信任危机。; Quēfá gōutōng de tuánduì wǎngwǎng huì yǐnfā yì chǎng yánzhòng de xìnrèn wēijī.; Teams lacking communication often trigger a severe crisis of trust.

通常; tōngcháng; Usually / Normally​

  • Usage: Marks general conventions, standard operating procedures, or baseline conditions; can occupy sentence-initial position or function as a noun-modifier.
  • Example: 大使馆通常需要两周时间审批一份签证申请。; Dàshǐguǎn tōngcháng xūyào liǎng zhōu shíjiān shěnpī yí fèn qiānzhèng shēnqǐng.; The embassy usually takes two weeks to process a visa application.

偶尔; ǒu'ěr; Occasionally / Once in a while​

  • Usage: Signals low, non-systematic occurrence frequency; acts as the direct antonym of 经常 (偶尔 + Verb).
  • Example: 这位外科医生偶尔会弹奏一架古老的钢琴来缓解压力。; Zhè wèi wàikē yīshēng ǒu'ěr huì tánzòu yí jià gǔlǎo de gāngqín lái huǎnjiě yālì.; The surgeon occasionally plays an antique piano to relieve stress.

从来; cónglái; Always / Never (with negative)​

  • Usage: Denotes absolute continuity from past to present; predominantly coupled with negative particles 不 (habitual refusal: 从来不) or 没 (lack of experiential occurrence: 从来没).
  • Example: 这家谨慎的企业从来不参与任何一项高风险的投机交易。; Zhè jiā jǐnshèn de qǐyè cónglái bù cānyù rènhé yí xiàng gāo-fēngxiǎn de tóujī jiāoyì.; This prudent enterprise never engages in any high-risk speculative transactions.

Linear Continuance & State Invariance (持续频率与始终)​

一直; yìzhí; Continuously / All along​

  • Usage: Specifies an unbroken temporal span or sustained action without interruption (一直 + Verb/Predicate).
  • Example: 监督员一直密切监测那座钢铁厂的污水排放指标。; Jiāndūyuán yìzhí mìqiè jiāncè nà zuò gāngtiěchǎng de wūshuǐ páifàng zhǐbiāo.; Inspectors have consistently and closely monitored the wastewater emission metrics of that steel plant.

始终; shǐzhōng; From beginning to end / Throughout​

  • Usage: Formal adverb indicating persistent adherence to an orientation, principle, or factual truth across an entire duration despite external shifts.
  • Example: 那个科研团队在实验中始终保持着一种严谨的治学态度。; Nàge kēyán tuánduì zài shíyàn zhōng shǐzhōng bǎochízhe yì zhǒng yánjǐn de zhìxué tàidu.; The research team maintained a rigorous scholarly attitude throughout their experiments.

Event Recurrence & Successive Actions (事件复发与再现)​

又; yòu; Again (realized) / Both... and...​

  • Usage: Marks the recurrence of an event that has already taken place (past/factual), or cyclical actions occurring by regular schedule.
  • Example: 这个苹果又大又甜。; Zhè ge píngguǒ yòu dà yòu tián.;This apple is both big and sweet

再; zài; Again (future) / Once more​

  • Usage: Directs repetition toward future, unfulfilled, or prospective events; commonly marks sequential completion (先...再...).
  • Example: 委员会建议他先补充一份财务证明，然后再递交申请表。; Wěiyuánhuì jiànyì tā xiān bǔchōng yí fèn cáiwù zhèngmíng, ránhòu zài dìjiāo shēnqǐngbiǎo.; The committee suggested that he supplement a financial certificate first before submitting the application form.

重新; chóngxīn; Anew / Again from the start​

  • Usage: Denotes initiating an operation from the beginning, typically to revise, correct, or adjust previous unsatisfactory outcomes (重新 + Verb).
  • Example: 董事会要求专家组重新评估那一套投资方案的可行性。; Dǒngshìhuì yāoqiú zhuānjiāzǔ chóngxīn pínggū nà yí tào tóuzī fāng'àn de kěxíngxìng.; The board of directors requested the expert group to re-evaluate the feasibility of that investment scheme.

Repeated Iteration & Insistence (反复施加与多次强调)​

反复; fǎnfù; Repeatedly / Over and over​

  • Usage: Highlights cyclical iterations, back-and-forth behavioral processes, or exhaustive deliberation before reaching a conclusion (反复 + Verb).
  • Example: 发言人反复推敲了一篇外交演讲稿中的用词细节。; Fāyánrén fǎnfù tuīqiāo le yì piān wàijiāo yǎnjiǎnggǎo zhōng de yòngcí xìjié.; The spokesperson repeatedly scrutinized the wording details in a diplomatic speech draft.

再三; zàisān; Over and over / Repeatedly​

  • Usage: Emphasizes high-stakes deliberate intent, urging, or consideration; restricted almost exclusively to communicative, cognitive, or intentional verbs (再三考虑, 再三叮嘱).
  • Example: 经理再三叮嘱出纳员务必妥善保管那一枚公章。; Jīnglǐ zàisān dīngzhǔ chūnàyuán wùbì tuǒshàn bǎoguǎn nà yì méi gōngzhāng.; The manager repeatedly instructed the cashier to properly safe-keep that official seal.

一再; yízài; Repeatedly / Time and again​

  • Usage: Stresses ongoing reiteration of an action, particularly in contexts where advice is unheeded or where an undesirable situation is repeatedly delayed or ignored.
  • Example: 组委会因为突发情况一再推迟了那一届博览会的开幕日期。; Zǔwěihuì yīnwèi tūfā qíngkuàng yízài tuīchí le nà yí jiè bólǎnhuì de kāimù rìqī.; Due to unexpected circumstances, the organizing committee repeatedly postponed the opening date of that expo.

屡次; lǚcì; Time and again / Repeatedly​

  • Usage: Formal adverb indicating repeated occurrences of an event, commonly applied to infractions, regulatory violations, or uncorrected errors.
  • Example: 那个司机屡次违反交通信号灯，最终被吊销了一本驾驶证。; Nàge sījī lǚcì wéifǎn jiāotōng xìnhàodēng, zuìzhōng bèi diàoxiāo le yì běn jiàshǐzhèng.; That driver repeatedly violated traffic lights and ultimately had his driver's license revoked.

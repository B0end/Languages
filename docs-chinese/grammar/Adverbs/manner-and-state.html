Manner & State

Manner and state adverbs delineate how an action is executed, characterizing personal agency, deliberate intent, interpersonal reciprocity, temporal succession, or stealth. They occupy the pre-verbal adverbial position (Adverb + Verb), qualifying the dynamics of the predicate without altering its core argument structure.

Intentionality & Purpose (主观意图与专向)​

故意; gùyì; Deliberately / On purpose​

  • Usage: Modifies actions executed with conscious premeditation, often carrying a negative pragmatic nuance of provocation, concealment, or calculated rule-breaking (故意 + Verb).
  • Example: 证人在法庭上故意隐瞒了一条能够证明被告清白的关键线索。; Zhèngrén zài fǎtíng shàng gùyì yǐnmánle yì tiáo nénggòu zhèngmíng bèigào qīngbái de guānjiàn xiànsuǒ.; The witness deliberately concealed a critical clue in court that could prove the defendant's innocence.

特意; tèyì; Specially / Purposefully​

  • Usage: Highlights dedicated effort, special thoughtfulness, or targeted accommodation made specifically for a positive or courteous purpose (特意 + 为/给... + Verb).
  • Example: 老教授特意为毕业典礼挑选了一套饱含学术传承的珍贵古籍。; Lǎo jiàoshòu tèyì wèi bìyè diǎnlǐ tiāoxuǎnle yí tào bǎohán xuéshù chuánchéng de zhēnguì gǔjí.; The old professor specially selected a set of precious ancient classics imbued with scholarly heritage for the graduation ceremony.

专门; zhuānmén; Exclusively / Specifically​

  • Usage: Indicates operational specialization, dedicated task assignment, or institutional purpose; functions both as a pre-verbal adverb (专门 + Verb) and as an attributive adjective modifying nouns (专门人才).
  • Example: 卫生部门专门设立了一所装备先进的康复中心来照料伤残退伍军人。; Wèishēng bùmén zhuānmén shèlìle yì suǒ zhuāngbèi xiānjìn de kāngfù zhōngxīn lái zhàoliào shāngcán tuìwǔ jūnrén.; The health department specially established a rehabilitation center with advanced equipment to care for disabled veterans.

轻易; qīngyì; Lightly / Rashly / Easily​

  • Usage: Characterizes actions performed casually, without due reflection or resistance; overwhelmingly negated (不轻易 / 切勿轻易) to signal prudent restraint.
  • Example: 资深投资人绝不会因为短期市场波动就轻易抛售手头那批优质股票。; Zīshēn tóuzīrén jué bú huì yīnwèi duǎnqī shìchǎng bōdòng jiù qīngyì pāoshòu shǒutóu nà pī yōuzhì gǔpiào.; Experienced investors will by no means rashly sell off that batch of high-quality shares in their hands due to short-term market fluctuations.

顺便; shùnbiàn; In passing / Conveniently​

  • Usage: Indicates that a secondary, incidental action is accomplished along the trajectory of a primary errand without incurring extra effort or travel (顺便 + Verb).
  • Example: 秘书去市政厅办事时，顺便投递了一封加急的商务邀请函。; Mìshū qù shìzhèngtīng bànshì shí, shùnbiàn tóudìle yì fēng jiājí de shāngwù yāoqǐnghán.; While running errands at City Hall, the secretary conveniently delivered an urgent commercial invitation letter in passing.

Personal Execution & Autonomy (亲历实施与独处)​

亲自; qīnzì; In person / Personally​

  • Usage: Emphasizes first-hand involvement, on-site presence, or personal leadership by a person of status, ruling out surrogates or subordinates (亲自 + Verb).
  • Example: 总建筑师冒着严寒亲自视察了那一处位于悬崖边缘的施工工地。; Zǒng jiànzhùshī màozhe yánhán qīnzì shìchále nà yí chù wèiyú xuányá biānyuán de shīgōng gōngdì.; Braving the severe cold, the chief architect personally inspected that construction site perched on the edge of the cliff.

单独; dāndú; Alone / By oneself / Independently​

  • Usage: Specifies an individual action conducted in isolation without companions, external assistance, or collaborative supervision (单独 + Verb).
  • Example: 经过严格考核，那位飞行学员终于获准单独驾驶一架轻型教练机。; Jīngguò yángé kǎohé, nà wèi fēixíng xuéyuán zhōngyú huòzhǔn dāndú jiàshǐ yí jià qīngxíng jiàoliànjī.; After rigorous assessment, that flight trainee was finally permitted to fly a light trainer aircraft alone.

Stealth & Covert Manner (隐秘情状与潜抑)​

悄悄; qiāoqiāo; Quietly / Stealthily​

  • Usage: Depicts physical actions carried out with minimal sound or overt motion to avoid detection or prevent disturbing others (悄悄 + Motion/Physical Verb).
  • Example: 值班护士替病人掖好被角，然后悄悄关上了那扇沉重的病房木门。; Zhíbān hùshi tì bìngrén yē hǎo bèijiǎo, ránhòu qiāoqiāo guānshàngle nà shàn chénzhòng de bìngfáng mùmén.; The nurse on duty tucked the patient in, then quietly closed that heavy wooden ward door.

暗暗; àn'àn; Inwardly / Secretly to oneself​

  • Usage: Characterizes unexpressed internal reflections, hidden emotional currents, or private determinations kept strictly inside one's thoughts (暗暗 + Cognitive/Psych Verb).
  • Example: 青年学者在心里暗暗下定决心，一定要攻克这一道困扰学界多年的几何难题。; Qīngnián xuézhě zài xīnli àn'àn xiàdìng juéxīn, yídìng yào gōngkè zhè yí dào kùnrǎo xuéjiè duō nián de jǐhé nántí.; The young scholar secretly resolved in his mind that he must conquer this geometry conundrum that has troubled academia for years.

Reciprocity & Collective Action (相互关系与协同)​

互相; hùxiāng; Mutually / Each other​

  • Usage: Pre-verbal reciprocal adverb signaling two-way interactive feedback between plural subjects; directly precedes transitive action verbs (互相 + Verb); cannot be modified by degree adverbs.
  • Example: 两家新兴科技企业在市场竞争中互相借鉴了一套高效的管理经验。; Liǎng jiā xīnxīng kējì qǐyè zài shìchǎng jìngzhēng zhōng hùxiāng jièjiànle yí tào gāoxiào de guǎnlǐ jīngyàn.; The two emerging technology companies mutually borrowed an efficient set of management experiences amidst market competition.

相互; xiānghù; Reciprocally / Mutually​

  • Usage: Formal reciprocal marker functioning flexibly as a pre-verbal adverb (相互支持), an attributive noun modifier (相互关系), or an abstract relational noun.
  • Example: 两国边界部队依据那份防务条约相互通报了近期的巡逻动态。; Liǎng guó biānjiè bùduì yījù nà fèn fángwù tiáoyuē xiānghù tōngbàole jìnqī de xúnduó dòngtài.; The border forces of both nations mutually briefed each other on recent patrol developments in accordance with that defense treaty.

一起; yìqǐ; Together / In company​

  • Usage: Standard connective adverb indicating joint spatial co-presence or simultaneous collaborative effort (A 与/跟 B 一起 + Verb).
  • Example: 青年学者同几位资深勘探队员一起绘制了一幅精密的地层剖面图。; Qīngnián xuézhě tóng jǐ wèi zīshēn kāntàn duìyuán yìqǐ huìzhìle yì fú jīngmì de dìcéng pōumiàntú.; The young scholar, together with several veteran prospectors, plotted a precise geological cross-section diagram.

一同; yìtóng; Together / In company with​

  • Usage: Formal and literary counterpart of 一起; predominantly employed in ceremonial, diplomatic, or institutional accounts depicting joint participation.
  • Example: 文化部长与外国使节一同出席了那一套珍稀非遗织锦的捐赠仪式。; Wénhuà bùzhǎng yǔ wàiguó shǐjié yìtóng chūxíle nà yí tào zhēnxī fēiyí zhījǐn de juānzèng yíshì.; The Minister of Culture, together with foreign envoys, attended the donation ceremony for that set of rare intangible heritage brocades.

Succession & Collective Flow (接续态势与群体动向)​

纷纷; fēnfēn; One after another / In succession​

  • Usage: Descriptive plural manner adverb depicting numerous diverse actors taking action in rapid, spontaneous succession or widespread wave-like concurrence (Plural Subject + 纷纷 + Verb).
  • Example: 灾情发生后，沿海市民纷纷向慈善机构捐献了一批急需的御寒衣物。; Zāiqíng fāshēng hòu, yánhǎi shìmín fēnfēn xiàng císhàn jīgòu juānxiànle yì pī jíxū de yùhán yīwù.; After the disaster occurred, coastal citizens one after another donated a batch of urgently needed cold-weather clothing to charity organizations.

陆续; lùxù; Successively / One after another​

  • Usage: Objective sequential adverb denoting an orderly, staged continuation where entities arrive or actions transpire intermittently across a timeline (陆续 + Verb).
  • Example: 各国代表团在清晨陆续登上了那艘停泊在港湾内的科考母船。; Gè guó dàibiǎotuán zài qīngchén lùxù dēngshàngle nà sōu tíngbó zài gǎngwān nèi de kěkǎo mǔchuán.; The delegations of various countries stepped one after another in succession onto that scientific research mothership moored in the harbor at dawn.

Persistence & Unchanged Manner (沿袭状态与依旧)​

照样; zhàoyàng; Still / As usual / All the same​

  • Usage: Denotes that an action or operational routine persists strictly according to standard precedent, completely undeterred by altered conditions, interference, or hazards (照样 + Verb).
  • Example: 尽管外头暴雨倾盆，老邮递员照样按时将那一捆报纸送达了深山哨所。; Jǐnguǎn wàitou bàoyǔ qīngpén, lǎo yóudìyuán zhàoyàng ànshí jiāng nà yì kǔn bàozhǐ sòngdá le shēnshān shàosuǒ.; Even though torrential rain poured outside, the veteran postman delivered that bundle of newspapers to the deep-mountain outpost on schedule all the same.

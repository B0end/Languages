Degree & Approximation

Degree and approximation adverbs modify adjectives, psych-verbs, and numeric structures to calibrate intensity, comparative rank, or quantitative precision. Selection depends on syntactic positioning (pre-verbal vs. post-verbal complement), register (colloquial vs. formal), and whether the comparison is relative, absolute, or dynamic.

Absolute & High Degree (绝对程度与极度)​

很; hěn; Very / Quite​

  • Usage: Unmarked, baseline intensifier placed before adjectives or psychological verbs (很 + Adj/Verb); serves as the standard structural placeholder in non-comparative descriptive predicates.
  • Example: 那个博物馆珍藏着一件历史价值很高的青铜器。; Nàge bówùguǎn zhēncángzhe yí jiàn lìshǐ jiàzhí hěn gāo de qīngtóngqì.; That museum preserves a bronze vessel of very high historical value.

非常; fēicháng; Very / Exceptionally​

  • Usage: Standard high-intensity degree adverb preceding adjectives, modal verbs, or cognitive-emotional verbs (非常 + Adj/Verb); conveys greater intensity than 很.
  • Example: 营养师推荐了一款对糖尿病患者非常有益的健康食谱。; Yíngyǎngshī tuījiànle yì kuǎn duì tángniàobìng huànzhě fēicháng yǒuyì de jiànkāng shípǔ.; The nutritionist recommended a healthy recipe that is exceptionally beneficial for diabetic patients.

特别; tèbié; Especially / Particularly​

  • Usage: Precedes predicates to express exceptional prominence or distinctive intensity; can also serve as an adjective meaning "unique".
  • Example: 工程师研发出一种抗压性能特别优异的特种合金。; Gōngchéngshī yánfā chū yì zhǒng kàngyā xìngnéng tèbié yōuyì de tèzhǒng héjīn.; The engineers developed a special alloy with particularly outstanding compression-resistant performance.

十分; shífēn; Fully / Utterly / Extremely​

  • Usage: Formal intensifier denoting a comprehensive, near-total degree (literally "ten tenths"); modifies gradable adjectives and stative verbs.
  • Example: 答辩委员会对那篇论文所展现的缜密逻辑感到十分满意。; Dábiàn wěiyuánhuì duì nà piān lùnwén suǒ zhǎnxiàn de zhěnmì luóji gǎndào shífēn mǎnyì.; The defense committee felt extremely satisfied with the rigorous logic demonstrated in that thesis.

极; jí; Extremely / Exceedingly​

  • Usage: Highest-tier degree marker; functions predominantly as a post-adjectival complement with 了 (Adj + 极了), or directly pre-modifies select monosyllabic adjectives in literary styles.
  • Example: 探险队在翻越那道险关后已经疲惫极了。; Tànxiǎnduì zài fānyuè nà dào xiǎnguān hòu yǐjīng píbèi jí le.; The expedition team was exhausted to the extreme after crossing that treacherous pass.

极其; jíqí; Exceedingly / Extremely​

  • Usage: Formal pre-verbal/pre-adjectival modifier denoting the absolute peak of an attribute (极其 + Multi-syllable Adj/Verb); strictly excluded from post-verbal complement positions.
  • Example: 物理实验室要求研究员遵循一套极其严格的安全操作规程。; Wùlǐ shíyànshì yāoqiú yánjiūyuán zūnxún yí tào jíqí yángé de ānquán cāozuò guīchéng.; The physics laboratory requires researchers to adhere to an exceedingly strict set of safety operating procedures.

格外; géwài; Especially / Exceptionally​

  • Usage: Marks an intensity that surpasses ordinary bounds, typically provoked by a specific background condition, contrast, or environmental context (格外 + Adj).
  • Example: 金色的晨光洒在那栋历史建筑上，显得格外庄严。; Jīnsè de chénguāng sǎ zài nà dòng lìshǐ jiànzhù shàng, xiǎnde géwài zhuāngyán.; The golden morning light spilled over that historical building, appearing exceptionally solemn.

相当; xiāngdāng; Considerably / Fairly​

  • Usage: Evaluative degree adverb expressing a prominent, noteworthy level that meets or exceeds expectations (相当 + Adj/Verb).
  • Example: 银行审批这笔巨额商业贷款的流程相当复杂。; Yínháng shěnpī zhè bǐ jù'é shāngyè dàikuǎn de liúchéng xiāngdāng fùzá.; The bank's approval process for this substantial commercial loan is considerably complex.

太; tài; Too / Excessively / Extremely​

  • Usage: Expresses an excessive degree that breaches an acceptable limit (太 + Adj/Verb), or conveys emphatic praise in exclamatory constructions (太 + Adj + 了).
  • Example: 那个剧团今晚演出的这出舞台剧太震撼了。; Nàge jùtuán jīnwǎn yǎnchū de zhè chū wǔtáijù tài zhènhàn le.; This stage play performed by that theatrical troupe tonight was breathtakingly astounding.

挺; tǐng; Quite / Pretty​

  • Usage: Colloquial and spoken intensifier conveying an approving or casual tone; typically frames adjectives alongside modal particle 的 (挺 + Adj + 的).
  • Example: 旅行社在景区为游客安排的那间客房挺宽敞的。; Lǚxíngshè zài jǐngqū wèi yóukè ānpái de nà jiān kèfáng tǐng kuānchǎng de.; That guest room arranged by the travel agency for tourists in the scenic area is quite spacious.

真; zhēn; Really / Truly​

  • Usage: Emphatic, emotive adverb used in affirmative assertions and direct observations (真 + Adj/Verb); expresses sincere conviction, subjective appraisal, or exclamatory surprise.
  • Example: 老裁缝手工缝制的这件旗袍剪裁真精致！; Lǎo cáifeng shǒugōng féngzhì de zhè jiàn qípáo jiǎncái zhēn jīngzhì!; The tailoring on this cheongsam hand-sewn by the master tailor is truly exquisite!

Comparative & Relative Degree (相对程度与比较)​

更; gèng; Even more / Further​

  • Usage: Relative degree adverb indicating escalation on top of an existing baseline; used in comparative sentences (比...更...) or implicit comparative contexts.
  • Example: 新提出的这套节能方案比原计划更能节省开支。; Xīn tíchū de zhè tào jiénéng fāng'àn bǐ yuán jìhuà gèng néng jiéshěng kāizhī.; This newly proposed energy-saving scheme can cut expenses even more than the original plan.

更加; gèngjiā; Even more / Still more​

  • Usage: Formal, multi-syllable equivalent of 更; primarily modifies two-syllable adjectives and complex verbal constructions to signify amplified progression.
  • Example: 政府颁布的这项新法规让企业减排的标准更加明确。; Zhèngfǔ bānbù de zhè xiàng xīn fǎguī ràng qǐyè jiǎnpái de biāozhǔn gèngjiā míngquè.; This new regulation promulgated by the government makes emission reduction standards for enterprises even more explicit.

最; zuì; Most / -est (Superlative)​

  • Usage: Superlative degree adverb expressing the supreme or terminal point within a referenced comparative set (最 + Adj/Verb).
  • Example: 这座国际机场是全国起降货运航班最繁忙的枢纽。; Zhè zuò guójì jīchǎng shì quánguó qǐjiàng huòyùn hángbān zuì fánmáng de shūniǔ.; This international airport is the most active hub in the entire country for landing and departing cargo flights.

比较; bǐjiào; Comparatively / Relatively​

  • Usage: Attenuates assertions by marking moderate comparative intensity ("fairly / relatively"); modifies adjectives and stative verbs without needing a direct comparator.
  • Example: 技术人员建议挑选那款性能比较稳定的操作系统。; Jìshù rényuán jiànyì tiāoxuǎn nà kuǎn xìngnéng bǐjiào wěndìng de cāozuò xìtǒng.; Technicians suggest selecting that operating system with relatively stable performance.

尤其; yóuqí; Especially / Particularly​

  • Usage: Singling-out adverb that isolates a specific item within a broader class to show a heightened degree; regularly paired with copula 是 (尤其/特别是 + Focal Element).
  • Example: 展厅里陈列的现代艺术品吸引了大量观众，尤其是那一幅抽象油画。; Zhǎntīng lǐ chénliè de xiàndài yìshùpǐn xīyǐnle dàliàng guānzhòng, yóuqí shì nà yì fú chōuxiàng yóuhuà.; The modern artworks displayed in the exhibition hall attracted vast audiences, especially that abstract oil painting.

稍微; shāowēi; Slightly / A little bit​

  • Usage: Designates minimal degree or slight adjustment; commonly coordinates with post-adjectival diminutive markers (稍微 + Adj/Verb + 一点儿 / 一些).
  • Example: 调琴师把那根琴弦稍微调紧了一点儿。; Tiáoqínshī bǎ nà gēn qínxián shāowēi tiáojǐn le yìdiǎnr.; The piano tuner tightened that musical string slightly.

Progressive & Dynamic Degree (渐进程度与动态)​

日益; rìyì; Day by day / Increasingly​

  • Usage: Written, progressive adverb meaning "more with each passing day"; exclusively modifies dynamic gradable adjectives and trend-oriented verbs (日益 + Adj/Verb).
  • Example: 跨国制造企业必须面对一场日益激烈的全球人才竞争。; Kuàguó zhìzào qǐyè bìxū miànduì yì chǎng rìyì jīliè de quánqiú réncái jìngzhēng.; Multinational manufacturing enterprises must confront an increasingly fierce global talent competition.

越; yuè; The more... / Increasingly​

  • Usage: Correlative progressive marker indicating scalar increase proportionally tied to time or conditions (越 A 越 B, or temporally anchored 越来越 + Adj).
  • Example: 登山队员越接近那座雪山的山顶，空气就越稀薄。; Dēngshānduìyuán yuè jiējìn nà zuò xuěshān de shāndǐng, kōngqì jiù yuè xībó.; The closer the mountaineers got to the summit of that snow mountain, the thinner the air became.

越发; yuèfā; All the more / Increasingly​

  • Usage: Literary variant of 更加/越来越, signaling intensified internal reactions or deepening conditions sparked by a specific development (越发 + Adj/Verb).
  • Example: 听完那位负责人的这番辩解，调查人员的心中越发感到困惑。; Tīngwán nà wèi fùzérén de zhè fān biànjiě, diàochá rényuán de xīnzhōng yuèfā gǎndào kùnhuò.; After listening to this justification from that person in charge, the investigators felt all the more perplexed.

Approximation & Thresholds (概数估测与逼近)​

几乎; jīhū; Almost / Nearly​

  • Usage: Denotes close approximation to a critical threshold, total state, or absolute limit without reaching it (几乎 + Verb/Adj/Quantifier); focuses on proximity to a whole.
  • Example: 昨晚席卷沿海的那场强台风几乎摧毁了整座果园。; Zuówǎn xíjuǎn yánhǎi de nà chǎng qiáng táifēng jīhū cuīhuǐle zhěng zuò guǒyuán.; That severe typhoon that swept the coast last night almost destroyed the entire orchard.

差不多; chàbuduō; Almost / About the same​

  • Usage: Flexible approximation marker; operates as a pre-predicate adverb ("nearly"), a predicative adjective ("comparable / almost alike"), or modifies numerical amounts.
  • Example: 翻译团队历时三年，差不多完成了那部哲学巨著的中文译稿。; Fānyì tuánduì lìshí sān nián, chàbuduō wánchéngle nà bù zhéxué jùzhù de zhōngwén yìgǎo.; Spending three years, the translation team has almost completed the Chinese draft of that monumental philosophical work.

大约; dàyuē; Approximately / About​

  • Usage: Objective approximation adverb used explicitly with quantities, numerical estimates, or factual intervals (大约 + Numeral-Classifier Phrase).
  • Example: 那艘满载矿石的货轮大约需要航行四十五天才能抵达目的港。; Nà sōu mǎnzǎi kuàngshí de huòlún dàyuē xūyào hángxíng sìshíwǔ tiān cái néng dǐdá mùdìgǎng. ; That freighter fully loaded with ore needs to sail for approximately forty-five days to arrive at the destination port.

大概; dàgài; Probably / Roughly​

  • Usage: Expresses either numerical approximation or epistemic conjecture ("most likely / presumably"); possesses broader subjective probability than purely factual 大约.
  • Example: 药剂师估算调制这一剂传统汤药大概需要耗费两百元。; Yàojìshī gūsuàn tiáozhì zhè jì chuántǒng tāngyào dàgài xūyào hàofèi liǎngbǎi yuán.; The pharmacist estimated that preparing this dose of traditional medicinal broth would probably cost two hundred yuan.

Exclamatory Intensity (感叹程度)​

多么; duōme; How / What (an intense...)​

  • Usage: Emphatic exclamatory adverb placed immediately before adjectives or psychological verbs to indicate heightened subjective sentiment (多么 + Adj/Verb (+ 啊)!); restricted to exclamative syntax.
  • Example: 能亲身参与并见证这一段伟大的历史是多么令人自豪的体验啊！; Néng qīnshēn cānyù bìng jiànzhèng zhè yí duàn wěidà de lìshǐ shì duōme lìngrén zìháo de tǐyàn a!; What a proud experience it is to personally participate in and witness this great period of history!

Time & Aspect

Time adverbs establish temporal location relative to the moment of speech, while aspectual adverbs delineate the phase, duration, and continuity of an event. These markers precede verbs or adjectives to indicate whether an action is completed, ongoing, imminent, or progressing incrementally.

1. Past & Completed Aspect (过去与完成)​

曾经; céngjīng; Once / Previously / Ever​

  • Usage: 曾经 + Verb (+ 过): Preverbal modifier indicating an action was experienced in the past and is no longer ongoing.
  • Example: 他曾经在这家跨国企业担任过市场部经理。; Tā céngjīng zài zhè jiā kuàguó qǐyè dānrèn guo shìchǎngbù jīnglǐ.; He once served as the marketing manager in this multinational enterprise.

已经; yǐjīng; Already​

  • Usage: 已经 + Verb / Predicate (+ 了): Marks the completion of an action or the attainment of a state before the reference time.
  • Example: 财务部门已经把上个月的报表审核完毕了。; Cáiwù bùmén yǐjīng bǎ shàng gè yuè de bàobiǎo shěnhé wánbì le.; The finance department has already finished auditing last month's report.

刚; gāng; Just / Only a moment ago​

  • Usage: Subject + 刚 + Verb: Monosyllabic adverb placed strictly after the subject to indicate an action occurred moments prior.
  • Example: 我们刚办完那笔房屋贷款手续。; Wǒmen gāng bàn wán nà bǐ fángwū dàikuǎn shǒuxù.; We just finished processing that housing loan procedure.

刚刚; gānggang; Just now / A moment ago​

  • Usage: (Time / Subject) + 刚刚 + Verb: Time adverbial placed either before or after the subject indicating recent occurrence.
  • Example: 那场关于环境保护的辩论赛刚刚结束。; Nà chǎng guānyú huánjìng bǎohù de biànlùnsài gānggang jiéshù.; That debate competition on environmental protection just concluded.

才; cái; Only then / Not until​

  • Usage: Time / Condition + 才 + Verb: Denotes that an action occurred late, slowly, or only after meeting a difficult prerequisite.
  • Example: 经过三个小时的协商，双方才达成一致。; Jīngguò sān gè xiǎoshí de xiéshāng, shuāngfāng cái dáchéng yízhì.; Only after three hours of consultation did both sides reach an agreement.

2. Ongoing & Continuous Aspect (进行与持续)​

正在; zhèngzài; In the process of / Right now​

  • Usage: 正在 + Verb (+ 呢): Highlights that a dynamic action is actively in progress at the anchor moment.
  • Example: 技术人员正在调试那台进口精密仪器。; Jìshù rényuán zhèngzài tiáoshì nà tái jìnkǒu jīngmì yíqì.; The technicians are debugging that imported precision instrument right now.

在; zài; In the middle of​

  • Usage: 在 + Verb (+ 呢): Core progressive aspect marker denoting ongoing action.
  • Example: 设计师在修改那个新产品的包装方案。; Shèjìshī zài xiūgǎi nà ge xīn chǎnpǐn de bāozhuāng fāng'àn.; The designer is modifying the packaging proposal for that new product.

一直; yìzhí; Continuously / All along​

  • Usage: 一直 + Verb / Predicate: Denotes uninterrupted temporal continuation of an action or consistent adherence to a state.
  • Example: 这项科研项目一直面临着资金短缺的困境。; Zhè xiàng kēyán xiàngmù yìzhí miànlínzhe zījīn duǎnquē de kùnjìng.; This scientific research project has all along faced the dilemma of funding shortages.

始终; shǐzhōng; From beginning to end / Always​

  • Usage: 始终 + Verb / Negation: Stresses unwavering persistence of an attitude, principle, or habit through the entire timeframe (cannot refer to future actions).
  • Example: 面对巨大的竞争压力，他始终保持着乐观的心态。; Miànduì jùdà de jìngzhēng yālì, tā shǐzhōng bǎochízhe lèguān de xīntài.; Facing immense competitive pressure, he maintained an optimistic mindset from beginning to end.

仍然; réngrán; Still / Yet / As before​

  • Usage: 仍然 + Verb / Adj: Objective adverb indicating that a previous state remains unchanged despite expectations or external shifts.
  • Example: 尽管市场竞争激烈，这家老字号仍然拥有稳定的客源。; Jǐnguǎn shìchǎng jìngzhēng jīliè, zhè jiā lǎozìhào réngrán yōngyǒu wěndìng de kèyuán.; Despite fierce market competition, this time-honored shop still possesses a stable customer base.

依然; yīrán; Still / As ever​

  • Usage: 依然 + Verb / Adj: Literary equivalent to 仍然; emphasizes persistent, undisturbed continuation of an original state.
  • Example: 离开故乡多年，那座古老的钟楼依然伫立在广场中央。; Líkāi gùxiāng duō nián, nà zuò gǔlǎo de zhōnglóu yīrán zhùlì zài guǎngchǎng zhōngyāng.; Having left the hometown for many years, that ancient bell tower still stands in the center of the square.

从来; cónglái; Always / Never (with negation)​

  • Usage: 从来 + 不 / 没(有) + Verb: Indicates that a condition or habit has applied without exception from the past up to the present.
  • Example: 那位严谨的教授从来没有缺席过任何一次学术研讨会。; Nà wèi yánjǐn de jiàoshòu cónglái méiyǒu quēxí guo rènhé yí cì xuéshù yántǎohuì.; That rigorous professor has never once been absent from any academic seminar.

3. Immediate, Imminent & Sequential Action (紧接、即将与承接)​

马上; mǎshàng; Immediately / Right away​

  • Usage: 马上 + Verb: Denotes action occurring immediately or very shortly after the anchor time.
  • Example: 维修工程师马上过来排查这台发动机的故障。; Wéixiū gōngchéngshī mǎshàng guòlai páichá zhè tái fādòngjī de gùzhàng.; The maintenance engineer will come immediately to troubleshoot the malfunction of this engine.

立刻; lìkè; Promptly / At once​

  • Usage: 立刻 + Verb: Emphasizes instantaneous temporal response with zero elapsed time; more formal than 马上.
  • Example: 听到火警铃声，全体员工立刻疏散到了安全地带。; Tīngdào huǒjǐng língshēng, quántǐ yuángōng lìkè shūsàn dào le ānquán dìdài.; Upon hearing the fire alarm, the entire staff evacuated to a safe area at once.

即将; jíjiāng; Soon / Imminently​

  • Usage: 即将 + Verb: Formal written adverb denoting imminent transition into an anticipated event.
  • Example: 那部耗时三年拍摄的纪录片即将公映。; Nà bù hàoshí sān nián pāishè de jìlùpiàn jíjiāng gōngyìng.; That documentary film, which took three years to shoot, is about to be publicly released.

快要; kuàiyào; About to / Soon​

  • Usage: 快要 + Verb / Adj + 了: Imminent aspect frame marking an event about to occur; cannot co-occur with specific clock times.
  • Example: 飞往伦敦的那趟国际航班快要停止登机了。; Fēiwǎng Lúndūn de nà tàng guójì hángbān kuàiyào tíngzhǐ dēngjī le.; That international flight bound for London is about to close boarding.

紧接着; jǐnjiēzhe; Immediately after / Next​

  • Usage: Clause 1，紧接着 + Clause 2: Temporal connector showing immediate sequential occurrence without interruption.
  • Example: 开幕式致辞完毕，紧接着举行了隆重的签约仪式。; Kāimùshì zhìcí wánbì, jǐnjiēzhe jǔxíng le lóngzhòng de qiānyuē yíshì.; The opening speeches finished, and immediately following that, a solemn signing ceremony was held.

便; biàn; Promptly / Then​

  • Usage: Condition / Time + 便 + Verb: Formal and literary equivalent of 就; denotes quick sequential occurrence or a direct causal link.
  • Example: 警方接到报案电话便迅速赶往现场。; Jǐngfāng jiēdào bào'àn diànhuà biàn xùnsù gǎnwǎng xiànchǎng.; Having received the emergency call, the police promptly rushed to the scene.

先; xiān; First / In advance​

  • Usage: 先 + Verb 1，(再 / 然后) + Verb 2: Marks the preliminary action in a multi-step sequence.
  • Example: 我们应当先收集消费者的反馈数据，然后再调整营销策略。; Wǒmen yīngdāng xiān shōují xiāofèizhě de fǎnkuì shùjù, ránhòu zài tiáozhěng yíngxiāo cèlüè.; We ought to first collect consumer feedback data, and then adjust our marketing strategy.

4. Suddenness, Urgency & Manner of Time (突发、急迫与应变)​

突然; tūrán; Suddenly / Abruptly​

  • Usage: 突然 + Verb / Adj / Predicate: Functions as adverb or adjective marking an unexpected, sudden shift in conditions.
  • Example: 行车途中，车辆前方的警示灯突然亮了起来。; Xíngchē túzhōng, chēliàng qiánfāng de jǐngshìdēng tūrán liàng le qǐlai.; Midway through driving, the warning light in front of the vehicle suddenly lit up.

忽然; hūrán; Suddenly / Unexpectedly​

  • Usage: (Subject) + 忽然 + Verb: Strict adverb indicating an unforeseen thought or event occurring in a split second.
  • Example: 散步时，他脑海中忽然闪现出一个绝妙的灵感。; Sànbù shí, tā nǎohǎi zhōng hūrán shǎnxiàn chū yí gè juémiào de línggǎn.; While taking a walk, a brilliant flash of inspiration suddenly emerged in his mind.

猛然; měngrán; Abruptly / Startlingly​

  • Usage: 猛然 + Verb: Denotes an intensely sudden physical jerk, violent movement, or sharp psychological awakening.
  • Example: 听到刹车声，坐在后排的乘客猛然惊醒。; Tīngdào shāchē shēng, zuò zài hòupái de chéngkè měngrán jīngxǐng.; Hearing the sound of screeching brakes, the passenger sitting in the back row was startled awake.

赶紧; gǎnjǐn; Hurriedly / Losing no time​

  • Usage: 赶紧 + Verb: Stresses urgency driven by external necessity, approaching deadlines, or weather shifts.
  • Example: 暴风雨即将来临，港口的渔船赶紧返回码头避风。; Bàofēngyǔ jíjiāng láilín, gǎngkǒu de yúchuán gǎnjǐn fǎnhuí mǎtou bìfēng.; The storm was imminent, so the fishing boats in the harbor hurried back to the pier for shelter.

赶快; gǎnkuài; Quickly / At once​

  • Usage: 赶快 + Verb: Stresses speeding up execution; dominant in spoken imperative directives.
  • Example: 提交申请的截止日期快到了，请赶快补齐缺少的材料。; Tíjiāo shēnqǐng de jiézhǐ rìqī kuài dào le, qǐng gǎnkuài bǔqí quēshǎo de cáiliào.; The deadline to submit applications is approaching; please quickly supply the missing materials.

连忙; liánmáng; Promptly / In response​

  • Usage: 连忙 + Verb: Describes an immediate physical or verbal counter-action triggered directly by a preceding event.
  • Example: 看到老教授抱着一叠沉重的书籍，班长连忙上前帮忙。; Kàndào lǎo jiàoshòu bào zhe yì dié chénzhòng de shūjí, bānzhǎng liánmáng shàngqián bāngmáng.; Seeing the old professor carrying a stack of heavy books, the class monitor promptly stepped forward to help.

5. Punctuality, Limits & Availability (时限、准点与临时)​

按时; ànshí; On schedule / According to plan​

  • Usage: 按时 + Verb: Emphasizes compliance with an established timetable, contract, or medication schedule.
  • Example: 建筑工人克服了恶劣天气，按时完成了桥梁加固工程。; Jiànzhù gōngrén kèfú le èliè tiānqì, ànshí wánchéng le qiáoliáng jiāgù gōngchéng.; Overcoming harsh weather, the construction workers completed the bridge reinforcement project on schedule.

准时; zhǔnshí; Punctually / On the dot​

  • Usage: 准时 + Verb / Adj: Highlights precise adherence to an explicit clock-face time; functions as an adverb or stative predicate.
  • Example: 开往哈尔滨的那趟高铁列车准时发车了。; Kāiwǎng Hā'ěrbīn de nà tàng gāotiě lièchē zhǔnshí fāchē le.; That high-speed train bound for Harbin departed right on the dot.

及时; jíshí; In the nick of time / Promptly​

  • Usage: 及时 + Verb / Adj: Marks action executed at the critical opportune moment, preventing damage or solving a crisis.
  • Example: 消防队及时扑灭了大火，没有造成人员伤亡。; Xiāofángduì jíshí pūmiè le dàhuǒ, méiyǒu zàochéng rényuán shāngwáng.; The fire brigade extinguished the blaze in time, preventing any casualties.

尽快; jǐnkuài; As soon as possible​

  • Usage: 尽快 + Verb: Stresses compressing the required window of time to a minimum.
  • Example: 请各位专家尽快审阅这份修改后的科研报告。; Qǐng gèwèi zhuānjiā jǐnkuài shěnyuè zhè fèn xiūgǎi hòu de kēyán bàogào.; Would all experts please review this revised scientific research report as soon as possible.

随时; suíshí; At any time / Whenever​

  • Usage: 随时 + Verb: Expresses that an action or service is continuously standing by under any circumstance.
  • Example: 遇到突发系统故障，值班工程师随时可以提供技术支持。; Yùdào tūfā xìtǒng gùzhàng, zhíbān gōngchéngshī suíshí kěyǐ tígōng jìshù zhīchí.; In the event of an unexpected system failure, the engineer on duty can provide technical support at any time.

暂时; zànshí; Temporarily / For the present​

  • Usage: 暂时 + Verb / Noun modifier: Restricts the operational validity of an action or rule to an interim timeframe.
  • Example: 由于资金周转困难，董事会暂时搁置了扩建工厂的计划。; Yóuyú zījīn zhōuzhuǎn kùnnan, dǒngshìhuì zànshí gēzhì le kuòjiàn gōngchǎng de jìhuà.; Due to cash flow difficulties, the board of directors temporarily shelved the plan to expand the factory.

临时; línshí; At short notice / Provisionally​

  • Usage: 临时 + Verb / Noun modifier: Denotes an arrangement made ad-hoc, off-schedule, or directly before execution.
  • Example: 主讲嘉宾因病缺席，组委会临时推选了一位代表上台发言。; Zhǔjiǎng jiābīn yīn bìng quēxí, zǔwěihuì línshí tuīxuǎn le yí wèi dàibiǎo shàngtái fāyán.; Because the keynote speaker was absent due to illness, the organizing committee nominated a representative at short notice to speak on stage.

6. Temporal Shift, Origins & Outcomes (界限、转折与结局)​

从此; cóngcǐ; From then on / Henceforth​

  • Usage: 从此 + Predicate: Designates a historical incident or agreement as the permanent starting point for a subsequent state.
  • Example: 签下那份长期供货合同后，这家初创工坊从此走上了正轨。; Qiānxià nà fèn chángqī gōnghuò hétong hòu, zhè jiā chūchuàng gōngfāng cóngcǐ zǒushang le zhèngguǐ.; After signing that long-term supply contract, this startup workshop got on the right track from then on.

至今; zhìjīn; Up to now / To this day​

  • Usage: (从……) 至今 + Predicate: Traces an unbroken state or ongoing validity from an origin point up to the speech moment.
  • Example: 那座修建于宋代的木质结构古塔至今保存完好。; Nà zuò xiūjiàn yú Sòngdài de mùzhì jiégòu gǔtǎ zhìjīn bǎocún wánhǎo.; That ancient pagoda with a wooden structure built in the Song Dynasty is well preserved to this day.

迟早; chízǎo; Sooner or later​

  • Usage: 迟早 + Verb / Predicate: Projects inevitable realization regardless of the temporal interval involved.
  • Example: 只要坚持科学严谨的实验方法，研究团队迟早会攻破这个医学难题。; Zhǐyào jiānchí kēxué yánjǐn de shíyàn fāngfǎ, yánjiū tuánduì chízǎo huì gōngpò zhè ge yīxué nántí.; As long as they adhere to scientific and rigorous experimental methods, the research team will sooner or later crack this medical conundrum.

原来; yuánlái; As it turns out / Originally​

  • Usage: 原来 + Predicate: Unveils an underlying fact that was previously unknown or overlooked by the speaker.
  • Example: 查验了那份原始档案，我们才发现原来这两项专利属于同一家发明机构。; Cháyàn le nà fèn yuánshǐ dàng'àn, wǒmen cái fāxiàn yuánlái zhè liǎng xiàng zhuānlì shǔyú tóng yì jiā fāmíng jīgòu.; Having examined that original file, we only then discovered that as it turns out, these two patents belong to the same inventing institution.

终于; zhōngyú; Finally / At last​

  • Usage: 终于 + Verb (+ 了): Marks the eventual realization of a long-awaited, hard-fought milestone.
  • Example: 经过长达数周的艰苦谈判，两家跨国集团终于签署了并购协议。; Jīngguò cháng dá shù zhōu de jiānkǔ tánpàn, liǎng jiā kuàguó jítuán zhōngyú qiānshǔ le bìnggòu xiéyì.; Following weeks of grueling negotiations, the two multinational conglomerates finally signed the merger agreement.

7. Gradual & Stepwise Progression (渐进与接续)​

渐渐; jiànjiàn; Gradually / Imperceptibly​

  • Usage: 渐渐 + Verb / Adj: Depicts slow, natural, organic transformations unfolding over an extended duration.
  • Example: 服用了那剂中草药后，患者胸口的胀痛感渐渐减轻了。; Fúyòng le nà jì zhōngcǎoyào hòu, huànzhě xiōngkǒu de zhàngtònggǎn jiànjiàn jiǎnqīng le.; After taking that dose of Chinese herbal medicine, the patient's feeling of chest pain gradually subsided.

逐渐; zhújiàn; Gradually / Steadily​

  • Usage: 逐渐 + Verb / Adj: Formal equivalent to 渐渐; marks orderly, cumulative shifts in state or trend.
  • Example: 随着一系列稳增长措施的出台，该地区的对外贸易额逐渐回升。; Suízhe yí xìliè wěn zēngzhǎng cuòshī de chūtái, gāi dìqū de duìwài màoyì'é zhújiàn huíshēng.; With the introduction of a series of growth-stabilizing measures, the foreign trade volume of this region gradually picked up.

逐步; zhúbù; Step by step / Systematically​

  • Usage: 逐步 + Multi-syllable Action Verb: Emphasizes deliberate, planned progression carried out phase by phase.
  • Example: 有关部门正在各个试点城市逐步推行垃圾分类制度。; Yǒuguān bùmén zhèngzài gè gè shìdiǎn chéngshì zhúbù tuīxíng lājī fēnlèi zhìdù.; Relevant departments are systematically rolling out the waste sorting system across various pilot cities step by step.

陆续; lùxù; In succession / One after another​

  • Usage: 陆续 + Verb: Denotes discrete, recurring actions executed asynchronously by multiple parties with intermittent gaps.
  • Example: 来自全球的科技代表陆续抵达了本次人工智能峰会的主会场。; Láilì quánqiú de kējì dàibiǎo lùxù dǐdá le běn cì réngōng zhìnéng fēnghuì de zhǔ huìchǎng.; Tech delegates from around the globe arrived at the main venue of this Artificial Intelligence summit one after another.

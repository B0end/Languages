# Personal Pronouns

Mandarin personal pronouns are remarkably regular: they do not decline for grammatical case (there are no distinct forms for *"I / me / my / mine"*), and plurals are consistently formed with the suffix **们 (*men*)**.

---

## 1. Singular Pronouns

### [我; wǒ; I, me](wo3.wav)
* **Usage:** 1st person singular; acts as subject, object, or possessive base.
* **Example:** [这是我的汉语书。; Zhè shì wǒ de Hànyǔ shū.; This is my Chinese book.](wo3_example1.wav)

---

### [你; nǐ; You](ni3.wav)
* **Usage:** 2nd person singular informal.
* **Example:** [你明天想去图书馆吗？; Nǐ míngtiān xiǎng qù túshūguǎn ma?; Do you want to go to the library tomorrow?](ni3_example1.wav)

---

### [您; nín; You (polite)](nin2.wav)
* **Usage:** Respectful 2nd person; elders, teachers, clients. No standard plural form (*您们 is non-standard).
* **Example:** [请问您贵姓？; Qǐngwèn nín guìxìng?; May I ask your honorable surname?](nin2_example1.wav)

---

### [他; tā; He, him](ta1.wav)
* **Usage:** 3rd person masculine singular (or gender unspecified).
* **Example:** [他在医院当医生。; Tā zài yīyuàn dāng yīshēng.; He works as a doctor in a hospital.](ta1_example1.wav)

---

### [她; tā; She, her](ta1.wav)
* **Usage:** 3rd person feminine singular; spoken identically to 他.
* **Example:** [她对中国历史很感兴趣。; Tā duì Zhōngguó lìshǐ hěn gǎn xìngqù.; She is very interested in Chinese history.](ta1_fem_example1.wav)

---

### [它; tā; It](ta1.wav)
* **Usage:** 3rd person neuter; inanimate objects, concepts, animals.
* **Example:** [那只小猫生病了，它不吃东西。; Nà zhī xiǎomāo shēngbìng le, tā bù chī dōngxi.; That kitten is sick; it isn't eating.](ta1_inan_example1.wav)

---

## 2. Plural Pronouns

### [我们; wǒmen; We, us](wo3men0.wav)
* **Usage:** 1st person plural; can be either inclusive or exclusive of the listener.
* **Example:** [我们去吃午饭了，再见！; Wǒmen qù chī wǔfàn le, zàijiàn!; We are going to have lunch, goodbye!](wo3men0_example.wav)

---

### [咱们; zánmen; We, us (inclusive)](zan2men0.wav)
* **Usage:** Northern vernacular; strictly includes both the speaker and the listener.
* **Example:** [咱们一起去吃午饭吧！; Zánmen yìqǐ qù chī wǔfàn ba!; Let’s go have lunch together!](zan2men0_example1.wav)

---

### [你们; nǐmen; You all](ni3men0.wav)
* **Usage:** 2nd person plural.
* **Example:** [你们打算什么时候出发？; Nǐmen dǎsuàn shénme shíhou chūfā?; When do you all plan to set out?](ni3men0_example1.wav)

---

### [他们; tāmen; They, them (male / mixed)](ta1men0.wav)
* **Usage:** 3rd person plural for all-male groups or mixed-gender groups.
* **Example:** [他们正在讨论工作计划。; Tāmen zhèngzài tǎolùn gōngzuò jìhuà.; They are discussing the work plan right now.](ta1men0_example1.wav)

---

### [她们; tāmen; They, them (all-female)](ta1men0.wav)
* **Usage:** 3rd person plural for exclusively female groups.
* **Example:** [她们都是大学同学。; Tāmen dōu shì dàxué tóngxué.; They are all university classmates.](ta1men0_fem_example1.wav)

---

### [它们; tāmen; They, them (inanimate / animals)](ta1men0.wav)
* **Usage:** 3rd person plural for non-human entities.
* **Example:** [这些照片很漂亮，把它们打印出来吧。; Zhèxiē zhàopiàn hěn piàoliang, bǎ tāmen dǎyìn chūlái ba.; These photos are beautiful; print them out.](ta1men0_inan_example1.wav)

---

## 3. Reflexive Pronouns

### [自己; zìjǐ; Oneself](zi4ji3.wav)
* **Usage:** Reflexive pronoun; can follow any personal pronoun or stand alone as subject/adverb.
* **Example:** [这道题你要自己做。; Zhè dào tí nǐ yào zìjǐ zuò.; You need to do this problem by yourself.](zi4ji3_example2.wav)

---

### [本人; běnrén; Myself, in person](ben3ren2.wav)
* **Usage:** Formal/legal self-reference; denotes physical presence or authoritative stance.
* **Example:** [办理护照必须本人亲自去。; Bànlǐ hùzhào bìxū běnrén qīnzì qù.; To apply for a passport, you must go in person.](ben3ren2_example2.wav)

---

### [自身; zìshēn; One's own condition](zi4shen1.wav)
* **Usage:** Abstract reflexive; refers to inherent qualities, internal capacity, or safety. Cannot act as an adverb.
* **Example:** [出国旅行时，首先要保证自身的安全。; Chūguó lǚxíng shí, shǒuxiān yào bǎozhèng zìshēn de ānquán.; When traveling abroad, ensuring one's own safety comes first.](zi4shen1_example1.wav)

---

## 4. Collective, Indefinite & Reciprocal Pronouns

### [大家; dàjiā; Everyone](da4jia1.wav)
* **Usage:** Collective pronoun; represents all individuals in a given group.
* **Example:** [大家都准备好了吗？; Dàjiā dōu zhǔnbèi hǎo le ma?; Is everyone ready?](da4jia1_example1.wav)

---

### [别人; biérén; Others](bie2ren2.wav)
* **Usage:** Indefinite pronoun; refers to other people outside the speaker's context.
* **Example:** [我们要多为别人着想。; Wǒmen yào duō wèi biérén zhuóxiǎng.; We should be more considerate of others.](bie2ren2_example2.wav)

---

### [人家; rénjia; others, me (colloquial)](ren2jia0.wav)
* **Usage:** Dual-function pronoun: (1) Refers to *other people / they* (synonym of 别人); (2) Colloquially and playfully refers to *the speaker themselves* ("me / I"), often conveying intimacy, grievance, or teasing.
* **Example:** [人家都等了你半天了！; Rénjia dōu děng le nǐ bàntiān le!; I've been waiting for you forever!](ren2jia0_example1.wav)

---

### [彼此; bǐcǐ; Each other](bi3ci3.wav)
* **Usage:** Reciprocal pronoun; indicates mutual interaction or shared state between parties.
* **Example:** [真正的朋友应该彼此信任。; Zhēnzhèng de péngyou yīnggāi bǐcǐ xìnrèn.; True friends should trust each other.](bi3ci3_example1.wav)

---

### [各自; gèzì; Each, respective](ge4zi4.wav)
* **Usage:** Distributive pronoun; emphasizes individual actions or attributes within a group.
* **Example:** [会议结束后，大家回到了各自的办公室。; Huìyì jiéshù hòu, dàjiā huí dào le gèzì de bàngōngshì.; After the meeting ended, everyone returned to their respective offices.](ge4zi4_example1.wav)

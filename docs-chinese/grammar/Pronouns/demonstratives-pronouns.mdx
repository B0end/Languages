# Demonstrative Pronouns

Demonstrative pronouns point to, differentiate, or categorize people, objects, locations, manners, degrees, and quantities. 

In spoken and standard Mandarin, basic demonstratives require a measure word to modify a noun (`这 / 那 + MW + Noun`), whereas plural, distributive, and formal demonstratives often attach directly.

---

## 1. Core Proximal & Distal

### [这; zhè; this](zhe4.wav)
* **Usage:** Proximal demonstrative (near speaker). Strictly requires a measure word before nouns (`这 + MW + Noun`). Often spoken as *zhèi*.
* **Example:** [这本书很有意思。; Zhè běn shū hěn yǒu yìsi.; This book is very interesting.](zhe4ben3shu1hen3you3yi4si0.wav)

---

### [那; nà; that](na4.wav)
* **Usage:** Distal demonstrative (away from speaker). Strictly requires a measure word before nouns (`那 + MW + Noun`). Often spoken as *nèi*.
* **Example:** [那是谁的车？; Nà shì shéi de chē?; Whose car is that?](na4shi4shei2de0che1.wav)

---

### [这些; zhèxiē; these](zhe4xie1.wav)
* **Usage:** Proximal plural. Combines 这 with the plural classifier 些 (*xiē*); directly modifies nouns without another measure word.
* **Example:** [这些苹果很甜。; Zhèxiē píngguǒ hěn tián.; These apples are very sweet.](zhe4xie1ping2guo3hen3tian2.wav)

---

### [那些; nàxiē; those](na4xie1.wav)
* **Usage:** Distal plural. Combines 那 with 些 (*xiē*); directly modifies nouns without another measure word.
* **Example:** [那些人是谁？; Nàxiē rén shì shéi?; Who are those people?](na4xie1ren2shi4shei2.wav)

---

## 2. Spatial Locations

### [这儿; zhèr; here](zher4.wav) / [这里; zhèlǐ; here](zhe4li3.wav)
* **Usage:** Locative demonstrative denoting the speaker's current location. 这儿 (*zhèr*) is Northern/colloquial; 这里 (*zhèlǐ*) is standard and Southern.
* **Example:** [我住在这里。; Wǒ zhù zài zhèlǐ.; I live here.](wo3zhu4zai4zhe4li3.wav)

---

### [那儿; nàr; there](nar4.wav) / [那里; nàlǐ; there](na4li3.wav)
* **Usage:** Locative demonstrative denoting a place away from the speaker. 那儿 (*nàr*) is Northern/colloquial; 那里 (*nàlǐ*) is standard and Southern.
* **Example:** [请去那儿等我。; Qǐng qù nàr děng wǒ.; Please go there and wait for me.](qing3qu4nar4deng3wo3.wav)

---

## 3. Manner & Degree

### [这样; zhèyàng; like this, this way](zhe4yang4.wav)
* **Usage:** Indicates manner (`这样 + Verb` = *"do it this way"*) or acts as an attributive/predicate (*"this kind of / like this"*).
* **Example:** [你应该这样做。; Nǐ yīnggāi zhèyàng zuò.; You should do it this way.](ni3ying1gai1zhe4yang4zuo4.wav)

---

### [那样; nàyàng; like that, that way](na4yang4.wav)
* **Usage:** Indicates manner or condition distant from the speaker (`那样 + Verb` = *"do it that way"*).
* **Example:** [不要那样说话。; Bú yào nàyàng shuōhuà.; Don't speak like that.](bu2yao4na4yang4shuo1hua4.wav)

---

### [这么; zhème; so, this much](zhe4me0.wav)
* **Usage:** Modifies adjectives or verbs to indicate a high degree close to the speaker's direct experience (*"so / this [adj]"*).
* **Example:** [今天怎么这么冷？; Jīntiān zěnme zhème lěng?; Why is it so cold today?](jin1tian1zen3me0zhe4me0leng3.wav)

---

### [那么; nàme; so, that much](na4me0.wav)
* **Usage:** Modifies adjectives or verbs to indicate degree (*"so / that [adj]"*). Also functions as a conjunction (*"in that case, then"*).
* **Example:** [那个电影没有那么好看。; Nà ge diànyǐng méiyǒu nàme hǎokàn.; That movie isn't that good.](na4ge0dian4ying3mei2you3na4me0hao3kan4.wav)

---

## 4. Distributive & Indefinite

### [每; měi; each, every](mei3.wav)
* **Usage:** Emphasizes comprehensive inclusion across an entire group. Mandatory classifier pattern: `每 + MW + Noun + 都`.
* **Example:** [每个人都喜欢音乐。; Měi ge rén dōu xǐhuan yīnyuè.; Everyone likes music.](mei3ge0ren2dou1xi3huan1yin1yue4.wav)

---

### [各; gè; each, respective](ge4.wav)
* **Usage:** Emphasizes individual differentiation or distinct categories. Directly precedes nouns without a measure word (`各 + Noun`).
* **Example:** [世界各国都有自己的文化。; Shìjiè gè guó dōu yǒu zìjǐ de wénhuà.; Every country in the world has its own culture.](shi4jie4ge4guo2dou1you3zi4ji3de0wen2hua4.wav)

---

### [各自; gèzì; each, respective](ge4zi4.wav)
* **Usage:** Emphasizes that individuals within a group act independently (`各自 + Verb`) or possess separate items (`各自的 + Noun`).
* **Example:** [会议结束后，大家回到了各自的办公室。; Huìyì jiéshù hòu, dàjiā huí dào le gèzì de bàngōngshì.; After the meeting ended, everyone returned to their respective offices.](ge4zi4_example1.wav)

---

### [有的; yǒude; some](you3de0.wav) / [有些; yǒuxiē; some](you3xie1.wav)
* **Usage:** Distributive/indefinite demonstrative used to single out a portion of a group. Commonly used in parallel structures (`有的……，有的……`).
* **Example:** [有的人喜欢喝茶，有的人喜欢喝咖啡。; Yǒude rén xǐhuan hē chá, yǒude rén xǐhuan hē kāfēi.; Some people like to drink tea; some like to drink coffee.](you3de0ren2xi3huan1he1cha2you3de0ren2xi3huan1he1ka1fei1.wav)

---

## 5. Alternative & Remainder

### [其他; qítā; other, else](qi2ta1.wav)
* **Usage:** Points to other people, things, or matters outside the immediate reference (`其他 (+ 的) + Noun` or stands alone).
* **Example:** [除了这个，你还有其他问题吗？; Chúle zhège, nǐ hái yǒu qítā wèntí ma?; Besides this one, do you have any other questions?](chu2le0zhe4ge0ni3hai2you3qi2ta1wen4ti2ma0.wav)

---

### [其余; qíyú; the rest, remainder](qi2yu2.wav)
* **Usage:** Designates the specific remaining subset of a previously defined group (`其余的 + Noun` or stands alone).
* **Example:** [三个人去了图书馆，其余的人留在教室。; Sān gè rén qù le túshūguǎn, qíyú de rén liú zài jiàoshì.; Three people went to the library; the rest of the people stayed in the classroom.](san1ge4ren2qu4le0tu2shu1guan3qi2yu2de0ren2liu2zai4jiao4shi4.wav)

---

### [其; qí; its, their, that (formal)](qi2.wav)
* **Usage:** Formal 3rd-person possessive (*"its / his / their"*) or distal demonstrative (*"that / this"*). Essential root in fixed compounds (`其中`, `其实`, `其内容`).
* **Example:** [这个方案很好，但其成本太高。; Zhè ge fāng'àn hěn hǎo, dàn qí chéngběn tài gāo.; This proposal is very good, but its cost is too high.](zhe4ge0fang1an4hen3hao3dan4qi2cheng2ben3tai4gao1.wav)

---

## 6. Universal, Indefinite & Formal

### [一切; yíqiè; all, everything](yi2qie4.wav)
* **Usage:** Universal demonstrative referring to *all things* or *everything* within a domain. Functions as a noun or modifies abstract nouns.
* **Example:** [祝你一切顺利！; Zhù nǐ yíqiè shùnlì!; Wishing you all the best / that everything goes smoothly!](zhu4ni3yi2qie4shun4li4.wav)

---

### [任何; rènhé; any, whatever](ren4he2.wav)
* **Usage:** Indicates absolute open inclusion with zero exceptions (`任何 (+ MW) + Noun + 都 / 也`).
* **Example:** [任何困难都不能阻止我们。; Rènhé kùnnan dōu bù néng zǔzhǐ wǒmen.; No difficulty whatsoever can stop us.](ren4he2kun4nan0dou1bu4neng2zu3zhi3wo3men0.wav)

---

### [某; mǒu; a certain, some](mou3.wav)
* **Usage:** Indefinite pronoun used when an entity is known to the speaker but left unnamed or non-specific to the listener (`某 + Noun` or `某 + MW + Noun`).
* **Example:** [某人把手机忘在桌子上了。; Mǒurén bǎ shǒujī wàng zài zhuōzi shang le.; A certain person left their mobile phone on the table.](mou3ren2ba3shou3ji1wang4zai4zhuo1zi0shang0le0.wav)

---

### [此; cǐ; this, here (formal)](ci3.wav)
* **Usage:** Literary/formal equivalent to 这 (*zhè*) or 这里 (*zhèlǐ*). Standard in official notices, legal contracts, and fixed compounds (此时, 此地, 此外, 因此).
* **Example:** [请在此处签名。; Qǐng zài cǐ chù qiānmíng.; Please sign your name here.](qing3zai4ci3chu4qian1ming2.wav)

---

### [本; běn; this, current (formal)](ben3.wav)
* **Usage:** Formal/business proximal demonstrative attached directly to nouns (`本 + Noun`) to denote *"this / our / the current one"*, establishing institutional or direct proximity.
* **Example:** [本公司明天放假。; Běn gōngsī míngtiān fàngjià.; Our / This company is on holiday tomorrow.](ben3gong1si1ming2tian1fang4jia4.wav)

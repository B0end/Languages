Interrogative Pronouns

Mandarin questions use in-situ word order: the sentence structure remains identical to a declarative statement—simply replace the unknown information slot with the interrogative pronoun without auxiliary verbs or inversion.

Never append the yes/no question particle 吗 (ma) to a sentence that already contains an interrogative pronoun.

1. Identity & Things​

什么; shénme; what​

  • Usage: Replaces direct objects or subjects. Can directly precede and modify a noun (什么 + Noun = "what kind of...").
  • Example: 你在看什么？; Nǐ zài kàn shénme?; What are you looking at?

谁; shéi; who, whom​

  • Usage: Replaces a human subject or object. Add 的 (de) to form the possessive "whose" (谁的). Also pronounced shuí.
  • Example: 这是谁的钥匙？; Zhè shì shéi de yàoshi?; Whose keys are these?

哪; nǎ; which​

  • Usage: Selective pronoun used to choose among options. Strictly requires a classifier/measure word (哪 + MW + Noun).
  • Example: 哪本书是你的？; Nǎ běn shū shì nǐ de?; Which book is yours?

哪些; nǎxiē; which ones​

  • Usage: Plural interrogative pronoun. Combines 哪 with the plural classifier 些 (xiē) to query multiple items or people from a group (哪些 + Noun).
  • Example: 你想借哪些书？; Nǐ xiǎng jiè nǎxiē shū?; Which books do you want to borrow?

2. Location & Time​

哪儿; nǎr; where / 哪里; nǎlǐ; where​

  • Usage: Replaces locational phrases or destinations. 哪儿 (nǎr) is Northern/colloquial; 哪里 (nǎlǐ) is Southern/formal standard.
  • Example: 你去哪儿？; Nǐ qù nǎr?; Where are you going?

什么时候; shénme shíhou; when​

  • Usage: Inquires about broad temporal frames (year, date, season, part of day). Placed before or after the subject.
  • Example: 你什么时候来？; Nǐ shénme shíhou lái?; When are you coming?

几点; jǐ diǎn; what time​

  • Usage: Inquires specifically about clock-face time (几 + 点 = "what hour / what o'clock").
  • Example: 我们几点见面？; Wǒmen jǐ diǎn jiànmiàn?; What time do we meet?

3. Quantity, Degree & Extent​

几; jǐ; how many (< 10)​

  • Usage: Inquires about small countable quantities (typically under 10). A measure word is strictly mandatory (几 + MW + Noun).
  • Example: 你有几个苹果？; Nǐ yǒu jǐ ge píngguǒ?; How many apples do you have?

多少; duōshao; how much, how many​

  • Usage: Inquires about large or unbounded amounts, telephone digits, and prices. The measure word is optional.
  • Example: 这个手机多少钱？; Zhè ge shǒujī duōshao qián?; How much is this mobile phone?

多; duō; how [adjective]​

  • Usage: Placed before scalar adjectives (多 + Adj) to inquire about degree, age, size, distance, height, or duration (e.g., 多大, 多高, 多远, 多长, 多久).
  • Example: 你学中文多长时间了？; Nǐ xué Zhōngwén duō cháng shíjiān le?; How long have you been studying Chinese?

4. Reason, Manner & State​

为什么; wèishénme; why​

  • Usage: Inquires about purpose, cause, or motivation. Standardly answered leading with 因为 (yīnwèi, "because").
  • Example: 你为什么学中文？; Nǐ wèishénme xué Zhōngwén?; Why do you study Chinese?

干嘛; gànmá; why, what for​

  • Usage: Colloquial/spoken interrogative asking "what are you doing?" or "why / what for?", often carrying an informal, surprised, or impatient tone (你干嘛……？).
  • Example: 你干嘛这么生气？; Nǐ gànmá zhème shēngqì?; Why are you so angry?

怎么; zěnme; how, how come​

  • Usage: Combined with verbs (怎么 + Verb) to ask for method/manner ("how to"), or predicates to express surprise/cause ("how come / why").
  • Example: 这个字怎么读？; Zhè ge zì zěnme dú?; How do you pronounce this character?

怎么了; zěnme le; what's wrong​

  • Usage: Standalone colloquial expression used to inquire about a problem, sudden situation, or emotional distress.
  • Example: 你怎么了？; Nǐ zěnme le?; What's wrong with you?

怎么样; zěnmeyàng; how is it, how about​

  • Usage: Placed after a topic (Topic + 怎么样？) to request an evaluation, state of health/weather, or opinion on a suggestion.
  • Example: 今天天气怎么样？; Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?; How is the weather today?

如何; rúhé; how, in what way​

  • Usage: Formal/written counterpart to both 怎么 (method: 如何 + Verb) and 怎么样 (state: Topic + 如何？).
  • Example: 这个问题应该如何解决？; Zhè ge wèntí yīnggāi rúhé jiějué?; How should this problem be resolved?

何; hé; what, why, how (formal)​

  • Usage: Classical/literary universal interrogative root used in formal writing. Forms fixed terms such as 何时 (when), 何地 (where), 为何 (why), and 何人 (who).
  • Example: 会议将于何时举行？; Huìyì jiāng yú héshí jǔxíng?; When will the meeting be held?

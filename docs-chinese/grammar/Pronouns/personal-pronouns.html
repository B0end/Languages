Personal Pronouns

Mandarin personal pronouns are remarkably regular: they do not decline for grammatical case (there are no distinct forms for "I / me / my / mine"), and plurals are consistently formed with the suffix 们 (men).

1. Singular Pronouns​

我; wǒ; I, me​

  • Usage: 1st person singular; acts as subject, object, or possessive base.
  • Example: 这是我的汉语书。; Zhè shì wǒ de Hànyǔ shū.; This is my Chinese book.

你; nǐ; You​

  • Usage: 2nd person singular informal.
  • Example: 你明天想去图书馆吗？; Nǐ míngtiān xiǎng qù túshūguǎn ma?; Do you want to go to the library tomorrow?

您; nín; You (polite)​

  • Usage: Respectful 2nd person; elders, teachers, clients. No standard plural form (*您们 is non-standard).
  • Example: 请问您贵姓？; Qǐngwèn nín guìxìng?; May I ask your honorable surname?

他; tā; He, him​

  • Usage: 3rd person masculine singular (or gender unspecified).
  • Example: 他在医院当医生。; Tā zài yīyuàn dāng yīshēng.; He works as a doctor in a hospital.

她; tā; She, her​

  • Usage: 3rd person feminine singular; spoken identically to 他.
  • Example: 她对中国历史很感兴趣。; Tā duì Zhōngguó lìshǐ hěn gǎn xìngqù.; She is very interested in Chinese history.

它; tā; It​

  • Usage: 3rd person neuter; inanimate objects, concepts, animals.
  • Example: 那只小猫生病了，它不吃东西。; Nà zhī xiǎomāo shēngbìng le, tā bù chī dōngxi.; That kitten is sick; it isn't eating.

2. Plural Pronouns​

我们; wǒmen; We, us​

  • Usage: 1st person plural; can be either inclusive or exclusive of the listener.
  • Example: 我们去吃午饭了，再见！; Wǒmen qù chī wǔfàn le, zàijiàn!; We are going to have lunch, goodbye!

咱们; zánmen; We, us (inclusive)​

  • Usage: Northern vernacular; strictly includes both the speaker and the listener.
  • Example: 咱们一起去吃午饭吧！; Zánmen yìqǐ qù chī wǔfàn ba!; Let’s go have lunch together!

你们; nǐmen; You all​

  • Usage: 2nd person plural.
  • Example: 你们打算什么时候出发？; Nǐmen dǎsuàn shénme shíhou chūfā?; When do you all plan to set out?

他们; tāmen; They, them (male / mixed)​

  • Usage: 3rd person plural for all-male groups or mixed-gender groups.
  • Example: 他们正在讨论工作计划。; Tāmen zhèngzài tǎolùn gōngzuò jìhuà.; They are discussing the work plan right now.

她们; tāmen; They, them (all-female)​

  • Usage: 3rd person plural for exclusively female groups.
  • Example: 她们都是大学同学。; Tāmen dōu shì dàxué tóngxué.; They are all university classmates.

它们; tāmen; They, them (inanimate / animals)​

  • Usage: 3rd person plural for non-human entities.
  • Example: 这些照片很漂亮，把它们打印出来吧。; Zhèxiē zhàopiàn hěn piàoliang, bǎ tāmen dǎyìn chūlái ba.; These photos are beautiful; print them out.

3. Reflexive Pronouns​

自己; zìjǐ; Oneself​

  • Usage: Reflexive pronoun; can follow any personal pronoun or stand alone as subject/adverb.
  • Example: 这道题你要自己做。; Zhè dào tí nǐ yào zìjǐ zuò.; You need to do this problem by yourself.

本人; běnrén; Myself, in person​

  • Usage: Formal/legal self-reference; denotes physical presence or authoritative stance.
  • Example: 办理护照必须本人亲自去。; Bànlǐ hùzhào bìxū běnrén qīnzì qù.; To apply for a passport, you must go in person.

自身; zìshēn; One's own condition​

  • Usage: Abstract reflexive; refers to inherent qualities, internal capacity, or safety. Cannot act as an adverb.
  • Example: 出国旅行时，首先要保证自身的安全。; Chūguó lǚxíng shí, shǒuxiān yào bǎozhèng zìshēn de ānquán.; When traveling abroad, ensuring one's own safety comes first.

4. Collective, Indefinite & Reciprocal Pronouns​

大家; dàjiā; Everyone​

  • Usage: Collective pronoun; represents all individuals in a given group.
  • Example: 大家都准备好了吗？; Dàjiā dōu zhǔnbèi hǎo le ma?; Is everyone ready?

别人; biérén; Others​

  • Usage: Indefinite pronoun; refers to other people outside the speaker's context.
  • Example: 我们要多为别人着想。; Wǒmen yào duō wèi biérén zhuóxiǎng.; We should be more considerate of others.

人家; rénjia; others, me (colloquial)​

  • Usage: Dual-function pronoun: (1) Refers to other people / they (synonym of 别人); (2) Colloquially and playfully refers to the speaker themselves ("me / I"), often conveying intimacy, grievance, or teasing.
  • Example: 人家都等了你半天了！; Rénjia dōu děng le nǐ bàntiān le!; I've been waiting for you forever!

彼此; bǐcǐ; Each other​

  • Usage: Reciprocal pronoun; indicates mutual interaction or shared state between parties.
  • Example: 真正的朋友应该彼此信任。; Zhēnzhèng de péngyou yīnggāi bǐcǐ xìnrèn.; True friends should trust each other.

各自; gèzì; Each, respective​

  • Usage: Distributive pronoun; emphasizes individual actions or attributes within a group.
  • Example: 会议结束后，大家回到了各自的办公室。; Huìyì jiéshù hòu, dàjiā huí dào le gèzì de bàngōngshì.; After the meeting ended, everyone returned to their respective offices.

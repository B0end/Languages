# Directional Words

Directional words (方位词) function as locative nouns that attach immediately after reference nouns (`Noun + Direction`) without prepositions to create locative phrases, or stand independently as subjects, objects, and attributive modifiers (`Direction + 的 + Noun`).

## Vertical Spatial Relations (上下)

### [上; shàng; On / Above / Up](shang4.wav)
* **Usage:** Bound postposition attached directly to nouns (`Noun + 上`) indicating physical surface contact or abstract scope.
* **Example:** [那一本词典在这一张桌子上。; Nà yī běn cídiǎn zài zhè yī zhāng zhuōzi shàng.; That dictionary is on this desk.](na4yi4ben3ci2dian3zai4zhe4yi4zhang1zhuo1zi0shang4.wav)

---

### [上面; shàngmiàn; Top / Above / Surface](shang4mian4.wav)
* **Usage:** Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 上边 (shàngbiān) and colloquial 上头 (shàngtou). Slots as subject, object, or attributive modifier.
* **Example:** [上面那一层货架上摆着三瓶红酒。; Shàngmiàn nà yī céng huòjià shàng bǎizhe sān píng hóngjiǔ.; Three bottles of red wine are arranged on that upper shelf.](shang4mian4na4yi4ceng2huo4jia4shang4bai3zhe0san1ping2hong2jiu3.wav)

---

### [下; xià; Under / Below / Beneath](xia4.wav)
* **Usage:** Bound postposition affixed directly to nouns (`Noun + 下`) signifying physical position underneath or an environmental condition.
* **Example:** [两只小猫躲在这一棵大树下乘凉。; Liǎng zhī xiǎomāo duǒ zài zhè yī kē dàshù xià chéngliáng.; Two kittens are hiding under this big tree to enjoy the shade.](liang3zhi1xiao3mao1duo3zai4zhe4yi4ke1da4shu4xia4cheng2liang2.wav)

---

### [下面; xiàmiàn; Below / Bottom / Underneath](xia4mian4.wav)
* **Usage:** Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 下边 (xiàbiān) and colloquial 下头 (xiàtou). Functions as subject, object, or attributive modifier.
* **Example:** [这一把椅子下面放着一只皮箱。; Zhè yī bǎ yǐzi xiàmiàn fàngzhe yī zhī píxiāng.; A leather suitcase is placed underneath this chair.](zhe4yi4ba3yi3zi0xia4mian4fang4zhe0yi1zhi1pi2xiang1.wav)

## Sagittal Spatial Relations (前后)

### [前; qián; Front / Before](qian2.wav)
* **Usage:** Bound postposition affixed directly to nouns (`Noun + 前`) indicating spatial position facing outward or temporal precedence.
* **Example:** [门前停着一辆崭新的自行车。; Mén qián tíngzhe yī liàng zhǎnxīn de zìxíngchē.; A brand-new bicycle is parked in front of the door.](men2qian2ting2zhe0yi1liang4zhan3xin1de0zi4xing2che1.wav)

---

### [前面; qiánmiàn; In front / Ahead](qian2mian4.wav)
* **Usage:** Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 前边 (qiánbiān) and spoken 前头 (qiántou). Slots as subject, object, or directional modifier.
* **Example:** [那一所学校前面有一家大型超市。; Nà yī suǒ xuéxiào qiánmiàn yǒu yī jiā dàxíng chāoshì.; There is a large supermarket in front of that school.](na4yi4suo3xue2xiao4qian2mian4you3yi4jia1da4xing2chao1shi4.wav)

---

### [后; hòu; Behind / Back / After](hou4.wav)
* **Usage:** Bound postposition affixed directly to nouns (`Noun + 后`) indicating physical position at the rear or temporal succession.
* **Example:** [房后有一片郁郁葱葱的竹林。; Fáng hòu yǒu yī piàn yùyùcōngcōng de zhúlín.; There is a lush bamboo grove behind the house.](fang2hou4you3yi1pian4yu4yu4cong1cong1de0zhu2lin2.wav)

---

### [后面; hòumiàn; Behind / Rear / Back](hou4mian4.wav)
* **Usage:** Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 后边 (hòubiān) and colloquial 后头 (hòutou). Functions as subject, object, or modifier.
* **Example:** [后面那一辆黑色轿车开得太快了。; Hòumiàn nà yī liàng hēisè jiàochē kāi de tài kuài le.; That black sedan behind is driving too fast.](hou4mian4na4yi4liang4hei1se4jiao4che1kai1de0tai4kuai4le0.wav)

## Lateral Spatial Relations (左右)

### [左边; zuǒbiān; Left side](zuo3bian1.wav)
* **Usage:** Independent locative noun; interchangeable with 左面 (zuǒmiàn). Expresses position to the left of a focal reference point (`Noun + 左边`).
* **Example:** [坐在我左边的那一位医生是李大夫。; Zuò zài wǒ zuǒbiān de nà yī wèi yīshēng shì Lǐ dàifu.; The doctor sitting on my left side is Dr. Li.](zuo4zai4wo3zuo3bian1de0na4yi2wei4yi1sheng1shi4li3dai4fu0.wav)

---

### [右边; yòubiān; Right side](you4bian1.wav)
* **Usage:** Independent locative noun; interchangeable with 右面 (yòumiàn). Expresses position to the right of a focal reference point (`Noun + 右边`).
* **Example:** [那一座红色的邮局就在图书馆右边。; Nà yī zuò hóngsè de yóujú jiù zài túshūguǎn yòubiān.; That red post office is right on the right side of the library.](na4yi1zuo4hong2se4de0you2ju2jiu4zai4tu2shu1guan3you4bian1.wav)

## Interior and Exterior Spatial Relations (内外)

### [里; lǐ; In / Inside](li3.wav)
* **Usage:** Bound postposition attached to common nouns (`Noun + 里`) to create a locative; never attaches directly to proper geographic country or city names.
* **Example:** [这一部手机在她的手提包里。; Zhè yī bù shǒujī zài tā de shǒutíbāo lǐ.; This mobile phone is inside her handbag.](zhe4yi4bu4shou3ji1zai4ta1de0shou3ti2bao1li3.wav)

---

### [里面; lǐmiàn; Inside / Interior](li3mian4.wav)
* **Usage:** Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 里边 (lǐbiān) and colloquial 里头 (lǐtou). Operates independently as subject, object, or modifier.
* **Example:** [这一间会议室里面摆着二十把新椅子。; Zhè yī jiān huìyìshì lǐmiàn bǎizhe èrshí bǎ xīn yǐzi.; Twenty new chairs are arranged inside this conference room.](zhe4yi4jian1hui4yi4shi4li3mian4bai3zhe0er4shi2ba3xin1yi3zi0.wav)

---

### [外; wài; Outside / Beyond](wai4.wav)
* **Usage:** Bound postposition affixed to nouns (`Noun + 外`) designating space exterior to an established boundary or limit.
* **Example:** [窗外正下着一场倾盆大雨。; Chuāng wài zhèng xiàzhe yī cháng qīngpén dàyǔ.; A torrential downpour is falling outside the window.](chuang1wai4zheng4xia4zhe0yi4chang2qing1pen2da4yu3.wav)

---

### [外面; wàimiàn; Outside / Exterior](wai4mian4.wav)
* **Usage:** Standalone locative noun; interchangeable with 外边 (wàibiān) and colloquial 外头 (wàitou). Operates as subject, object, or modifier.
* **Example:** [外面停着两辆空载的出租车。; Wàimiàn tíngzhe liǎng liàng kōngzài de chūzūchē.; Two vacant taxis are parked outside.](wai4mian4ting2zhe0liang3liang4kong1zai4de0chu1zu1che1.wav)

## Proximity and Relative Spatial Relations (中·旁·对面)

### [中间; zhōngjiān; Middle / Between / Center](zhong1jian1.wav)
* **Usage:** Locative noun designating the intermediate interval between two coordinates (`A 和 B 中间`) or the interior center of a defined perimeter.
* **Example:** [在两座大厦中间有一条狭窄的小巷。; Zài liǎng zuò dàshà zhōngjiān yǒu yī tiáo xiázhǎi de xiǎoxiàng.; There is a narrow alley between the two skyscrapers.](zai4liang3zuo4da4sha4zhong1jian1you3yi4tiao2xia2zhai3de0xiao3xiang4.wav)

---

### [旁边; pángbiān; Beside / Side / Next to](pang2bian1.wav)
* **Usage:** Locative noun designating immediate lateral adjacency (`Noun + 旁边`).
* **Example:** [那一栋教学楼旁边有一片宽阔的操场。; Nà yī dòng jiàoxuélóu pángbiān yǒu yī piàn kuānkuò de cāochǎng.; Beside that classroom building is an expansive sports field.](na4yi1dong4jiao4xue2lou2pang2bian1you3yi1pian4kuan1kuo4de0cao1chang3.wav)

---

### [对面; duìmiàn; Opposite / Across from](dui4mian4.wav)
* **Usage:** Locative noun designating an opposing location facing the reference entity directly across a dividing axis (`Noun + 对面`).
* **Example:** [那一家银行对面新开了一家花店。; Nà yī jiā yínháng duìmiàn xīn kāi le yī jiā huādiàn.; A new flower shop opened across from that bank.](na4yi4jia1yin2hang2dui4mian4xin1kai1le0yi4jia1hua1dian4.wav)

## Environmental Scope and Vicinity (周围·附近)

### [周围; zhōuwéi; Surroundings / Perimeter / Around](zhou1wei2.wav)
* **Usage:** Locative noun denoting the circumambient boundary or peripheral area encircling a core target (`Noun + 周围`).
* **Example:** [那一座水库周围栽种了数千棵柳树。; Nà yī zuò shuǐkù zhōuwéi zāizhòng le shùqiān kē liǔshù.; Thousands of willow trees are planted around the perimeter of that reservoir.](na4yi1zuo4shui3ku4zhou1wei2zai1zhong4le0shu4qian1ke1liu3shu4.wav)

---

### [附近; fùjìn; Vicinity / Nearby area](fu4jin4.wav)
* **Usage:** Locative noun denoting the immediate geographic area adjacent to a landmark (`Noun + 附近`), or functioning independently as a spatial noun.
* **Example:** [火车站附近有三家二十四小时营业的药店。; Huǒchēzhàn fùjìn yǒu sān jiā èrshísì xiǎoshí yíngyè de yàodiàn.; There are three 24-hour pharmacies in the vicinity of the railway station.](huo3che1zhan4fu4jin4you3san1jia1er4shi2si4xiao3shi2ying2ye4de0yao4dian4.wav)

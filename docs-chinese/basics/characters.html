Characters

Chinese characters are not random scribbles. Over 95% of everyday characters are built logically from meaningful categories (Radicals) and pronunciation hints (Phonetic components).

1. The Anatomy: Meaning + Sound​

Over 80% of all Chinese characters are "Phono-Semantic Compounds." They function like Lego blocks assembled from two distinct parts:

Character = Radical (Meaning/Category) + Phonetic Component (Sound Hint)

The Famous "Horse" (马 - mǎ) Family​

Notice how the pronunciation remains anchored to ma, while the radical on the left dictates the meaning:

CharacterRadicalRadical MeaningPhonetic ComponentMeaning
妈 (mā)女Woman / Female马 (mǎ)Mother
吗 (ma)口Mouth / Speech马 (mǎ)Question particle
骂 (mà)吅Double mouth (arguing)马 (mǎ)To scold

tip

When you memorize the most common Radicals, you can instantly guess the general theme of thousands of unfamiliar characters at first glance.

2. Strokes in the Digital Age​

You do not need to spend hundreds of hours learning to handwrite thousands of characters from memory. In the modern world, you only need to understand stroke mechanics for one vital reason: using your phone's handwriting keyboard to look up characters you cannot pronounce.

Rule 1: The 5 Fundamental Strokes (What Touchscreens Track)​

Every character in Chinese is written using combinations of just 5 basic stroke trajectories:

StrokeChinese NameDirection & MovementExample Character
一横 (héng)Horizontal line (Left ➔ Right)一 (yī – one)
丨竖 (shù)Vertical line (Top ➔ Bottom)十 (shí – ten)
丿撇 (piě)Left falling slash (Top-Right ➔ Bottom-Left)八 (bā – eight)
丶 / ㇏捺 (nà)Right falling slash / dot (Top-Left ➔ Bottom-Right)人 (rén – person)
𠃍 / 𠃊 / 乙折 (zhé)Any stroke that turns a corner口 (kǒu – mouth)

Rule 2: The Flow of Gravity (Stroke Order for Phone Input)​

Smartphone handwriting recognition relies on the sequence of your strokes. Follow these 3 intuitive rules:

  1. Top to Bottom:
    Write upper elements before lower elements.
    Example: 三 (sān – three) ➔ Top line, middle line, bottom line.

  2. Left to Right:
    Write left-hand components before right-hand components.
    Example: 好 (hǎo – good) ➔ Write 女 on the left first, then 子 on the right.

  3. Frame Outside, Fill Inside, Seal Bottom Last:
    Build the outer box, place the interior content, then close the door.
    Example: 国 (guó – country) ➔ Draw outer frame walls (冂), write 玉 inside, seal the bottom line (一).

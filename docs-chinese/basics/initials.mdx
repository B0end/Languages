# Initials

In Mandarin, every syllable begins with an **Initial** (a consonant) or a **Zero-Initial** (a glide marker).

There are **21 standard consonants** plus **2 semi-vowel glides (`y` and `w`)**, making 23 in total.

---

## Group 1: `M, F, N, L`

These sounds exist naturally in both English and Spanish with identical mouth positions.

| Initial | Sound Match | Hanzi / <br /> Pinyin| Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **m** | *mamá* / *mom* | [妈 mā](ma1.wav) | Mom |
| **f** | *foco* / *fish* | [发 fā](fa1.wav) | Hair |
| **n** | *no* / *nest* | [拿 ná](na2.wav) | To take |
| **l** | *luna* / *love* | [拉 lā](la1.wav) | To pull |

## Group 2: `B/P, D/T, G/K, H`

- Mandarin does **not** distinguish consonants by voicing (vocal cord vibr /ation).
  - Instead, it contrasts **Unaspirated (No Air)** vs. **Aspirated (Huge Puff of Air)**.
* **Unaspirated (`b`, `d`, `g`):** Like Spanish clean, dry consonants (*p, t, k* in *papá, taco, casa*). No puff of air!
* **Aspirated (`p`, `t`, `k`):** Like English br /eathy consonants (*pool, tea, king*). Strong puff of air!

> 💡 **The Tissue Paper Test:**  
> Hold a tissue 5 cm in front of your lips and speak:
> * **Unaspirated (`b`, `d`, `g`, `z`, `zh`, `j`):** The tissue must **remain completely still**.
> * **Aspirated (`p`, `t`, `k`, `c`, `ch`, `q`):** The tissue must **fly forward aggressively**.

### Plosives (Unaspirated vs. Aspirated)

| Unaspirated <br /> *(No Air)* | Hanzi / <br /> Pinyin | Meaning | Aspirated <br /> *(Puff of Air)* | Hanzi / <br /> Pinyin | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **b** | [八 bā](ba1.wav) | Eight | **p** | [趴 pā](pa1.wav) | Lie on stomach |
| **d** | [搭 dā](da1.wav) | Build, board | **t** | [他 tā](ta1.wav) | He, him |
| **g** | [哥 gē](ge1.wav) | Older brother | **k** | [科 kē](ke1.wav) | Science, subject |

### Fricative (Continuous Airflow)

| Initial | Hanzi / <br /> Pinyin | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **h** | [喝 hē](he1.wav) | To drink |

## Group 3: `Z, C, S`

* **`z` (Unaspirated Affricate — No Air)**
  * Similar to the ***ds*** in *ki**ds***, but **completely dry**—no vocal cord vibration and **zero puff of air**.
* **`c` (Aspirated Affricate — Strong Puff)**
  * Similar to the ***ts*** in *ca**ts***, but with an intentional, forceful push of breath.
* **`s` (Fricative — Continuous Airflow)**
  * Identical to the standard English **s** sound in ***s**un*.

:::warning
Crucial Note on the Vowel *-i* (`zi`, `ci`, `si`)

The **-i** here does **not** sound like "ee". 

It is a **"buzzing vowel"**: after you produce the consonant, **do not move your tongue at all**.
:::

### Affricates (Unaspirated vs. Aspirated)

| Vowel Form | Unaspirated <br /> (**z**) | Meaning | Aspirated <br /> (**c**) | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Base Sound (*-i*)** | [资 zī](zi1.wav) | Capital, resources | [疵 cī](ci1.wav) | Flaw, defect |
| **Other Vowel (*-a*)** | [杂 zá](za2.wav) | Mixed | [擦 cā](ca1.wav) | To wipe |

### Fricative (Continuous Airflow)

| Initial | Base Sound (*-i*) | Meaning | Other Vowel (*-a*) | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **s** | [思 sī](si1.wav) | To think | [撒 sā](sa1.wav) | To scatter |

## Group 4: `J, Q, X`

:::tip
**The Technique:** Smile widely! Place the tip of your tongue firmly behind your **lower front teeth** and arch the **flat middle (blade)** of your tongue up toward the hard roof of your mouth.  
:::

:::note
**Golden Rule:** **`j`**, **`q`**, and **`x`** only pair with **`i`** and **`ü`**. When combined with **`ü`**, the umlaut dots are dropped in spelling (written as **`u`**), but the sound is still strictly **`ü`**.
:::

### Affricates (Unaspirated vs. Aspirated)

| Vowel Form | Unaspirated <br /> (**j**) | Meaning | Aspirated <br /> (**q**) | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Vowel with *-i*** | [鸡 jī](ji1.wav) | Chicken | [七 qī](qi1.wav) | Seven |
| **Vowel with *-ü*** <br /> *(written as "u")* | [句 jù](ju4.wav) | Sentence | [去 qù](qu4.wav) | To go |

### Fricative (Continuous Airflow)

| Initial | Vowel with *-i* | Meaning | Vowel with *-ü* <br /> *(written as "u")* | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **x** | [西 xī](xi1.wav) | West | [需 xū](xu1.wav) | To need |

## Group 5: `ZH, CH, SH, R`

:::tip
**The Technique:** Curl the tip of your tongue upward and backward toward the hard palate (roof of your mouth) without pressing it completely flat against it.  
:::

:::note
**Phonetic Note:** When paired with **`-i`**, do **not** pronounce an English "ee". Keep your tongue curled in the exact same position to produce a muffled, buzzing retroflex vowel.
:::

### Affricates (Unaspirated vs. Aspirated)

| Vowel Form | Unaspirated <br /> (**zh**) | Meaning | Aspirated <br /> (**ch**) | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Retroflex Vowel (*-i*)** | [知 zhī](zhi1.wav) | To know | [吃 chī](chi1.wav) | To eat |
| **Back Vowel (*-u*)** | [猪 zhū](zhu1.wav) | Pig | [初 chū](chu1.wav) | Beginning |

### Fricatives / Approximant (Continuous Airflow)

| Initial | Type | Retroflex Vowel (*-i*) | Meaning | Back Vowel (*-u*) | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **sh** | Voiceless | [诗 shī](shi1.wav) | Poem | [书 shū](shu1.wav) | Book |
| **r** | Voiced | [日 rì](ri4.wav) | Sun, day | [如 rú](ru2.wav) | As if, like |

## Group 6: `Y` and `W` (Semi-Vowel Glides / Zero-Initials)

> In Pinyin, a standard syllable **cannot officially begin with the letters *i*, *u*, or *ü***. When a syllable has no consonant initial, **`y`** and **`w`** are used as visual placeholders.

> 🧠 **The Two Golden Rules:**
> 1. **Add `y` / `w`:** Used for standalone single vowels or finals ending in nasal consonants (*-n / -ng*).
> 2. **Swap to `y` / `w`:** Used for compound finals starting with another vowel (`i ➔ y`, `u ➔ w`, `ü ➔ yu`).

---

### 1. The "i" Family ➔ Spelled with `y`

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |

| | **in** | [阴 yīn](yin1.wav) | Cloudy, shade |
| | **ing** | [鹰 yīng](ying1.wav) | Eagle |
| **Swap `i ➔ y`** <br />*(Compound finals)* | **ia** | [鸭 yā](ya1.wav) | Duck |
| | **iao** | [药 yào](yao4.wav) | Medicine |
| | **ie** | [叶 yè](ye4.wav) | Leaf |

## The "Buzzing / Apical" `I`

> 🧠 **The Golden Rule:** The letter **`i`** in Mandarin is strictly binary:
> * **7 syllables** produce the **Buzzing / Apical vowel** (only after *z, c, s, zh, ch, sh, r*). The tongue freezes in the consonant's position while your vocal cords buzz—there is **no** English "ee" sound.
> * **11 syllables** produce the **True "ee" vowel** (like English *see* / Spanish *i*). The tongue moves forward and lips spread into a wide smile.
>
> *(Note: The initials **f, g, k, h** never pair with a standalone **i** in Mandarin).*

---

| | **shi** | [是 shì](shi4.wav) | To be, yes |

### Drill 1B: The 3-Way Tongue Position Showdown

1. **Flat-Tongue (z, c, s):** Tip behind upper/lower front teeth; tongue flat.
2. **"Smile" Palatals (j, q, x):** Flat blade of tongue pressed against hard palate; lips smiling.
3. **Curled Retroflexes (zh, ch, sh):** Tongue tip curled up and back toward the roof of the mouth.

| Consonant Type | Flat Tongue <br /> *(z, c, s)* | Meaning | Smile Palatals <br /> *(j, q, x)* | Meaning | Curled Retroflex <br /> *(zh, ch, sh)* | Meaning |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Unaspirated** *(No Air)* | [字 zì](zi4.wav) | Character | [记 jì](ji4.wav) | Remember | [智 zhì](zhi4.wav) | Wisdom |
| **Aspirated** *(Puff of Air)* | [次 cì](ci4.wav) | Occurrence | [气 qì](qi4.wav) | Air, gas | [翅 chì](chi4.wav) | Wing |
| **Fricative** *(Hiss)* | [四 sì](si4.wav) | Four | [细 xì](xi4.wav) | Thin, fine | [是 shì](shi4.wav) | To be, yes |

## Module 2: Vowel Shifts & "Hidden" Spelling Traps

### Drill 2A: The Buzzing "I" vs. True "EE" Audit
> ⚠️ **The Rule:** After *z, c, s, zh, ch, sh, r*, the letter **`i`** is an **apical/buzzing vowel** (tongue freezes in the consonant's position). After all other initials, it is a true smiling **"ee"** vowel.

| Consonant Contrast | Buzzing "i" *(Tongue Frozen)* | Meaning | True "ee" *(Smiling Lips)* | Meaning |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Flat vs. Palatal** | [思 sī](si1.wav) | To think | [西 xī](xi1.wav) | West |
| **Curled vs. Palatal** | [知 zhī](zhi1.wav) | To know | [鸡 jī](ji1.wav) | Chicken |
| **Voiced vs. Zero Initial** | [日 rì](ri4.wav) | Sun, day | [意 yì](yi4.wav) | Meaning |

### Drill 2C: Unmasking the 3 "Hidden Vowels"
Pinyin omits letters in spelling to save space. Always pronounce the **hidden middle vowel**:

| Written Form | Underlying Final | Spoken Sound | Example | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **-ui** | **-uei** *(Hidden "e")* | *"oo-ay"* *(sounds like way)* | [水 shuǐ](shui3.wav) | Water |
| **-iu** | **-iou** *(Hidden "o")* | *"ee-oh-oo"* *(sounds like yo)* | [六 liù](liu4.wav) | Six |
| **-un** | **-uen** *(Hidden "e")* | *"oo-un"* *(sounds like won)* | [轮 lún](lun2.wav) | Wheel |

### Drill 2D: The Ü Dot Trap & `-uan` Decryption
> ⚠️ **The Golden Check:**  
> * After **j, q, x, y**: Written `u` is **always ü** (dots dropped). The ending **`-uan`** is pronounced **"ü-en"**.  
> * After **other initials**: Written `u` is **regular "oo"**. The ending **`-uan`** is pronounced **"oo-ahn"**.

| Written Form | Underlying Final | Spoken Rhyme | Example | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **lù** | Regular **-u** | *"oo"* | [路 lù](lu4.wav) | Road |
| **lǜ** | Umlaut **-ü** *(dots kept)* | Whistling *"ee"* | [绿 lǜ](lü4.wav) | Green |
| **guān** | Regular **-uan** | *"oo-ahn"* *(open "ah")* | [关 guān](guan1.wav) | To close |
| **quān** | Hidden **-üan** *(dots dropped)* | *"ü-en"* *(rhymes with pen)* | [圈 quān](quan1.wav) | Circle |

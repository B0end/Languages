# Family & Titles

## Chinese Family Terms & Measure Words

> 💡 **Measure Word Quick Guide:**
> * **个 (*gè*)**: The universal, everyday measure word for individual people.
> * **位 (*wèi*)**: A respectful / polite measure word (敬词) used for elders, respected figures, or formal contexts (e.g., *一位母亲*, *一位爷爷*). Not typically used for children or younger siblings.
> * **对 (*duì*)**: "A pair / couple", used for paired relationships like parents (*一对父母*) or married couples (*一对夫妻*).
> * **群 (*qún*)**: "A group", used for multiple children (*一群孩子*).
> * **口 (*kǒu*)**: Specifically used when **counting the total number of members in a household** (e.g., *一家五口人* = "a family of five").

## Social Titles & Respectful Address

> ⚠️ **The Golden Word Order for Titles:**  
> In English, titles precede the surname (*"Mr. Wang"*, *"Teacher Zhang"*).  
> In Mandarin, **the surname ALWAYS comes first**:  
> **[Surname] + [Title]** ➔ [王先生; Wáng xiānsheng; Mr. Wang](wangxiansheng.wav), [张老师; Zhāng lǎoshī; Teacher Zhang](zhanglaoshi.wav), [李经理; Lǐ jīnglǐ; Manager Li](lijingli.wav)
>
> 💡 **Measure Word Guide for Titles:**
> * **位 (*wèi*)**: The primary polite / respectful measure word (敬词) for guests, experts, professionals, and elders (*一位教授*, *一位领导*).
> * **名 (*míng*)**: Formal and objective, common in news, administrative, legal, and academic registers (*一名律师*, *一名警察*).
> * **个 (*gè*)**: Universal and casual; acceptable in everyday informal conversation (*一个服务员*, *一个同事*).

HSK 1-5 Adjectives

Colors​

💡 How to Use Colors with Nouns:

  • Formal / Descriptive: Color + 色的 + Noun ➡️ 红色的衣服 (hóngsè de yīfu — red clothes)
  • Compound / Direct: Drop the 色 and 的 for simple fixed collocations ➡️ 红茶 (hóngchá — black tea), 蓝天 (lántiān — blue sky)
ColorCultural & Usage NotesHigh-Frequency Examples & Idioms
红色; hóngsè; RedLuck, joy, and prosperity.
Worn at weddings, Chinese New Year, and birthdays of the elderly.		• 红包 (hóngbāo) — red envelope / gift money
• 大红大紫 (dà hóng dà zǐ) — wildly popular / successful
• 红茶 (hóngchá) — black tea (lit. "red tea")
蓝色; lánsè; BlueCalmness, sky, ocean, and trust.
Widely used in corporate logos and technology contexts.		• 蓝天白云 (lántiān báiyún) — blue sky and white clouds
• 蓝色海洋 (lánsè hǎiyáng) — blue ocean
黄色; huángsè; YellowRoyalty and imperial power in ancient China.
⚠️ Modern Slang: Refers to erotic, NSFW, or adult content.		• 黄金 (huángjīn) — gold / imperial wealth
• 黄色电影 (huángsè diànyǐng) — adult / rated-R film
• 扫黄 (sǎo huáng) — anti-pornography crackdown
绿色; lǜsè; GreenNature, health, and eco-friendliness.
⚠️ Cultural Taboo: Never give or wear a green hat!		• 绿色食品 (lǜsè shípǐn) — organic / eco-friendly food
• 戴绿帽子 (dài lǜ màozi) — to be cheated on by one's partner (taboo)
• 绿茶 (lǜchá) — green tea
黑色; hēisè; BlackFormality, mystery, and seriousness.
Associated with the underground, secrecy, or illegal activity.		• 黑马 (hēimǎ) — dark horse (unexpected winner)
• 黑社会 (hēishèhuì) — mafia / criminal underworld
• 黑色幽默 (hēisè yōumò) — dark humor
白色; báisè; WhitePurity and innocence.
⚠️ Traditionally the color of mourning and funerals (白事).		• 白开水 (báikāishuǐ) — plain boiled water
• 白白浪费 (báibái làngfèi) — waste in vain ("white" can mean doing something with no result)
橙色; chéngsè; OrangeVitality, cheerfulness, and warmth.
Frequently used for public transit lines and safety alerts.		• 橙汁 (chéngzhī) — orange juice
• 橙色预警 (chéngsè yùjǐng) — orange alert (second-highest weather warning)
紫色; zǐsè; PurpleNobility, elegance, and mystique.
Historically associated with the emperor and divine favor.		• 紫禁城 (Zǐjìnchéng) — Forbidden City (lit. "Purple Forbidden City")
• 紫气东来 (zǐ qì dōng lái) — purple air comes from the east (propitious omen)
粉色; fěnsè; PinkYouth, romance, sweetness, and cuteness.
Often used in love, spring blossoms, and lifestyle branding.		• 粉色连衣裙 (fěnsè liányīqún) — pink dress
• 粉红色的回忆 (fěnhóngsè de huíyì) — fond, sweet romantic memories
灰色; huīsè; GrayNeutrality, minimalism, and modesty.
Also associated with gloominess or moral/legal ambiguity.		• 灰色地带 (huīsè dìdài) — gray area (neither strictly legal nor illegal)
• 灰心 (huīxīn) — discouraged / lose heart
青色; qīngsè; Cyan / Teal / GrueNature, vitality, and historical mystery.
One of the traditional "Five Elements" colors (associated with Wood, Spring, and the East).
Depending on the noun, it can mean green, blue, or black.		• 青年 (qīngnián) — youth / young person
• 青山绿水 (qīngshān lǜshuǐ) — green hills and clear waters
• 青天 (qīngtiān) — clear blue sky (also refers to an upright official)
• 青出于蓝 (qīng chū yú lán) — the student surpasses the master

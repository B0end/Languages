HSK 1-5 Verbs

The greatest relief in learning Mandarin is that Chinese verbs never conjugate:

  • No past, present, or future verb inflections (no "eat / ate / eaten").
  • No agreement with pronouns (no "I do / he does").
  • The verb 吃 (chī) remains 吃 in all contexts.

Basic Sentence Structure:
Subject + Verb + Object (SVO)

# Days, Months & Dates

Like telling time, dates in Mandarin follow the universal Chinese rule: **Largest Unit ➔ Smallest Unit**:

[年; nián; Year](nian2.wav) ➔ [月; yuè; Month](yue4.wav) ➔ [日; rì; Day (written)](ri4.wav) / [号; hào; Day (spoken)](hao4.wav) ➔ [星期; xīngqī; Day of week](xing1qi1.wav)

> **Example:**  
> [2026年9月1日星期二; èr líng èr liù nián, jiǔ yuè yī rì, xīngqī'èr; Tuesday, September 1, 2026](er4ling2er4liu4nian2jiu3yue4yi1ri4xing1qi1er4.wav)  
> *(Literal order: Year 2026, September, 1st Day, Tuesday)*

## 1. How to Say Years: The "Single-Digit" Rule

> ⚠️ **The Year Trap:**  
> In English, we say *"twenty twenty-six"* or *"two thousand twenty-six"*.  
> In Mandarin, **years are simply read digit-by-digit + [年; nián; Year](nian2.wav)**. Never read years as thousands!

| Year | Spoken Breakdown |
| :---: | :--- |
| [一九九八年; yī jiǔ jiǔ bā nián; Year 1998](yi1jiu3jiu3ba1nian2.wav) | 1 + 9 + 9 + 8 + 年 |

| [二零二五年; èr líng èr wǔ nián; Year 2025](2025.wav) | 2 + 0 + 2 + 5 + 年 |
| [今年; jīnnián; This year](jin1nian2.wav) | Base vocabulary |

## 5. Relative Time Matrix (The Past ➔ Future Grid)

> 💡 **The Spatial Mental Model:**  
> * **Weeks & Months** follow a **vertical calendar**: the past is "above" ([上; shàng; Above / Previous](shang4.wav)), the future is "below" ([下; xià; Below / Next](xia4.wav)).  
> * **Days & Years** follow a **horizontal timeline**: the past is "front / before" ([前; qián; Before / Ago](qian2.wav)), the future is "behind / after" ([后; hòu; After / Later](hou4.wav)).

| Time Frame | Days ([天; tiān; Day](tian1.wav)) | Weeks ([星期; xīngqī; Week](xing1qi1.wav)) | Months ([月; yuè; Month](yue4.wav)) | Years ([年; nián; Year](nian2.wav)) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **-2 Cycles** | [前天; qiántiān; Day before yesterday](qian2tian1.wav) | [上上个星期; shàngshàng ge xīngqī; 2 weeks ago](shang4shang4ge4xing1qi1.wav) | [上上个月; shàngshàng ge yuè; 2 months ago](shang4shang4ge4yue4.wav) | [前年; qiánnián; Year before last](qian2nian2.wav) |
| **-1 Cycle** | [昨天; zuótiān; Yesterday](zuo2tian1.wav) | [上个星期; shàng ge xīngqī; Last week](shang4ge4xing1qi1.wav) | [上个月; shàng ge yuè; Last month](shang4ge4yue4.wav) | [去年; qùnián; Last year](qu4nian2.wav) ⚠️ |

### What Happens Beyond ±2 Cycles?

You do not stack words infinitely (avoid saying *"上上上个星期"*). Instead, use these two systematic patterns:

#### 1. The "大" (*dà*) Extension for Exactly ±3 Cycles (Days & Years Only)

| Time Frame | Days ([天; tiān; Day](tian1.wav)) | Years ([年; nián; Year](nian2.wav)) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **-3 Cycles** *(3 units ago)* | [大前天; dàqiántiān; 3 days ago](da4qian2tian1.wav) | [大前年; dàqiánnián; 3 years ago](da4qian2nian2.wav) |
| **+3 Cycles** *(3 units later)* | [大后天; dàhòutiān; 3 days from now](da4hou4tian1.wav) | [大后年; dàhòunián; 3 years from now](da4hou4nian2.wav) |

*([大; dà; Big / Great](da4.wav) is not used for weeks or months).*

---

#### 2. The Universal Mathematical Formula (3+ Cycles & General Time)

> **Past (Ago):** `[Number] + Unit + [前; qián; Ago / Before](qian2.wav)`  
> **Future (Later):** `[Number] + Unit + [后; hòu; Later / After](hou4.wav)`

| Time Unit | Past: `[#] + Unit + 前` | Future: `[#] + Unit + 后` |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Days** | [三天前; sān tiān qián; 3 days ago](san1tian1qian2.wav) | [五天后; wǔ tiān hòu; 5 days from now](wu3tian1hou4.wav) |
| **Weeks** | [三个星期前; sān gè xīngqī qián; 3 weeks ago](san1ge4xing1qi1qian2.wav) | [两周后; liǎng zhōu hòu; 2 weeks later](liang3zhou1hou4.wav) |

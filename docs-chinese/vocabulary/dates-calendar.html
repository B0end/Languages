Days, Months & Dates

Like telling time, dates in Mandarin follow the universal Chinese rule: Largest Unit ➔ Smallest Unit:

年; nián; Year ➔ 月; yuè; Month ➔ 日; rì; Day (written) / 号; hào; Day (spoken) ➔ 星期; xīngqī; Day of week

Example:
2026年9月1日星期二; èr líng èr liù nián, jiǔ yuè yī rì, xīngqī'èr; Tuesday, September 1, 2026
(Literal order: Year 2026, September, 1st Day, Tuesday)

1. How to Say Years: The "Single-Digit" Rule​

⚠️ The Year Trap:
In English, we say "twenty twenty-six" or "two thousand twenty-six".
In Mandarin, years are simply read digit-by-digit + 年; nián; Year. Never read years as thousands!

3. Days of the Month: 号 (hào) vs. 日 (rì)​

Mandarin has two words for the calendar day of the month:

UnitRegister / UsageExample
号; hào; Day of month (spoken)Daily Speech & Spoken Conversation (casual)五月五号; wǔ yuè wǔ hào; May 5th
日; rì; Day of month (written)Writing, Forms, Documents & Official Dates (formal)五月五日; wǔ yuè wǔ rì; May 5th

4. Days of the Week​

Sunday is the only irregular day—never say "星期七"!

5. Relative Time Matrix (The Past ➔ Future Grid)​

💡 The Spatial Mental Model:

  • Weeks & Months follow a vertical calendar: the past is "above" (上; shàng; Above / Previous), the future is "below" (下; xià; Below / Next).
  • Days & Years follow a horizontal timeline: the past is "front / before" (前; qián; Before / Ago), the future is "behind / after" (后; hòu; After / Later).
Time FrameDays (天; tiān; Day)Weeks (星期; xīngqī; Week)Months (月; yuè; Month)Years (年; nián; Year)
-2 Cycles前天; qiántiān; Day before yesterday上上个星期; shàngshàng ge xīngqī; 2 weeks ago上上个月; shàngshàng ge yuè; 2 months ago前年; qiánnián; Year before last
-1 Cycle昨天; zuótiān; Yesterday上个星期; shàng ge xīngqī; Last week上个月; shàng ge yuè; Last month去年; qùnián; Last year ⚠️
Present今天; jīntiān; Today这个星期; zhè ge xīngqī; This week这个月; zhè ge yuè; This month今年; jīnnián; This year
+1 Cycle明天; míngtiān; Tomorrow下个星期; xià ge xīngqī; Next week下个月; xià ge yuè; Next month明年; míngnián; Next year
+2 Cycles后天; hòutiān; Day after tomorrow下下个星期; xiàxià ge xīngqī; 2 weeks from now下下个月; xiàxià ge yuè; 2 months from now后年; hòunián; Year after next

What Happens Beyond ±2 Cycles?​

You do not stack words infinitely (avoid saying "上上上个星期"). Instead, use these two systematic patterns:

1. The "大" (dà) Extension for Exactly ±3 Cycles (Days & Years Only)​

Time FrameDays (天; tiān; Day)Years (年; nián; Year)
-3 Cycles (3 units ago)大前天; dàqiántiān; 3 days ago大前年; dàqiánnián; 3 years ago
+3 Cycles (3 units later)大后天; dàhòutiān; 3 days from now大后年; dàhòunián; 3 years from now

(大; dà; Big / Great is not used for weeks or months).

2. The Universal Mathematical Formula (3+ Cycles & General Time)​

Past (Ago): [Number] + Unit + [前; qián; Ago / Before](qian2.wav)
Future (Later): [Number] + Unit + [后; hòu; Later / After](hou4.wav)

Time UnitPast: [#] + Unit + 前Future: [#] + Unit + 后
Days三天前; sān tiān qián; 3 days ago五天后; wǔ tiān hòu; 5 days from now
Weeks三个星期前; sān gè xīngqī qián; 3 weeks ago两周后; liǎng zhōu hòu; 2 weeks later
Months四个月前; sì gè yuè qián; 4 months ago六个月后; liù gè yuè hòu; 6 months later
Years十年前; shí nián qián; 10 years ago三年后; sān nián hòu; 3 years later

Present tense of regular verbs

Regular verbs in the Present Tense are categorized into three main patterns according to their infinitive endings: -ER, -IR, and -RE.

Just like Spanish (-ar, -er, -ir), you remove the infinitive ending to find the root/stem and add person endings. However, there are two crucial rules your brain must adapt to:

  1. Mandatory Pronouns (Like English): Spanish drops subject pronouns ("Hablo francés"). French cannot drop them ("Je parle français"), exactly like English ("I speak French").
  2. Silent Endings (Phonetic Trap!): In Spanish, every ending sounds different (hablo, hablas, habla, hablan). In French, many written endings are completely silent, making different grammatical forms sound identical.

Group 1: Verbs ending in -ER​

This group represents about 90% of all French verbs (equivalent to Spanish -ar verbs).

  • Infinitive: parler;; hablar / to speak
  • Stem: parl-
PronounEndingExamplePhonetic Note
Je-eJe parle;; Yo hablo / I speakSilent -e. Sounds like: /paʁl/
Tu-esTu parles;; Tú hablas / You speakSilent -es. Sounds identical to parle
Il / Elle / On-eIl parle;; Él habla / He speaksSilent -e. Sounds identical to parle
Nous-onsNous parlons;; Nosotros hablamos / We speakNasal -ons (/ɔ̃/), silent -s
Vous-ezVous parlez;; Usted habla (o Vosotros habláis) / You speakSounds like Spanish -é (/e/)
Ils / Elles-entIls parlent;; Ellos hablan / They speakWARNING: The -ent is totally silent! Sounds identical to parle

💡 Phonetic shortcut: Je parle, tu parles, il parle, and ils parlent all sound 100% identical (/paʁl/). You only hear a difference with nous (/paʁ.lɔ̃/) and vous (/paʁ.le/).

More common -ER verbs:

  • aimer;; amar (o gustar) / to love (or to like)
  • habiter;; vivir (o habitar) / to live (reside)
  • travailler;; trabajar / to work

Group 2: Regular verbs ending in -IR​

These verbs are characterized by an -iss- sound that appears in all plural forms (nous, vous, ils/elles).

  • Infinitive: finir;; terminar / to finish
  • Stem: fin-
PronounEndingExamplePhonetic Note
Je-isJe finis;; Yo termino / I finishSilent -s. Sounds like: /fi.ni/
Tu-isTu finis;; Tú terminas / You finishSilent -s. Sounds identical to finis
Il / Elle / On-itElle finit;; Ella termina / She finishesSilent -t. Sounds identical to finis
Nous-issonsNous finissons;; Nosotros terminamos / We finishUses the English /s/ sound + nasal /ɔ̃/
Vous-issezVous finissez;; Usted termina / You finishUses the English /s/ sound + /e/
Ils / Elles-issentElles finissent;; Ellas terminan / They finishSilent -ent, but the -ss- is pronounced (/fi.nis/)

More regular -IR verbs:

  • choisir;; elegir (o escoger) / to choose
  • réussir;; tener éxito (o aprobar) / to succeed
  • grandir;; crecer / to grow

Group 3: Regular verbs ending in -RE​

While the third group contains many irregular verbs, regular -RE verbs follow a very steady pattern.

  • Infinitive: vendre;; vender / to sell
  • Stem: vend-
PronounEndingExamplePhonetic Note
Je-sJe vends;; Yo vendo / I sellSilent -ds. Nasal vowel: /vɑ̃/
Tu-sTu vends;; Tú vendes / You sellSilent -ds. Sounds identical to vends
Il / Elle / On-(nothing)Il vend;; Él vende / He sellsSilent -d. Sounds identical to vends
Nous-onsNous vendons;; Nosotros vendemos / We sellThe d is pronounced: /vɑ̃.dɔ̃/
Vous-ezVous vendez;; Usted vende / You sellThe d is pronounced: /vɑ̃.de/
Ils / Elles-entIls vendent;; Ellos venden / They sellSilent -ent, but the d is pronounced: /vɑ̃d/

More regular -RE verbs:

  • attendre;; esperar / to wait
  • entendre;; oír (o escuchar) / to hear
  • répondre;; responder / to answer

Quick Comparison​

  1. No Present Continuous form needed:

    • English has: "I speak" and "I am speaking".
    • Spanish has: "Hablo" and "Estoy hablando".
    • French uses the standard present for both:
      • Je mange;; Yo como (o estoy comiendo) / I eat (or I am eating)

  2. The "Silent -ENT" Trap:

    • In Spanish, third-person plural adds a clear syllable: habla → hablan.
    • In French, the letters -ent in verb conjugation make no sound at all:
      • Il écoute;; Él escucha / He listens
      • Ils écoutent;; Ellos escuchan / They listen (Phonetically identical to the singular!)

# Present tense of regular verbs

Regular verbs in the Present Tense are categorized into three main patterns according to their infinitive endings: **-ER**, **-IR**, and **-RE**.

Just like Spanish (*-ar, -er, -ir*), you remove the infinitive ending to find the root/stem and add person endings. However, there are two crucial rules your brain must adapt to:

1. **Mandatory Pronouns (Like English):** Spanish drops subject pronouns (*"Hablo francés"*). French cannot drop them (*"Je parle français"*), exactly like English (*"I speak French"*).
2. **Silent Endings (Phonetic Trap!):** In Spanish, every ending sounds different (*hablo, hablas, habla, hablan*). In French, many written endings are **completely silent**, making different grammatical forms sound identical.

---

## Group 1: Verbs ending in -ER

This group represents about 90% of all French verbs (equivalent to Spanish *-ar* verbs).

* **Infinitive:** [parler;; hablar / to speak](parler.wav)
* **Stem:** `parl-`

| Pronoun | Ending | Example | Phonetic Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Je** | **-e** | [Je parle;; Yo hablo / I speak](Je_parle.wav) | Silent **-e**. Sounds like: /paʁl/ |
| **Tu** | **-es** | [Tu parles;; Tú hablas / You speak](Tu_parles.wav) | Silent **-es**. Sounds identical to *parle* |
| **Il / Elle / On** | **-e** | [Il parle;; Él habla / He speaks](Il_parle.wav) | Silent **-e**. Sounds identical to *parle* |
| **Nous** | **-ons** | [Nous parlons;; Nosotros hablamos / We speak](Nous_parlons.wav) | Nasal **-ons** (/ɔ̃/), silent **-s** |
| **Vous** | **-ez** | [Vous parlez;; Usted habla (o Vosotros habláis) / You speak](Vous_parlez.wav) | Sounds like Spanish **-é** (/e/) |
| **Ils / Elles** | **-ent** | [Ils parlent;; Ellos hablan / They speak](Ils_parlent.wav) | **WARNING:** The **-ent** is totally silent! Sounds identical to *parle* |

> 💡 **Phonetic shortcut:** *Je parle*, *tu parles*, *il parle*, and *ils parlent* all sound **100% identical** (/paʁl/). You only hear a difference with *nous* (/paʁ.lɔ̃/) and *vous* (/paʁ.le/).

More common -ER verbs:
* [aimer;; amar (o gustar) / to love (or to like)](aimer.wav)
* [habiter;; vivir (o habitar) / to live (reside)](habiter.wav)
* [travailler;; trabajar / to work](travailler.wav)

## Group 2: Regular verbs ending in -IR

These verbs are characterized by an **-iss-** sound that appears in all plural forms (*nous, vous, ils/elles*).

* **Infinitive:** [finir;; terminar / to finish](finir.wav)
* **Stem:** `fin-`

| Pronoun | Ending | Example | Phonetic Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Je** | **-is** | [Je finis;; Yo termino / I finish](Je_finis.wav) | Silent **-s**. Sounds like: /fi.ni/ |
| **Tu** | **-is** | [Tu finis;; Tú terminas / You finish](Tu_finis.wav) | Silent **-s**. Sounds identical to *finis* |
| **Il / Elle / On** | **-it** | [Elle finit;; Ella termina / She finishes](Elle_finit.wav) | Silent **-t**. Sounds identical to *finis* |
| **Nous** | **-issons** | [Nous finissons;; Nosotros terminamos / We finish](Nous_finissons.wav) | Uses the English /s/ sound + nasal /ɔ̃/ |
| **Vous** | **-issez** | [Vous finissez;; Usted termina / You finish](Vous_finissez.wav) | Uses the English /s/ sound + /e/ |
| **Ils / Elles** | **-issent** | [Elles finissent;; Ellas terminan / They finish](Elles_finissent.wav) | Silent **-ent**, but the **-ss-** is pronounced (/fi.nis/) |

More regular -IR verbs:
* [choisir;; elegir (o escoger) / to choose](choisir.wav)
* [réussir;; tener éxito (o aprobar) / to succeed](reussir.wav)
* [grandir;; crecer / to grow](grandir.wav)

## Group 3: Regular verbs ending in -RE

While the third group contains many irregular verbs, regular **-RE** verbs follow a very steady pattern.

* **Infinitive:** [vendre;; vender / to sell](vendre.wav)
* **Stem:** `vend-`

| Pronoun | Ending | Example | Phonetic Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Je** | **-s** | [Je vends;; Yo vendo / I sell](Je_vends.wav) | Silent **-ds**. Nasal vowel: /vɑ̃/ |
| **Tu** | **-s** | [Tu vends;; Tú vendes / You sell](Tu_vends.wav) | Silent **-ds**. Sounds identical to *vends* |
| **Il / Elle / On** | **-(nothing)** | [Il vend;; Él vende / He sells](Il_vend.wav) | Silent **-d**. Sounds identical to *vends* |
| **Nous** | **-ons** | [Nous vendons;; Nosotros vendemos / We sell](Nous_vendons.wav) | The **d** is pronounced: /vɑ̃.dɔ̃/ |
| **Vous** | **-ez** | [Vous vendez;; Usted vende / You sell](Vous_vendez.wav) | The **d** is pronounced: /vɑ̃.de/ |
| **Ils / Elles** | **-ent** | [Ils vendent;; Ellos venden / They sell](Ils_vendent.wav) | Silent **-ent**, but the **d** is pronounced: /vɑ̃d/ |

More regular -RE verbs:
* [attendre;; esperar / to wait](attendre.wav)
* [entendre;; oír (o escuchar) / to hear](entendre.wav)
* [répondre;; responder / to answer](répondre.wav)

## Quick Comparison

1. **No Present Continuous form needed:**
   * English has: *"I speak"* and *"I am speaking"*.
   * Spanish has: *"Hablo"* and *"Estoy hablando"*.
   * French uses the standard present for **both**:
     * [Je mange;; Yo como (o estoy comiendo) / I eat (or I am eating)](Je_mange.wav)

2. **The "Silent -ENT" Trap:**
   * In Spanish, third-person plural adds a clear syllable: *habla* → *hablan*.
   * In French, the letters **-ent** in verb conjugation make **no sound at all**:
     * [Il écoute;; Él escucha / He listens](Il_écoute.wav)
     * [Ils écoutent;; Ellos escuchan / They listen](Ils_écoutent.wav) *(Phonetically identical to the singular!)*

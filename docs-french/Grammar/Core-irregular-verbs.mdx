# Core irregular verbs

In French, irregular verbs are verbs that change their stem drastically or take non-standard endings. 

There are **four fundamental irregular verbs** (*Être, Avoir, Aller, Faire*) that serve as the structural backbone of the entire language, plus a few essential modal and everyday verbs.

---

### 1. The Two Pillars: ÊTRE & AVOIR

Just like in Spanish (*ser/estar* and *haber/tener*) and English (*to be* and *to have*), these are both stand-alone verbs and essential auxiliary verbs for compound tenses.

#### A. Être (To be)
Notice how closely the French forms mirror Spanish: *suis/soy*, *es/eres*, *est/es*, *sommes/somos*, *sont/son*.

* **Infinitive:** [être;; ser (o estar) / to be](etre.wav)

| Pronoun | Form | Example | Phonetic Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Je** | **suis** | [Je suis;; Yo soy (o estoy) / I am](Je_suis.wav) | Silent **-s** (/sɥi/) |
| **Tu** | **es** | [Tu es;; Tú eres (o estás) / You are](Tu_es.wav) | Silent **-s**, sounds like open /ɛ/ |
| **Il / Elle** | **est** | [Il est;; Él es (o está) / He is](Il_est.wav) | Silent **-st**, sounds identical to *tu es* (/ɛ/) |
| **Nous** | **sommes** | [Nous sommes;; Nosotros somos (o estamos) / We are](Nous_sommes.wav) | Silent **-es** (/sɔm/) |
| **Vous** | **êtes** | [Vous êtes;; Usted es / Vosotros sois / You are](Vous_etes.wav) | **Mandatory liaison with Z sound:** /vu.zɛt/ |
| **Ils / Elles** | **sont** | [Ils sont;; Ellos son (o están) / They are](Ils_sont.wav) | Clean /s/ sound + nasal: /il.sɔ̃/ |

---

#### B. Avoir (To have)
Used both for possession (*tener*) and for compound tenses (*haber* / *to have*).

* **Infinitive:** [avoir;; tener (o haber) / to have](avoir.wav)

| Pronoun | Form | Example | Phonetic Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **J'** | **ai** | [J'ai;; Yo tengo (o he) / I have](J_ai.wav) | Contraction: *Je* becomes *J'* before a vowel |
| **Tu** | **as** | [Tu as;; Tú tienes (o has) / You have](Tu_as.wav) | Silent **-s** (/a/) |
| **Il / Elle** | **a** | [Elle a;; Ella tiene (o ha) / She has](Elle_a.wav) | Single vowel /a/ |
| **Nous** | **avons** | [Nous avons;; Nosotros tenemos / We have](Nous_avons.wav) | **Liaison with Z:** /nu.za.vɔ̃/ |
| **Vous** | **avez** | [Vous avez;; Usted tiene / You have](Vous_avez.wav) | **Liaison with Z:** /vu.za.ve/ |
| **Ils / Elles** | **ont** | [Ils ont;; Ellos tienen / They have](Ils_ont.wav) | **Liaison with Z:** /il.zɔ̃/ |

> ⚠️ **The #1 Phonetic Trap for Spanish Speakers:**
> * [Ils sont;; Ellos son / They are](Ils_sont.wav) $\rightarrow$ Pronounced with an **S** sound: **/il.sɔ̃/**
> * [Ils ont;; Ellos tienen / They have](Ils_ont.wav) $\rightarrow$ Pronounced with a **Z** sound (English buzz): **/il.zɔ̃/**
> * *Confusing these two means saying "they have" instead of "they are"!*

### 2. Action & Movement: ALLER & FAIRE

#### A. Aller (To go)
This verb is completely irregular: it starts with **V** in the singular, switches to **All-** in the plural, and ends in **Vont**. It is used to form the immediate future tense (*futur proche*), identical to English *"going to"* or Spanish *"ir a"*.

* **Infinitive:** [aller;; ir / to go](aller.wav)

* [Je vais;; Yo voy / I go](Je_vais.wav)
* [Tu vas;; Tú vas / You go](Tu_vas.wav)
* [Il va;; Él va / He goes](Il_va.wav)
* [Nous allons;; Nosotros vamos / We go](Nous_allons.wav) *(Liaison: /nu.za.lɔ̃/)*
* [Vous allez;; Usted va (o Vosotros vais) / You go](Vous_allez.wav) *(Liaison: /vu.za.le/)*
* [Ils vont;; Ellos van / They go](Ils_vont.wav)

---

#### B. Faire (To do / To make)
Equivalent to both Spanish *hacer* and English *to do / to make*.

* **Infinitive:** [faire;; hacer / to do (or to make)](faire.wav)

* [Je fais;; Yo hago / I do](Je_fais.wav)
* [Tu fais;; Tú haces / You do](Tu_fais.wav)
* [Elle fait;; Ella hace / She does](Elle_fait.wav)
* [Nous faisons;; Nosotros hacemos / We do](Nous_faisons.wav) *(Pronunciation note: the "ai" sounds like a weak "uh" /fə.zɔ̃/)*
* [Vous faites;; Usted hace (o Vosotros hacéis) / You do](Vous_faites.wav) *(Irregular ending: **-tes**, not -ez)*
* [Ils font;; Ellos hacen / They do](Ils_font.wav)

### 3. The Core Modals: POUVOIR & VOULOIR

These two verbs are twins. Notice that their endings are identical: **-x, -x, -t, -ons, -ez, -ent**.

#### A. Pouvoir (Can / To be able to / Poder)
* **Infinitive:** [pouvoir;; poder / can (or to be able to)](pouvoir.wav)

* [Je peux;; Yo puedo / I can](Je_peux.wav)
* [Tu peux;; Tú puedes / You can](Tu_peux.wav)
* [Il peut;; Él puede / He can](Il_peut.wav)
* [Nous pouvons;; Nosotros podemos / We can](Nous_pouvons.wav)
* [Vous pouvez;; Usted puede / You can](Vous_pouvez.wav)
* [Ils peuvent;; Ellos pueden / They can](Ils_peuvent.wav)

#### B. Vouloir (To want / Querer)
* **Infinitive:** [vouloir;; querer / to want](vouloir.wav)

* [Je veux;; Yo quiero / I want](Je_veux.wav)
* [Tu veux;; Tú quieres / You want](Tu_veux.wav)
* [Elle veut;; Ella quiere / She wants](Elle_veut.wav)
* [Nous voulons;; Nosotros queremos / We want](Nous_voulons.wav)
* [Vous voulez;; Usted quiere / You want](Vous_voulez.wav)
* [Elles veulent;; Ellas quieren / They want](Elles_veulent.wav)

### 4. High-Frequency Stems: VENIR & PRENDRE

#### A. Venir (To come / Venir)
Used to express the recent past: *venir de + infinitive* (*acabar de + infinitivo*).
* **Infinitive:** [venir;; venir / to come](venir.wav)

* [Je viens;; Yo vengo / I come](Je_viens.wav)
* [Tu viens;; Tú vienes / You come](Tu_viens.wav)
* [Il vient;; Él viene / He comes](Il_vient.wav)
* [Nous venons;; Nosotros venimos / We come](Nous_venons.wav)
* [Vous venez;; Usted viene / You come](Vous_venez.wav)
* [Ils viennent;; Ellos vienen / They come](Ils_viennent.wav)

#### B. Prendre (To take / Tomar)
Also the root for [apprendre;; aprender / to learn](apprendre.wav) and [comprendre;; entender (o comprender) / to understand](comprendre.wav).
* **Infinitive:** [prendre;; tomar (o coger) / to take](prendre.wav)

* [Je prends;; Yo tomo / I take](Je_prends.wav)
* [Tu prends;; Tú tomas / You take](Tu_prends.wav)
* [Elle prend;; Ella toma / She takes](Elle_prend.wav)
* [Nous prenons;; Nosotros tomamos / We take](Nous_prenons.wav)
* [Vous prenez;; Usted toma / You take](Vous_prenez.wav)
* [Elles prennent;; Ellas toman / They take](Elles_prennent.wav)

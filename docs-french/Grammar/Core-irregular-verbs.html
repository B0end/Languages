Core irregular verbs

In French, irregular verbs are verbs that change their stem drastically or take non-standard endings.

There are four fundamental irregular verbs (Être, Avoir, Aller, Faire) that serve as the structural backbone of the entire language, plus a few essential modal and everyday verbs.

1. The Two Pillars: ÊTRE & AVOIR​

Just like in Spanish (ser/estar and haber/tener) and English (to be and to have), these are both stand-alone verbs and essential auxiliary verbs for compound tenses.

A. Être (To be)​

Notice how closely the French forms mirror Spanish: suis/soy, es/eres, est/es, sommes/somos, sont/son.

  • Infinitive: être;; ser (o estar) / to be
PronounFormExamplePhonetic Note
JesuisJe suis;; Yo soy (o estoy) / I amSilent -s (/sɥi/)
TuesTu es;; Tú eres (o estás) / You areSilent -s, sounds like open /ɛ/
Il / ElleestIl est;; Él es (o está) / He isSilent -st, sounds identical to tu es (/ɛ/)
NoussommesNous sommes;; Nosotros somos (o estamos) / We areSilent -es (/sɔm/)
VousêtesVous êtes;; Usted es / Vosotros sois / You areMandatory liaison with Z sound: /vu.zɛt/
Ils / EllessontIls sont;; Ellos son (o están) / They areClean /s/ sound + nasal: /il.sɔ̃/

B. Avoir (To have)​

Used both for possession (tener) and for compound tenses (haber / to have).

  • Infinitive: avoir;; tener (o haber) / to have
PronounFormExamplePhonetic Note
J'aiJ'ai;; Yo tengo (o he) / I haveContraction: Je becomes J' before a vowel
TuasTu as;; Tú tienes (o has) / You haveSilent -s (/a/)
Il / ElleaElle a;; Ella tiene (o ha) / She hasSingle vowel /a/
NousavonsNous avons;; Nosotros tenemos / We haveLiaison with Z: /nu.za.vɔ̃/
VousavezVous avez;; Usted tiene / You haveLiaison with Z: /vu.za.ve/
Ils / EllesontIls ont;; Ellos tienen / They haveLiaison with Z: /il.zɔ̃/

⚠️ The #1 Phonetic Trap for Spanish Speakers:

  • Ils sont;; Ellos son / They are $\rightarrow$ Pronounced with an S sound: /il.sɔ̃/
  • Ils ont;; Ellos tienen / They have $\rightarrow$ Pronounced with a Z sound (English buzz): /il.zɔ̃/
  • Confusing these two means saying "they have" instead of "they are"!

2. Action & Movement: ALLER & FAIRE​

A. Aller (To go)​

This verb is completely irregular: it starts with V in the singular, switches to All- in the plural, and ends in Vont. It is used to form the immediate future tense (futur proche), identical to English "going to" or Spanish "ir a".

  • Infinitive: aller;; ir / to go

  • Je vais;; Yo voy / I go

  • Tu vas;; Tú vas / You go

  • Il va;; Él va / He goes

  • Nous allons;; Nosotros vamos / We go (Liaison: /nu.za.lɔ̃/)

  • Vous allez;; Usted va (o Vosotros vais) / You go (Liaison: /vu.za.le/)

  • Ils vont;; Ellos van / They go

B. Faire (To do / To make)​

Equivalent to both Spanish hacer and English to do / to make.

  • Infinitive: faire;; hacer / to do (or to make)

  • Je fais;; Yo hago / I do

  • Tu fais;; Tú haces / You do

  • Elle fait;; Ella hace / She does

  • Nous faisons;; Nosotros hacemos / We do (Pronunciation note: the "ai" sounds like a weak "uh" /fə.zɔ̃/)

  • Vous faites;; Usted hace (o Vosotros hacéis) / You do (Irregular ending: -tes, not -ez)

  • Ils font;; Ellos hacen / They do

3. The Core Modals: POUVOIR & VOULOIR​

These two verbs are twins. Notice that their endings are identical: -x, -x, -t, -ons, -ez, -ent.

A. Pouvoir (Can / To be able to / Poder)​

  • Infinitive: pouvoir;; poder / can (or to be able to)

  • Je peux;; Yo puedo / I can

  • Tu peux;; Tú puedes / You can

  • Il peut;; Él puede / He can

  • Nous pouvons;; Nosotros podemos / We can

  • Vous pouvez;; Usted puede / You can

  • Ils peuvent;; Ellos pueden / They can

B. Vouloir (To want / Querer)​

  • Infinitive: vouloir;; querer / to want

  • Je veux;; Yo quiero / I want

  • Tu veux;; Tú quieres / You want

  • Elle veut;; Ella quiere / She wants

  • Nous voulons;; Nosotros queremos / We want

  • Vous voulez;; Usted quiere / You want

  • Elles veulent;; Ellas quieren / They want

4. High-Frequency Stems: VENIR & PRENDRE​

A. Venir (To come / Venir)​

Used to express the recent past: venir de + infinitive (acabar de + infinitivo).

  • Infinitive: venir;; venir / to come

  • Je viens;; Yo vengo / I come

  • Tu viens;; Tú vienes / You come

  • Il vient;; Él viene / He comes

  • Nous venons;; Nosotros venimos / We come

  • Vous venez;; Usted viene / You come

  • Ils viennent;; Ellos vienen / They come

B. Prendre (To take / Tomar)​

Also the root for apprendre;; aprender / to learn and comprendre;; entender (o comprender) / to understand.

  • Infinitive: prendre;; tomar (o coger) / to take

  • Je prends;; Yo tomo / I take

  • Tu prends;; Tú tomas / You take

  • Elle prend;; Ella toma / She takes

  • Nous prenons;; Nosotros tomamos / We take

  • Vous prenez;; Usted toma / You take

  • Elles prennent;; Ellas toman / They take

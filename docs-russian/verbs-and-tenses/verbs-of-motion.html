Verbs of Motion

In Spanish and English, you use one single verb to move around: Ir / To Go.

  • Voy al supermercado a pie.
  • Voy a París en avión.
  • Voy al gimnasio todos los días.

In Slavic languages, there is no generic word for "to go". Instead, Russian forces you to answer two mandatory questions every time you move:

  1. How are you moving? ➡️ On foot or by transport?
  2. What kind of trip is it? ➡️ A one-way line right now, or a round-trip / regular habit?

Question 1: Feet or Wheels?​

Russian makes a strict physical distinction between moving under your own muscle power versus using a vehicle:

Method of MovementUnidirectional (One-way / Now)Multidirectional (Habit / Round-trip)
On Foot (Caminando, a pie)Идти;; Ir a pie (en camino) / To go on foot (unidirectional)Ходить;; Ir a pie (hábito, ida y vuelta) / To go on foot (multidirectional)
By Vehicle (En coche, tren, avión, barco)Ехать;; Ir en vehículo (en camino) / To go by vehicle (unidirectional)Ездить;; Ir en vehículo (hábito, ida y vuelta) / To go by vehicle (multidirectional)
warning

In Spanish, you can say "Voy a Rusia".
If you say Я иду в Россию, Russians will look at you in shock: it literally means you are walking across continents on your own two feet!
If you use an airplane, bus, train, or car, you must use Ехать;; Ir en vehículo / To go by transport:

  • Я еду в Россию;; Voy a Rusia (en transporte) / I am traveling to Russia

Question 2: The "Arrow" Concept (Uni vs. Multi)​

Every method of motion has two paired verbs:

1. Unidirectional Verbs (The One-Way Arrow: ➡️)​

Use these when you are in the middle of a trip heading toward a single specific destination:

  • Я иду на работу;; Voy al trabajo (a pie, ahora mismo) / I am walking to work
  • Мы едем в центр;; Vamos al centro (en coche/metro, en camino) / We are driving to downtown

2. Multidirectional Verbs (The Round-Trip & Habit Arrow: 🔁)​

Use these for three specific situations:

A. Habits and Routines (Todos los días, a menudo):​

  • Я хожу на работу каждый день;; Voy al trabajo todos los días (a pie) / I walk to work every day
  • Они часто ездят в горы;; Ellos a menudo van a la montaña (en coche) / They often go to the mountains

B. Completed Round-Trips in the Past (Fui y volví):​

  • Вчера я ходил в музей;; Ayer fui al museo (a pie, y ya volví) / Yesterday I went to the museum (Compare with: Вчера я шёл в музей;; Ayer iba de camino al museo / Yesterday I was on my way to the museum)

C. Moving with No Fixed Direction (Paseando por el parque):​

  • Дети бегают в парке;; Los niños corren por el parque (en círculos) / Children are running around in the park

Adding Prefixes: The Slavic GPS System​

Once you know Идти;; Ir a pie / To walk and Ехать;; Ir en vehículo / To drive, ride, you can create dozens of new, super-precise verbs simply by adding directional prefixes.

Notice how the prefix changes the direction:

PrefixMeaningOn Foot (A pie)By Transport (En vehículo)
При-Arrival (Llegar)Прийти;; Llegar a pie / To arrive on footПриехать;; Llegar en transporte / To arrive by vehicle
У-Departure far away (Irse)Уйти;; Irse a pie / To leave on footУехать;; Irse en transporte / To leave by vehicle
В- / Во-Entering (Entrar)Войти;; Entrar a pie / To enter on footВъехать;; Entrar en vehículo / To drive in
Вы-Exiting (Salir)Выйти;; Salir a pie / To exit on footВыехать;; Salir en transporte / To drive out
Пере-Crossing across (Cruzar)Перейти;; Cruzar la calle (a pie) / To cross on footПереехать;; Cruzar o mudarse / To drive across, move
До-Reaching a destination (Llegar hasta)Дойти;; Llegar hasta (a pie) / To reach on footДоехать;; Llegar hasta (en transporte) / To reach by transport

Practical Examples with Prefixes:​

  • Поезд приехал в Москву;; El tren llegó a Moscú / The train arrived in Moscow
  • Он вышел из комнаты;; Él salió de la habitación / He walked out of the room
  • Осторожно переходи улицу;; Cruza la calle con cuidado / Cross the street carefully

Summary Checklist​

Before choosing a motion verb, run this mental filter:

  1. Step 1: Transport check:
    • Walking? ➡️ Base root: -идти- / -ходить-
    • Vehicle (car, bus, train, plane)? ➡️ Base root: -ехать- / -ездить-
  2. Step 2: Direction check:
    • One-way right now? ➡️ Идти / Ехать
    • Routine, habit, or complete round-trip? ➡️ Ходить / Ездить
  3. Step 3: Specific direction needed?
    • Add При- for arriving, У- for leaving, В- for entering, and Вы- for exiting.

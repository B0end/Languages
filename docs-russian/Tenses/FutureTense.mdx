# Future Tense

In Spanish, you choose between future tenses (*leeré* vs. *voy a leer*) mostly based on formality or immediacy.

In Cyrillic/Slavic languages, your choice of future tense is decided **100% by VERB ASPECT**:

1. **Do you care about the process, duration, or habit?** ➡️ Use the **Compound Future** (Imperfective).
2. **Do you care about the completed result or finish line?** ➡️ Use the **Simple Future** (Perfective).

---

## Imperfective / Process

Use this when you want to say you will be spending time doing something, with **no guarantee that you will finish it**, or for actions that repeat.

> **Formula:** Conjugated form of **Быть** (To Be) + **Imperfective Infinitive**

This works almost identically to Spanish *"Voy a [infinitivo]"* or English *"I will be [gerund]"*.

### The Future of "TO BE"
This is the only auxiliary verb you ever need to conjugate in the future:

| Person | Auxiliary Verb |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Я** *(Yo)* | [Я буду;; Yo seré, estaré, voy a / I will be](ЯБуду.wav) |
| **Ты** *(Tú)* | [Ты будешь;; Tú serás, estarás, vas a / You will be](ТыБудешь.wav) |
| **Он / Она / Оно** *(Él / Ella)* | [Он будет;; Él será, estará, va a / He will be](ОнБудет.wav) |
| **Мы** *(Nosotros)* | [Мы будем;; Nosotros seremos, estaremos, vamos a / We will be](МыБудем.wav) |
| **Вы** *(Vosotros / Usted / Ustedes)* | [Вы будете;; Vosotros seréis, Usted será / You all will be](ВыБудете.wav) |
| **Они** *(Ellos / Ellas)* | [Они будут;; Ellos serán, estarán, van a / They will be](ОниБудут.wav) |

---

### Slap on ANY standard imperfective infinitive
Leave the main verb in its basic **-ть** form:

* [Я буду читать;; Voy a leer, estaré leyendo / I will be reading](ЯБудуЧитать.wav)
* [Мы будем смотреть фильм;; Vamos a ver una película / We will be watching a movie](МыБудемСмотретьФильм.wav)
* [Они будут работать завтра;; Van a trabajar mañana / They will be working tomorrow](ОниБудутРаботатьЗавтра.wav)

:::tip
When you say Я буду читать, you are simply describing how you will spend your time. It gives **zero promise** that you will actually finish the book.
:::

## Perfective / Result

Use this when you want to promise a concrete, finished milestone or a completed result.

> **Formula:** Perfective Verb + Present Tense Endings

There is **no auxiliary word** (no буду). You take the **Perfective twin verb** and conjugate it using the Present Tense endings.

**Why does this create the future?** A Perfective verb represents an action that is **100% completed and finished**. Because an action cannot be already finished in the present moment while you are talking about it, putting present tense endings on a Perfective verb automatically launches the meaning into the **FUTURE**!

### Examples:

#### 1. With [Прочитать;; Leer por completo / To read to completion](Прочитать.wav) (Perfective twin of читать):
* [Я прочитаю эту книгу;; Leeré y terminaré este libro / I will read this book (to completion)](ЯПрочитаюЭтуКнигу.wav)
* [Ты прочитаешь эту книгу;; Leerás y terminarás este libro / You will read this book](ТыПрочитаешьЭтуКнигу.wav)
* [Они прочитают эту книгу;; Ellos leerán y terminarán este libro / They will read this book](ОниПрочитаютЭтуКнигу.wav)

#### 2. With [Сделать;; Hacer y terminar / To complete](Сделать.wav) (Perfective twin of делать):
* [Я сделаю это завтра;; Haré y terminaré esto mañana / I will get this done tomorrow](ЯСделаюЭтоЗавтра.wav)
* [Мы сделаем всё вовремя;; Lo terminaremos todo a tiempo / We will finish everything on time](МыСделаемВсёВовремя.wav)

#### 3. With [Написать;; Escribir y terminar / To write](Написать.wav) (Perfective twin of писать):
* [Я напишу тебе сообщение;; Te escribiré un mensaje / I will text you](ЯНапишуТебеСообщение.wav)

## Asking Questions in the Future

Notice how native speakers ask questions depending on what they want to know:

### 1. Asking about plans or how someone will spend their time (Route A):
* [Что ты будешь делать сегодня вечером?;; ¿Qué vas a hacer esta noche? / What will you be doing tonight?](ЧтоТыБудешьДелать.wav)
  *(Neutral: What will you be occupying your time with?)*

### 2. Asking about a concrete outcome or resolution (Route B):
* [Что ты сделаешь?;; ¿Qué vas a hacer/decidir al respecto? / What will you get done?](ЧтоТыСделаешь.wav)
  *(Focus on the specific result or solution you plan to achieve).*

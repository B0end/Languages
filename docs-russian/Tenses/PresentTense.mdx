# Present Tense

If your native language is Spanish, learning the Present Tense in Cyrillic/Slavic languages will feel remarkably natural to you. 

Russian conjugates verbs by person (Yo, Tú, Él...) just like Spanish, rather than keeping the verb almost unchanging like English (*I speak, you speak, he speaks*).

---

## The Verb "TO BE" Does Not Exist!

There is no present tense form of *Ser* or *Estar* (English *To Be*). You simply omit it:

* [Я доктор;; Yo soy doctor / I am a doctor](ЯДоктор.wav)
* [Книга здесь;; El libro está aquí / The book is here](КнигаЗдесь.wav)
* [Она умная;; Ella es inteligente / She is smart](ОнаУмная.wav)

---

## The Subject Pronouns

* [Я;; Yo / I](Я.wav)
* [Ты;; Tú (informal) / You (informal)](Ты.wav)
* [Он / Она / Оно;; Él / Ella / "Ello" (neuter)](ОнОнаОно.wav)
* [Мы;; Nosotros / We](Мы.wav)
* [Вы;; Usted / Vosotros / Ustedes / You (formal or plural)](Вы.wav)
* [Они;; Ellos / Ellas / They](Они.wav)

## The Two Verb Families (Conjugations)

Just like Spanish divides verbs into families (**-ar**, **-er**, **-ir**), Russian divides verbs into **two families**:
1. **Group 1 (The "E" Group):** Most verbs whose infinitives end in **-ать** or **-ять**.
2. **Group 2 (The "И" Group):** Most verbs whose infinitives end in **-ить**.

---

### Group 1: The "E" Conjugation

Why is it called the "E" group? Because 4 out of the 6 endings contain the letter **Е** (**-ешь, -ет, -ем, -ете**), just like Spanish *-ar* verbs repeat the vowel **A** (*cant-as, cant-a, cant-amos, cant-an*).

#### Formula:
* Infinitive: [Знать;; Saber, conocer / To know](Знать.wav)
* Cut off **-ть** ➡️ Stem: **Зна-**
* Now, glue the endings directly onto **Зна-**:

| Math: [Stem] + [Ending] | Resulting Verb |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Зна-** + **-ю** | [Я знаю;; Yo sé / I know](ЯЗнаю.wav) |
| **Зна-** + **-ешь** | [Ты знаешь;; Tú sabes / You know](ТыЗнаешь.wav) |
| **Зна-** + **-ет** | [Он / Она знает;; Él / Ella sabe / He, She knows](ОнОнаЗнает.wav) |
| **Зна-** + **-ем** | [Мы знаем;; Nosotros sabemos / We know](МыЗнаем.wav) |
| **Зна-** + **-ете** | [Вы знаете;; Vosotros sabéis, Usted sabe / You (all) know](ВыЗнаете.wav) |
| **Зна-** + **-ют** | [Они знают;; Ellos saben / They know](ОниЗнают.wav) |

---

### Group 2: The "И" Conjugation

Why is it called the "И" group? Because 4 out of the 6 endings contain the letter **И** (**-ишь, -ит, -им, -ите**), just like Spanish *-er/-ir* verbs repeat the vowel **E** or **I** (*viv-es, viv-e, viv-imos*).

#### The Lego Formula:
* Infinitive: [Говорить;; Hablar / To speak](Говорить.wav)
* Cut off **-ить** ➡️ Stem: **Говор-**
* Now, glue the endings directly onto **Говор-**:

| Math: [Stem] + [Ending] | Resulting Verb |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Говор-** + **-ю** | [Я говорю;; Yo hablo / I speak](ЯГоворю.wav) |
| **Говор-** + **-ишь** | [Ты говоришь;; Tú hablas / You speak](ТыГоворишь.wav) |
| **Говор-** + **-ит** | [Он / Она говорит;; Él / Ella habla / He, She speaks](ОнОнаГоворит.wav) |
| **Говор-** + **-им** | [Мы говорим;; Nosotros hablamos / We speak](МыГоворим.wav) |
| **Говор-** + **-ите** | [Вы говорите;; Vosotros habláis, Usted habla / You (all) speak](ВыГоворите.wav) |
| **Говор-** + **-ят** | [Они говорят;; Ellos hablan / They speak](ОниГоворят.wav) |

## Two Crucial Things

### 1. One Present Tense for Everything (No *Estar + Gerundio*)
In English: *I read* vs. *I am reading*.  
In Spanish: *Yo leo* vs. *Yo estoy leyendo*.

In Slavic languages, **there is only one present tense:**
* [Я читаю;; Yo leo / Yo estoy leyendo / I read / I am reading](ЯЧитаю.wav)

You do not need to construct progressive tenses. Context tells the listener everything.

### 2. Pronoun Dropping (Pro-Drop)
* In Spanish, you often drop the pronoun (*"Hablo español"*, without *"Yo"*).
* In Russian, even though the endings reveal who is doing the action, native speakers **usually keep the subject pronouns in everyday speech**:
  * [Я говорю по-русски;; (Yo) hablo ruso / I speak Russian](ЯГоворюПоРусски.wav)

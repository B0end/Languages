# Verb Aspects: Imperfective vs. Perfective

If you are a native Spanish speaker, **you already master this intuitively in your past tenses**:

* Spanish *Pretérito Imperfecto* (**leía, comía, hacía**) = Focus on the **process / habit**.
* Spanish *Pretérito Indefinido* (**leí, comí, hice**) = Focus on the **completed result**.

Slavic languages take that exact same distinction and apply it across **all tenses**.

---

## The Core Concept: Process vs. Result

In Russian, verbs come in two **Aspect Pairs**:

### 1. Imperfective
 
* Focuses on the **process**, duration, repetition, or habit.
* Answers: *What were you doing?* (¿Qué estabas haciendo?)
* Example: [Читать;; To read (process)](Читать.wav)

### 2. Perfective

* Focuses on the **result**, completion, or a single finished event.
* Answers: *What did you get done?* (¿Qué lograste / completaste?)
* Example: [Прочитать;; To read (completion)](Прочитать.wav)

## The Tense Matrix

| Tense | Imperfective: Читать *(Proceso)* | Perfective: Прочитать *(Resultado)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past** | [Я читал;; Yo leía / I was reading](ЯЧитал.wav) | [Я прочитал;; Yo leí / I finished reading](ЯПрочитал.wav) |
| **Present** | [Я читаю;; Yo leo / I read, I am reading](ЯЧитаю.wav) | ❌ **DOES NOT EXIST** |
| **Future** | [Я буду читать;; Yo voy a leer / I will be reading](ЯБудуЧитать.wav) | [Я прочитаю;; Yo leeré / I will read to completion](ЯПрочитаю.wav) |

## How Aspect Pairs Are Formed

Russian creates the "twin" verb in three main ways:

### 1. Adding a prefix

You take the basic imperfective verb and add a prefix (like **по-**, **про-**, **на-**, **с-**):

| Imperfective (Process) | Perfective (Result) | Prefix |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| [Делать;; Hacer / To do](Делать.wav) | [Сделать;; Hacer y terminar / To complete](Сделать.wav) | **с-** |
| [Писать;; Escribir / To write](Писать.wav) | [Написать;; Escribir / To finish writing](Написать.wav) | **на-** |
| [Пить;; Beber / To drink](Пить.wav) | [Выпить;; Beberse todo / To drink up](Выпить.wav) | **вы-** |

### 2. Changing the Suffix (Vowel shift in the middle)

| Imperfective (Process) | Perfective (Result) |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Решать;; Intentar resolver / To be solving](Решать.wav) | [Решить;; Resolver / To solve, decide](Решить.wav) |
| [Открывать;; Abrir / To be opening](Открывать.wav) | [Открыть;; Abrir / To open](Открыть.wav) |
| [Покупать;; Comprar / To buy](Покупать.wav) | [Купить;; Comprar / To buy](Купить.wav) |

### 3. Totally Different Words (Suppletion)
Just like Spanish *ir* becomes *fui* in the past, a few essential verbs use different roots:

| Imperfective (Process) | Perfective (Result) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Говорить;; Hablar / To speak](Говорить.wav) | [Сказать;; Decir / To say](Сказать.wav) | Hablar ➡️ Decir |
| [Брать;; Tomar, coger / To take](Брать.wav) | [Взять;; Coger, tomar / To take](Взять.wav) | Tomar ➡️ Agarrar |

## Trigger Words

When writing or speaking, certain trigger words automatically force one aspect over the other:

### Imperfective Triggers (Habits, Duration, Frequency):
* [Всегда;; Siempre](Всегда.wav)
* [Каждый день;; Todos los días](КаждыйДень.wav)
* [Часто;; A menudo](Часто.wav)
* [Долго;; Por mucho tiempo](Долго.wav)
* [Обычно;; Normalmente](Обычно.wav)

*Example:* [Я всегда пил кофе;; Yo siempre bebía café / I always drank coffee](ЯВсегдаПилКофе.wav)

---

### Perfective Triggers (Specific Result, Sudden Action):
* [Вдруг;; De repente](Вдруг.wav)
* [Наконец;; Por fin, finalmente](Наконец.wav)
* [Сразу;; De inmediato](Сразу.wav)
* [Уже;; Ya - when indicating completion](Уже.wav)

*Example:* [Наконец я понял;; Por fin entendí / Finally I understood](НаконецЯПонял.wav)

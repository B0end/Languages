Noun Number

In Spanish and English, making a word plural is simple: you slap an -S or -ES at the end (casa ➔ casas, book ➔ books).

In Cyrillic/Slavic languages, you almost never use consonants to make a word plural. Instead, plurals are formed with vowels:

  • -Ы and -И (for Masculine and Feminine)
  • -А and -Я (for Neuter)

Masculine and Feminine Team Up​

Masculine and Feminine nouns share the exact same plural endings!

Neuter Nouns: The Transgenders​

Neuter nouns do something funny: they take the endings that look feminine in the singular (-А and -Я):

  • Singular in -О (Hard) ➔ Plural in -А
    • Окно;; Ventana / Window ➔ Окна;; Ventanas / Windows
    • Слово;; Palabra / Word ➔ Слова;; Palabras / Words
  • Singular in -Е (Soft) ➔ Plural in -Я
    • Море;; Mar / Sea ➔ Моря;; Mares / Seas
    • Здание;; Edificio / Building ➔ Здания;; Edificios / Buildings

The 7-Letter Spelling Rule (The "Anti-Ы" Shield)​

In Russian phonetics, it is physically uncomfortable to pronounce the deep guttural sound Ы right after certain throat and hissing sounds.

warning

NEVER write Ы after these 7 letters: Г, К, Х (throat sounds) and Ж, Ч, Ш, Щ (hissing sounds).
Always write И instead!

Even if a word is "hard" and logically should take -Ы, the 7-letter rule overrides everything:

  • Книга;; Libro / Book ➔ Книги;; Libros / Books (NOT Книгы)
  • Мальчик;; Niño, chico / Boy ➔ Мальчики;; Chicos / Boys
  • Врач;; Médico / Doctor ➔ Врачи;; Médicos / Doctors

Common Irregular Plurals​

Just like English has child ➔ children and man ➔ men, Russian has a few common irregular plural patterns.

1. Masculine Nouns with Stressed -Á​

A group of very common masculine nouns take a stressed -А́ instead of -Ы:

  • Дом;; Casa / House ➔ Дома;; Casas / Houses
  • Город;; Ciudad / City ➔ Города;; Ciudades / Cities
  • Поезд;; Tren / Train ➔ Поезда;; Trenes / Trains
  • Паспорт;; Pasaporte / Passport ➔ Паспорта;; Pasaportes / Passports

2. The "-ЬЯ" Family​

Some masculine nouns add a soft sign + -я:

  • Друг;; Amigo / Friend ➔ Друзья;; Amigos / Friends
  • Брат;; Hermano / Brother ➔ Братья;; Hermanos / Brothers
  • Стул;; Silla / Chair ➔ Стулья;; Sillas / Chairs

3. Disappearing Vowels​

When some masculine nouns become plural, an Е or О in the final syllable drops out to make speech faster:

  • День;; Día / Day ➔ Дни;; Días / Days (The 'Е' vanished!)
  • Подарок;; Regalo / Gift ➔ Подарки;; Regalos / Gifts (The 'О' vanished!)

4. Total Word Change (Suppletion)​

Just like Spanish persona ➔ gente or English person ➔ people:

  • Человек;; Persona / Person ➔ Люди;; Personas, gente / People
  • Ребёнок;; Niño, bebé / Child ➔ Дети;; Niños / Children

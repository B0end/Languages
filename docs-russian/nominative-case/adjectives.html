Adjectives

Native English speakers struggle because English adjectives never change (white car, white cars). But Spanish speakers already know that an adjective must mirror its noun (coche blanco, casa blanca, casas blancas).

In Russian, adjectives work almost the same way—with two nuances:

  1. Word order is like English: Adjectives go BEFORE the noun (Белый дом = White House).
  2. Plurals have NO gender: Unlike Spanish (blancos vs. blancas), Russian adjectives use the exact same ending for all genders in the plural.

The 4 Endings​

In the dictionary (Nominative case), an adjective has four forms based on the gender and number of the noun it describes:

Look at our hero adjective: Новый (Nuevo / New):

GenderEndingExample NounResulting Phrase
Masculine-ыйДом;; Casa (masc) / HouseНовый дом;; Casa nueva / New house
Feminine-аяМашина;; Coche (fem) / CarНовая машина;; Coche nuevo / New car
Neuter-оеОкно;; Ventana (neut) / WindowНовое окно;; Ventana nueva / New window
Plural (All genders!)-ыеКниги;; Libros (plur) / BooksНовые книги;; Libros nuevos / New books

The Three Varieties of Adjectives​

While 85% of Russian adjectives look like новый, there are three varieties you will encounter based on stress and stem endings:

1. Hard Adjectives (Standard)​

The most common type. Stems end in a regular hard consonant:

  • Endings: -ый, -ая, -ое, -ые
  • Красивый город;; Ciudad hermosa / Beautiful city
  • Красивая девушка;; Chica hermosa / Beautiful girl
  • Красивое место;; Lugar hermoso / Beautiful place
  • Красивые места;; Lugares hermosos / Beautiful places

2. Stressed-Ending Adjectives (The -ОЙ Group)​

When the accent mark falls on the ending in the masculine form, -ый changes to a stressed -о́й:

  • Endings: -о́й, -а́я, -о́е, -ы́е / -и́е
  • Большой дом;; Casa grande / Big house (Masc takes -о́й)
  • Большая комната;; Habitación grande / Big room
  • Большое окно;; Ventana grande / Big window
  • Большие дома;; Casas grandes / Big houses

Another common example: Плохой;; Malo / Bad ➡️ Плохая погода;; Mal tiempo / Bad weather

3. Soft Adjectives (The -ИЙ Group)​

A small group of adjectives (mostly colors, time words, and directions) have a "soft" stem:

  • Endings: -ий, -яя, -ее, -ие
  • Синий костюм;; Traje azul marino / Dark blue suit
  • Синяя сумка;; Bolsa azul marino / Dark blue bag
  • Синее море;; Mar azul marino / Dark blue sea
  • Синие глаза;; Ojos azules / Blue eyes

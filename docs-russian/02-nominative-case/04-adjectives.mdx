# Adjectives

Native English speakers struggle because English adjectives never change (*white car, white cars*). But Spanish speakers already know that an adjective must mirror its noun (*coche blanc**o**, casa blanc**a**, casas blanc**as***).

In Russian, adjectives work almost the same way—with two nuances:
1. **Word order is like English:** Adjectives go **BEFORE** the noun (Белый дом = White House).
2. **Plurals have NO gender:** Unlike Spanish (*blancos* vs. *blancas*), Russian adjectives use the **exact same ending for all genders in the plural**.

---

## The 4 Endings

In the dictionary (Nominative case), an adjective has four forms based on the gender and number of the noun it describes:

Look at our hero adjective: **Новый** (*Nuevo / New*):

| Gender | Ending | Example Noun | Resulting Phrase |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Masculine** | **-ый** | [Дом;; Casa (masc) / House](Дом.wav) | [Новый дом;; Casa nueva / New house](НовыйДом.wav) |
| **Feminine** | **-ая** | [Машина;; Coche (fem) / Car](Машина.wav) | [Новая машина;; Coche nuevo / New car](НоваяМашина.wav) |
| **Neuter** | **-ое** | [Окно;; Ventana (neut) / Window](Окно.wav) | [Новое окно;; Ventana nueva / New window](НовоеОкно.wav) |
| **Plural** *(All genders!)* | **-ые** | [Книги;; Libros (plur) / Books](Книги.wav) | [Новые книги;; Libros nuevos / New books](НовыеКниги.wav) |

## The Three Varieties of Adjectives

While 85% of Russian adjectives look like **новый**, there are three varieties you will encounter based on stress and stem endings:

### 1. Hard Adjectives (Standard)
The most common type. Stems end in a regular hard consonant:
* **Endings:** **-ый**, **-ая**, **-ое**, **-ые**
* [Красивый город;; Ciudad hermosa / Beautiful city](КрасивыйГород.wav)
* [Красивая девушка;; Chica hermosa / Beautiful girl](КрасиваяДевушка.wav)
* [Красивое место;; Lugar hermoso / Beautiful place](КрасивоеМесто.wav)
* [Красивые места;; Lugares hermosos / Beautiful places](КрасивыеМеста.wav)

---

### 2. Stressed-Ending Adjectives (The -ОЙ Group)
When the accent mark falls on the ending in the masculine form, **-ый** changes to a stressed **-о́й**:
* **Endings:** **-о́й**, **-а́я**, **-о́е**, **-ы́е / -и́е**
* [Большой дом;; Casa grande / Big house](БольшойДом.wav) (Masc takes **-о́й**)
* [Большая комната;; Habitación grande / Big room](БольшаяКомната.wav)
* [Большое окно;; Ventana grande / Big window](БольшоеОкно.wav)
* [Большие дома;; Casas grandes / Big houses](БольшиеДома.wav)

*Another common example:* [Плохой;; Malo / Bad](Плохой.wav) ➡️ [Плохая погода;; Mal tiempo / Bad weather](ПлохаяПогода.wav)

---

### 3. Soft Adjectives (The -ИЙ Group)
A small group of adjectives (mostly colors, time words, and directions) have a "soft" stem:
* **Endings:** **-ий**, **-яя**, **-ее**, **-ие**
* [Синий костюм;; Traje azul marino / Dark blue suit](СинийКостюм.wav)
* [Синяя сумка;; Bolsa azul marino / Dark blue bag](СиняяСумка.wav)
* [Синее море;; Mar azul marino / Dark blue sea](СинееМоре.wav)
* [Синие глаза;; Ojos azules / Blue eyes](СиниеГлаза.wav)

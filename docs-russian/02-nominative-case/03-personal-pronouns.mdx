# Personal Pronouns

Just like in Spanish and English, personal pronouns in Russian replace nouns when referring to people, animals, or objects. 

Because you speak Spanish, you already understand two major concepts that native English speakers often struggle with:
1. **The Formal vs. Informal "You":** Russian has a direct equivalent to the Spanish distinction between **tú** and **usted / ustedes**.
2. **Pronoun Declension:** In Spanish, "yo" becomes "me", "mí", or "conmigo" depending on the role in the sentence. Russian does the exact same thing across its **6 grammatical cases**.

---

## The Subject Pronouns (Nominative Case)

These are the dictionary forms of personal pronouns used when the pronoun is the **subject** of the sentence (the one performing the action).

| Russian | English | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Я;; Yo / I](Я.wav) | I | Always lowercase in the middle of a sentence (unlike English "I"). |
| [Ты;; Tú / You](Ты.wav) | you *(informal)* | Used with friends, family, children, and peers. |
| [Он;; Él / He, It](Он.wav) | he / it *(masculine)* | Used for male people/animals **and** masculine objects. |
| [Она;; Ella / She, It](Она.wav) | she / it *(feminine)* | Used for female people/animals **and** feminine objects. |
| [Оно;; Ello / It](Оно.wav) | it *(neuter)* | Used for neuter nouns (e.g., [Окно;; Ventana / Window](Окно.wav), [Письмо;; Carta / Letter](Письмо.wav)). |
| [Мы;; Nosotros / We](Мы.wav) | we | Gender-neutral. |
| [Вы;; Usted, ustedes / You](Вы.wav) | you *(formal/plural)* | Used for polite singular ("usted") and all plurals ("ustedes"). |
| [Они;; Ellos, ellas / They](Они.wav) | they | Gender-neutral plural for people and objects. |

### Important Concept: "He" and "She" for Inanimate Objects

Just like in Spanish, Russian objects have grammatical gender. If you are talking about a masculine object like a table ([Стол;; Mesa / Table](Стол.wav)), you refer to it as [Он;; Él / He, It](Он.wav) (like in Spanish: *"el teléfono... él está en la mesa"*).

* [Где мой телефон? Он на столе;; ¿Dónde está mi teléfono? Está en la mesa / Where is my phone? It is on the table](ГдемойтелефонОннастоле.wav)
* [Где моя книга? Она здесь;; ¿Dónde está mi libro? Está aquí / Where is my book? It is here](ГдемоякнигаОназдесь.wav)

#### Basic Subject Examples:
* [Я говорю по-русски;; Hablo ruso / I speak Russian](Яговорюпо-русски.wav)
  *(Unlike Spanish, Russian does **not** regularly drop subject pronouns! Keep the "[Я;; Yo / I](Я.wav)" in the sentence).*
* [Ты говоришь по-испански?;; ¿Hablas español? / Do you speak Spanish?](Тыговоришьпо-испански.wav)
* [Вы говорите по-английски?;; ¿Usted habla inglés? / Do you speak English?](Выговоритепо-английски.wav)
* [Мы учимся вместе;; Estudiamos juntos / We study together](Мыучимсявместе.wav)

## Pronoun Cases

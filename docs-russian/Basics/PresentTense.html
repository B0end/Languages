Present Tense

If your native language is Spanish, learning the Present Tense in Cyrillic/Slavic languages will feel remarkably natural to you.

Russian conjugates verbs by person (Yo, Tú, Él...) just like Spanish, rather than keeping the verb almost unchanging like English (I speak, you speak, he speaks).

Surprise #1: The Verb "TO BE" Does Not Exist!​

There is no present tense form of Ser or Estar (English To Be). You simply omit it:

  • Я доктор;; Yo soy doctor / I am a doctor
  • Книга здесь;; El libro está aquí / The book is here
  • Она умная;; Ella es inteligente / She is smart

The Subject Pronouns​

First, here is who is doing the action:

  • Я;; Yo / I
  • Ты;; Tú (informal) / You (informal)
  • Он / Она / Оно;; Él / Ella / "Ello" (neuter)
  • Мы;; Nosotros / We
  • Вы;; Usted / Vosotros / Ustedes / You (formal or plural)
  • Они;; Ellos / Ellas / They

The Two Verb Families (Conjugations)​

Just like Spanish divides verbs into families (-ar, -er, -ir), Russian divides verbs into two families:

  1. Group 1 (The "E" Group): Most verbs whose infinitives end in -ать or -ять.
  2. Group 2 (The "И" Group): Most verbs whose infinitives end in -ить.

Group 1: The "E" Conjugation​

Why is it called the "E" group? Because 4 out of the 6 endings contain the letter Е (-ешь, -ет, -ем, -ете), just like Spanish -ar verbs repeat the vowel A (cant-as, cant-a, cant-amos, cant-an).

The Lego Formula:​

  • Infinitive: Знать;; Saber, conocer / To know
  • Cut off -ть ➡️ Stem: Зна-
  • Now, glue the endings directly onto Зна-:
Math: [Stem] + [Ending]Resulting Verb
Зна- + -юЯ знаю;; Yo sé / I know
Зна- + -ешьТы знаешь;; Tú sabes / You know
Зна- + -етОн / Она знает;; Él / Ella sabe / He, She knows
Зна- + -емМы знаем;; Nosotros sabemos / We know
Зна- + -етеВы знаете;; Vosotros sabéis, Usted sabe / You (all) know
Зна- + -ютОни знают;; Ellos saben / They know

Group 2: The "И" Conjugation​

Why is it called the "И" group? Because 4 out of the 6 endings contain the letter И (-ишь, -ит, -им, -ите), just like Spanish -er/-ir verbs repeat the vowel E or I (viv-es, viv-e, viv-imos).

The Lego Formula:​

  • Infinitive: Говорить;; Hablar / To speak
  • Cut off -ить ➡️ Stem: Говор-
  • Now, glue the endings directly onto Говор-:
Math: [Stem] + [Ending]Resulting Verb
Говор- + -юЯ говорю;; Yo hablo / I speak
Говор- + -ишьТы говоришь;; Tú hablas / You speak
Говор- + -итОн / Она говорит;; Él / Ella habla / He, She speaks
Говор- + -имМы говорим;; Nosotros hablamos / We speak
Говор- + -итеВы говорите;; Vosotros habláis, Usted habla / You (all) speak
Говор- + -ятОни говорят;; Ellos hablan / They speak

Two Crucial Things Spanish Speakers Must Know​

1. One Present Tense for Everything (No Estar + Gerundio)​

In English: I read vs. I am reading.
In Spanish: Yo leo vs. Yo estoy leyendo.

In Slavic languages, there is only one present tense:

  • Я читаю;; Yo leo / Yo estoy leyendo / I read / I am reading

You do not need to construct progressive tenses. Context tells the listener everything.

2. Pronoun Dropping (Pro-Drop)​

  • In Spanish, you often drop the pronoun ("Hablo español", without "Yo").
  • In Russian, even though the endings reveal who is doing the action, native speakers usually keep the subject pronouns in everyday speech:
    • Я говорю по-русски;; (Yo) hablo ruso / I speak Russian

The "Catch": Verbal Aspect (Un Adelanto)​

In Slavic languages, verbs come in pairs: Imperfective (habitual, continuous, incomplete actions) and Perfective (completed, one-time actions with a result).

The Golden Rule of the Present Tense:

  • Only Imperfective verbs can be used in the Present Tense.

  • If you take a Perfective verb and apply the present endings shown above, it automatically refers to the FUTURE:

  • Я читаю;; Yo leo / I read (Present - Imperfective)

  • Я прочитаю;; Yo leeré / I will read (Future - Perfective)

Summary Checklist​

  1. Find the stem:
    • Group 1: Remove -ть from infinitives ending in -ать (e.g., Зна-ть ➡️ Зна-).
    • Group 2: Remove -ить (e.g., Говор-ить ➡️ Говор-).
  2. Glue on the ending matching your subject:
    • Group 1 uses Е: -ю, -ешь, -ет, -ем, -ете, -ют
    • Group 2 uses И: -ю, -ишь, -ит, -им, -ите, -ят
  3. No "to be": Never try to translate am/is/are in the present tense.

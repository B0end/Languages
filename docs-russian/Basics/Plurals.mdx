# Plural Nouns

In Spanish and English, making a word plural is simple: you slap an **-S** or **-ES** at the end (*casa ➡️ casas*, *book ➡️ books*).

In Cyrillic/Slavic languages, you **almost never use consonants** to make a word plural. Instead, plurals are formed with **vowels**:

* **-Ы** and **-И** (for Masculine and Feminine)
* **-А** and **-Я** (for Neuter)

---

## Masculine and Feminine Team Up

**Masculine and Feminine nouns share the exact same plural endings!**

| Noun Type | Singular Ending | Plural Ending | Example Singular | Plural Result |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Hard** | Consonant *(M)* or **-А** *(F)* | **-Ы** | [Стол;; Mesa / Table](Стол.wav)<br />[Газета;; Periódico / Newspaper](Газета.wav) | [Столы;; Mesas / Tables](Столы.wav)<br />[Газеты;; Periódicos / Newspapers](Газеты.wav) |
| **Soft** | **-Й / -Ь** *(M)* or **-Я / -Ь** *(F)* | **-И** | [Музей;; Museo / Museum](Музей.wav)<br />[Неделя;; Semana / Week](Неделя.wav)<br />[Ночь;; Noche / Night](Ночь.wav) | [Музеи;; Museos / Museums](Музеи.wav)<br />[Недели;; Semanas / Weeks](Недели.wav)<br />[Ночи;; Noches / Nights](Ночи.wav) |

## Neuter Nouns: The Transgenders

Neuter nouns do something funny: they take the endings that look feminine in the singular (**-А** and **-Я**):

* Singular in **-О** (Hard) ➡️ Plural in **-А**
  * [Окно;; Ventana / Window](Окно.wav) ➡️ [Окна;; Ventanas / Windows](Окна.wav)
  * [Слово;; Palabra / Word](Слово.wav) ➡️ [Слова;; Palabras / Words](Слова.wav)
* Singular in **-Е** (Soft) ➡️ Plural in **-Я**
  * [Море;; Mar / Sea](Море.wav) ➡️ [Моря;; Mares / Seas](Моря.wav)
  * [Здание;; Edificio / Building](Здание.wav) ➡️ [Здания;; Edificios / Buildings](Здания.wav)

## The 7-Letter Spelling Rule (The "Anti-Ы" Shield)

In Russian phonetics, it is physically uncomfortable to pronounce the deep guttural sound **Ы** right after certain throat and hissing sounds.

:::warning
**NEVER write Ы after these 7 letters:**
**Г, К, Х** *(throat sounds)* and **Ж, Ч, Ш, Щ** *(hissing sounds)*.  
**Always write И instead!**
:::

Even if a word is "hard" and logically should take **-Ы**, the 7-letter rule overrides everything:

* [Книга;; Libro / Book](Книга.wav) ➡️ [Книги;; Libros / Books](Книги.wav) **(NOT Книгы)**
* [Мальчик;; Niño, chico / Boy](Мальчик.wav) ➡️ [Мальчики;; Chicos / Boys](Мальчики.wav)
* [Врач;; Médico / Doctor](Врач.wav) ➡️ [Врачи;; Médicos / Doctors](Врачи.wav)

## Common Irregular Plurals

Just like English has *child ➡️ children* and *man ➡️ men*, Russian has a few common irregular plural patterns.

### 1. Masculine Nouns with Stressed -Á
A group of very common masculine nouns take a stressed **-А́** instead of **-Ы**:

* [Дом;; Casa / House](Дом.wav) ➡️ [Дома;; Casas / Houses](Дома.wav)
* [Город;; Ciudad / City](Город.wav) ➡️ [Города;; Ciudades / Cities](Города.wav)
* [Поезд;; Tren / Train](Поезд.wav) ➡️ [Поезда;; Trenes / Trains](Поезда.wav)
* [Паспорт;; Pasaporte / Passport](Паспорт.wav) ➡️ [Паспорта;; Pasaportes / Passports](Паспорта.wav)

### 2. The "-ЬЯ" Family
Some masculine nouns add a soft sign + **-я**:

* [Друг;; Amigo / Friend](Друг.wav) ➡️ [Друзья;; Amigos / Friends](Друзья.wav)
* [Брат;; Hermano / Brother](Брат.wav) ➡️ [Братья;; Hermanos / Brothers](Братья.wav)
* [Стул;; Silla / Chair](Стул.wav) ➡️ [Стулья;; Sillas / Chairs](Стулья.wav)

### 3. Disappearing Vowels (Fleeting Vowels)
When some masculine nouns become plural, an **Е** or **О** in the final syllable drops out to make speech faster:

* [День;; Día / Day](День.wav) ➡️ [Дни;; Días / Days](Дни.wav) *(The 'Е' vanished!)*
* [Подарок;; Regalo / Gift](Подарок.wav) ➡️ [Подарки;; Regalos / Gifts](Подарки.wav) *(The 'О' vanished!)*

### 4. Total Word Change (Suppletion)
Just like Spanish *persona ➡️ gente* or English *person ➡️ people*:

* [Человек;; Persona / Person](Человек.wav) ➡️ [Люди;; Personas, gente / People](Люди.wav)
* [Ребёнок;; Niño, bebé / Child](Ребёнок.wav) ➡️ [Дети;; Niños / Children](Дети.wav)

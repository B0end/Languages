# Verbs of Motion

In Spanish and English, you use one single verb to move around: **Ir / To Go**.
* *Voy al supermercado a pie.*
* *Voy a París en avión.*
* *Voy al gimnasio todos los días.*

In Slavic languages, there is **no generic word for "to go"**. Instead, Russian forces you to answer two mandatory questions every time you move:

1. **How are you moving?** ➡️ On foot or by transport?
2. **What kind of trip is it?** ➡️ A one-way line right now, or a round-trip / regular habit?

---

## Question 1: Feet or Wheels?

Russian makes a strict physical distinction between moving under your own muscle power versus using a vehicle:

| Method of Movement | Unidirectional (One-way / Now) | Multidirectional (Habit / Round-trip) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **On Foot** *(Caminando, a pie)* | [Идти;; Ir a pie (en camino) / To go on foot (unidirectional)](Идти.wav) | [Ходить;; Ir a pie (hábito, ida y vuelta) / To go on foot (multidirectional)](Ходить.wav) |
| **By Vehicle** *(En coche, tren, avión, barco)* | [Ехать;; Ir en vehículo (en camino) / To go by vehicle (unidirectional)](Ехать.wav) | [Ездить;; Ir en vehículo (hábito, ida y vuelta) / To go by vehicle (multidirectional)](Ездить.wav) |

:::warning
In Spanish, you can say *"Voy a Rusia"*.  
If you say **Я иду в Россию**, Russians will look at you in shock: it literally means you are walking across continents on your own two feet!  
If you use an airplane, bus, train, or car, you **must** use [Ехать;; Ir en vehículo / To go by transport](Ехать.wav):  
* [Я еду в Россию;; Voy a Rusia (en transporte) / I am traveling to Russia](ЯЕдуВРоссию.wav)
:::

## Question 2: The "Arrow" Concept (Uni vs. Multi)

Every method of motion has two paired verbs:

### 1. Unidirectional Verbs (The One-Way Arrow: ➡️)
Use these when you are in the middle of a trip heading toward a single specific destination:

* [Я иду на работу;; Voy al trabajo (a pie, ahora mismo) / I am walking to work](ЯИдуНаРаботу.wav)
* [Мы едем в центр;; Vamos al centro (en coche/metro, en camino) / We are driving to downtown](МыЕдемВЦентр.wav)

---

### 2. Multidirectional Verbs (The Round-Trip & Habit Arrow: 🔁)
Use these for three specific situations:

#### A. Habits and Routines (Todos los días, a menudo):
* [Я хожу на работу каждый день;; Voy al trabajo todos los días (a pie) / I walk to work every day](ЯХожуНаРаботуКаждыйДень.wav)
* [Они часто ездят в горы;; Ellos a menudo van a la montaña (en coche) / They often go to the mountains](ОниЧастоЕздятВГоры.wav)

#### B. Completed Round-Trips in the Past (Fui y volví):
* [Вчера я ходил в музей;; Ayer fui al museo (a pie, y ya volví) / Yesterday I went to the museum](ВчераЯХодилВМузей.wav)
  *(Compare with: [Вчера я шёл в музей;; Ayer iba de camino al museo / Yesterday I was on my way to the museum](ВчераЯШёлВМузей.wav))*

#### C. Moving with No Fixed Direction (Paseando por el parque):
* [Дети бегают в парке;; Los niños corren por el parque (en círculos) / Children are running around in the park](ДетиБегаютВПарке.wav)

## Adding Prefixes: The Slavic GPS System

Once you know [Идти;; Ir a pie / To walk](Идти.wav) and [Ехать;; Ir en vehículo / To drive, ride](Ехать.wav), you can create dozens of new, super-precise verbs simply by adding directional prefixes.

Notice how the prefix changes the direction:

| Prefix | Meaning | On Foot (A pie) | By Transport (En vehículo) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **При-** | **Arrival** *(Llegar)* | [Прийти;; Llegar a pie / To arrive on foot](Прийти.wav) | [Приехать;; Llegar en transporte / To arrive by vehicle](Приехать.wav) |
| **У-** | **Departure far away** *(Irse)* | [Уйти;; Irse a pie / To leave on foot](Уйти.wav) | [Уехать;; Irse en transporte / To leave by vehicle](Уехать.wav) |
| **В- / Во-** | **Entering** *(Entrar)* | [Войти;; Entrar a pie / To enter on foot](Войти.wav) | [Въехать;; Entrar en vehículo / To drive in](Въехать.wav) |
| **Вы-** | **Exiting** *(Salir)* | [Выйти;; Salir a pie / To exit on foot](Выйти.wav) | [Выехать;; Salir en transporte / To drive out](Выехать.wav) |
| **Пере-** | **Crossing across** *(Cruzar)* | [Перейти;; Cruzar la calle (a pie) / To cross on foot](Перейти.wav) | [Переехать;; Cruzar o mudarse / To drive across, move](Переехать.wav) |
| **До-** | **Reaching a destination** *(Llegar hasta)* | [Дойти;; Llegar hasta (a pie) / To reach on foot](Дойти.wav) | [Доехать;; Llegar hasta (en transporte) / To reach by transport](Доехать.wav) |

### Practical Examples with Prefixes:
* [Поезд приехал в Москву;; El tren llegó a Moscú / The train arrived in Moscow](ПоездПриехалВМоскву.wav)
* [Он вышел из комнаты;; Él salió de la habitación / He walked out of the room](ОнВышелИзКомнаты.wav)
* [Осторожно переходи улицу;; Cruza la calle con cuidado / Cross the street carefully](ОсторожноПереходиУлицу.wav)

## Summary Checklist

Before choosing a motion verb, run this mental filter:

1. **Step 1: Transport check:**
   * Walking? ➡️ Base root: **-идти- / -ходить-**
   * Vehicle (car, bus, train, plane)? ➡️ Base root: **-ехать- / -ездить-**
2. **Step 2: Direction check:**
   * One-way right now? ➡️ **Идти / Ехать**
   * Routine, habit, or complete round-trip? ➡️ **Ходить / Ездить**
3. **Step 3: Specific direction needed?**
   * Add **При-** for *arriving*, **У-** for *leaving*, **В-** for *entering*, and **Вы-** for *exiting*.
